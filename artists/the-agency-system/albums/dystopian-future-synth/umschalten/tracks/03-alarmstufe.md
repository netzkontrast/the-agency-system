## Concept

S2 is the mobilization peak: an intrusion fires every warning system, and the two EPs who answer the alarm collide over how. One voice wants to burn the threat down; the other wants to hold position and cover, care expressed only as "stay behind me." The friction is method: attack versus shield, heat versus held line. The refrain is spat as a challenge, doubled by both voices pulling against each other.
