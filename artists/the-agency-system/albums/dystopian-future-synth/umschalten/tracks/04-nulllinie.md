## Concept

The system's overload finally tips past holding. The collapsed voice takes the floor and carries the unbearable so nothing else ruptures — near-spoken, sub-tempo, breath audible and then absent. The recurring question is answered, at last, by no one: the counted breath distorts into a flatline tone and stops. Loudest, then emptiest.
