## Concept

The closer answers the EP's haunting refrain in the plural. Where S3 flatlined to a single held breath, S4 fills the seams between the parts with gold — not by fusing them smooth, but by letting each voice stay audible inside the Wir. The integrator opens the room ("Noch nicht" finally becomes "Jetzt"), and the chorus arrives as durchhörbare polyphony: every register remembered, none erased. To "Wer atmet, wenn ich schlafe?" the we-voice answers: wir atmen.
