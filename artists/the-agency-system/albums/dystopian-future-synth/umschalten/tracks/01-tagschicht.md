## Concept

Tagschicht is the surface-normal opener: a workday rendered as a maintained facade by the two ANP voices. The host carries weary present-tense observation in trailing, counting lines; the rationalist answers in cold conditional syntax, a proof of normalcy that almost closes and won't. The S0 refrain "Wer atmet, wenn ich schlafe?" lands cool and rhetorical, a question filed and not opened.

## Pronunciation Notes

| Word/Phrase | Pronunciation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Protokoll | pro-toh-KOLL | Suno may anglicize to "protocol"; force German stress on final syllable |
| zweitausenddreihundertvier | tsvai-TAU-zent-DRAI-hoon-dert-FEER | Long compound numeral risks being read digit-by-digit or in English |
| Einundzwanzig | AIN-oont-TSVAN-tsig | Compound number, German order; avoid English "twenty-one" |
| Schicht | SHIKHT | guttural German "ch", not English "sh-ick-t" |
| Tagschicht | TAHK-shikht | Title term; both halves German, not "tag-shift" |
