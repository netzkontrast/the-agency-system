## Concept

S1 freeze: the system slows to a stop and watches itself from behind glass. The child-freeze holds her breath in the lowest corner, asking the EP's half-question that won't finish; the collapsed voice answers from underneath, leaden, near-spoken. The S0 refrain returns muffled and trailing off, the first crack in the surface "Tagschicht" kept flawless.
