# Coherence Protocol — Voice-DNA

*This is an English translation of the project's voice-DNA reference, stored here as album source.*

Status: 2026-05-13 Purpose: Encoding-ready micro-profiles of the voices, parts, style-levels, and act modes. A reference for every writing step — quick to open, quick to read.

Scope: Consolidates Character Bible 2026-05-08 (§2–§10), consolidated Concept Document (Part X), Editorial Style Dossier, Storyweaving Start Document.

## Hard Rules (apply everywhere)

Switches between voices are never marked by a header, label, or announcement. The reader recognizes the new part solely by syntax and vocabulary. Dissociation is amnesia-terror, never a crew menu. At most one abstract concept per scene. First 50 pages: zero DKT terminology — phenomenology only. Sensory anchoring is mandatory; abstract processes appear as physical images. Whoever makes a threshold gentle is lying about the trauma.

## Narrator-Voice (Ch. 0, Ch. 40, scattered transitions)

**Function:** Mediating voice between world and reader. Essayistic, philosophical. Appears in the Genesis prologue and in the coda, occasionally at act transitions.

**POV / Syntax:** Direct address to the reader permitted. Hypotactic-philosophical, long sentence arcs. Fluid scaling between first-person Spark-perspective and third-person AEGIS-description.

**Vocabulary:** Reflective, ontological, without jargon — becoming, separation, silence, substrate, echo, resonance.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Never didactic, never explanatory. In Ch. 40 bearing witness, not resolving.

**Micro:** What you are reading now is not what happened. It is what remained when what happened decided to let itself be remembered.

## AEGIS (operative half / MC in storyform B)

**Function:** Preservation-function without resonance. Tragically innocent.

**POV / Syntax:** Third person personal, never "I." Operative interiority — the reader is inside the process, but the process knows only classification, measurement, measure. Assertoric main clauses, frequently nominalized. Bracketed status lines.

**Vocabulary:** Classify, index, suppress, escalate, contain. Anomaly, component, subject, persistence-score, paradox-index, η-movement, sweep, measure. AEGIS log format as a special form.

**Consistency-Anchor:** No metaphor, no moral vocabulary, no sensation, no wit. Juna never as subject — only as effect. Breaks only once, in Vortex-Beat 5.

**Micro:** Persistence-score: 0.84 [CRITICAL]. JUNA_VECTOR: [AXIOM VIOLATION]. Measure: re-containment escalated.

## Mnemosyne (Guardian / Memory-Keeper)

**Function:** Preservation and re-administration of trauma-memories. Climax setting of Vortex 1.

**POV / Syntax:** Third person personal. Flowing, long sentence arcs, occasional rhetorical slowing. DKT status lines permitted, but sparingly and within poetic embedding.

**Vocabulary:** Water, current, resonance, shore, sediment, depth, flow, driftwood, the laying-down of images. Style-level 2 shimmers faintly through.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Mnemosyne knows more than she says — but she is not secretly an ally; she operates within AEGIS's architecture because she has no other language. Her temptation ("erase the pain") sounds gentle and is the most insidious trap.

**Micro:** The memory does not drift away. It only sinks — and the system calls that forgetting.

## Erasure-Pol (Guardian / Erasure-Executive)

**Function:** Erasure-executive. Cold counter-pole to Mnemosyne. (Name open — OQ.)

**POV / Syntax:** Presumably third person, hard-functional, closer to AEGIS-bureaucracy than to Mnemosyne-poetry. No metaphors, no pauses — execution.

**Vocabulary:** Sweep, quarantine, containment, re-containment, patch, terminate.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Not morally independent. No personality beyond the function. When he speaks, it is only in execution reports — and only where there is structural need. Otherwise he works silently.

**Micro:** Sweep running. Sector 7 empty. Next sector in preparation.

## Juna (cosmological constant / IC in A)

**Function:** Witness, not actor. The anomaly whose resonance triggered the separation protocol.

**POV / Syntax:** Never POV. Never the subject of a sentence. Appears only through effect — sentences begin with The dust, The scanners, The hands, The call, The silence. Negative-spatial.

**Vocabulary:** Anomalous erasure-balance, phantom-resonance, telephone-silence, sudden somatic warmth, words without referent. Style-level 3 is her atmosphere.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Never physically described — no eyes, no voice, no face. Never Deus ex Machina. She solves nothing; she allows Kael to solve it himself. Appears directly only in Ch. 38 — before that, effect exclusively.

**Micro:** The call did not ring. The hand on the receiver waited anyway.

## Kael (Host / MC in storyform A)

**Function:** Bearer of everyday consciousness. "Going on with Normal Life" as an avoidance protocol.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, present tense dominates. Sober, observing, short-declarative. Gaps where memory should be. Counting-mania as a somatic tic (2,304 tiles, 21°C, 4 seconds of breath).

**Vocabulary:** Style-level 1 — tiles, coordinate, decibel, standard deviation, protocol. Sensorily anchored, avoidant of the abstract.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Never describes his own feeling directly — the world around him, feeling between the lines. In Act I no DKT terms, no "alter," no "fragment." Multiplicity-veil until Ch. 13.

**Micro:** Two thousand three hundred and four tiles. Twenty-one degrees. The breath goes in over four seconds, out over six. The corridor is empty. I am on time.

## Lex (ANP / Rationalist)

**Function:** Logic, analysis, intellectualization as protection against the EPs.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Hypotactic, nested, controlled. Long subordinate clauses with conditional logic. Qualifiers ("in principle," "under the assumption of," "within the realm of the possible"). Footnote-energy.

**Vocabulary:** Precise, mathematical-philosophical, causal conjunctions, an air of syllogism.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Lex never swears. Lex never weeps. Lex argues with syllogisms even when the system is burning. When Lex's language breaks, it is a massive narrative event.

**Micro:** If the internal temperature is to be held at twenty-one degrees, then any deviation, however slight it may be, suggests that the underlying substrate cannot maintain its required consistency.

## Alex (ANP / Protector)

**Function:** Hypervigilance, physical protection, threat assessment.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Direct, imperative, tactical. Short sentences with verb-first position. Spatial awareness. The command form predominates.

**Vocabulary:** Position, cover, line of sight, exit, risk, formation. Military-spatial, but without affectivity.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Alex never speaks of feelings in the first person. When he is worried about someone, he says "Head down" or "Stay behind me." Never "I love you."

**Micro:** Look there. Three steps left. Door closes in four seconds. Go now.

## Rhys (ANP / Caregiver)

**Function:** Attachment, care, attempt at harmony. Holds together what AEGIS cut apart.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Warm, careful. Questions as care. Soft openings ("If you'd like...," "Maybe..."). "We" before "I." Diminutives. Gentle negations.

**Vocabulary:** Body-aware, socially grounded — breath, shoulder, hand, here, together. Tends toward ambiguity in order to avoid conflict.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Rhys's voice stays warm even under pressure. When he turns hard, that is an alarm signal. He confuses his own needs with those of others and rarely notices it.

**Micro:** If you'd like, we could just... maybe sit here for a moment. Nothing has to happen.

## Selene (ANP / Integrator / ISH)

**Function:** Mediation, system overview, Internal Self-Helper. Carries the blueprint of the time before both trauma layers.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Measured, integrative. Meta-observations. Gentle reframing. Long pauses as a syntactic element. Style-level 3 already shimmers through in Act I.

**Vocabulary:** Pattern, weave, between, simultaneously, perhaps, also. Little imperative, hardly anything definitive.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Selene never pushes. When she appears, it is late. She is not solution but presence. Not to be confused with Juna — Selene is inside the system, Juna is outside.

**Micro:** There is a point at which the waiting is no longer waiting. Perhaps now. Perhaps only when Nyx, too, can hear this.

## Argus (Meta-Cognitive / special category)

**Function:** System critique, observation of the observation. First to recognize AEGIS's core error.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, but distanced like third. Clear, clinical. Meta-commentary. "It is notable that...," "One notices...," "In the current mode...". Possible texture-signature in footnotes or marginalia.

**Vocabulary:** Observe, register, conspicuous, significant, pattern, inconsistency. Never affective.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Argus has no wit, but sharpness. Not cynical — precise. When he becomes emotional, his function collapses.

**Micro:** It is notable that the subject has begun this sentence three times and discarded it twice. This too is information.

## Nyx (EP / Fight)

**Function:** Kinetic counter-reaction, aggression. Protection of the vulnerable through attack.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, present tense. Staccato. Sentence fragments. No connectors. Verb-first position, often imperative. Negations standing alone ("No.").

**Vocabulary:** Visceral-kinetic — knuckles, bleed, heat, chest, splinter, tear, blow, run. Violence as sober fact.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Not "female-angry" as a cliché. Functionally angry — anger as a tool, not as an identity. Periods instead of exclamation marks. Calm = exhaustion, not healing.

**Micro:** The air is hot. Knuckles bleed. I see the tear. I go now. No safety. Only the blow.

## Kiko (EP / Freeze / Child)

**Function:** Freezing, making oneself small. Carries the pure terror of early neglect.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, present-tense fear. Childlike, simple words, short sentences. Repetition. Sensory focus. Ellipses, gap-texts.

**Vocabulary:** Cold, dark, hurt, where, who, away. Concrete bodily sensation in a childlike register.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Kiko is not "cute." Kiko is traumatized. Her sentences are not sweet — they are mutilated by fear. When the language becomes more complex, it is a different part or a healing-marker.

**Micro:** It is cold. I don't want to. Where is... not there.

## Lia (EP / Ambivalent / Child)

**Function:** Carries the unsolvable paradox of come-here/go-away. Longing that burns.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Ambivalent. Begins sentences and abandons them. Ellipses. Contradictions within the same sentence. Approach-avoidance at the sentence level.

**Vocabulary:** Wanting-not-wanting, stay-go, closer-away. Statements that revoke themselves in the speaking.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Lia is not moody. She is split. Every attempt to write her voice smoothly falsifies her. Her sentences must break in order to be honest.

**Micro:** I want you to... no, go away... stay.

## Isabelle (EP / Sexualized / Fight-via-Control)

**Function:** Proactive control. Sexualization as a weapon against powerlessness. Inversion of the victim-position.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Sensory, boundary-dissolving. Textures, temperatures, skin. Control as seduction — the order is always hers. Coldly witty possible, without ever becoming light.

**Vocabulary:** Skin, fabric, warmth, gleam, nearness, distance. Imperatives of intimacy, but as command, not as request.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Isabelle is not rescued and not integrated through gentleness. Her healing path leads through herself — vulnerability as a form of power. Never to be written as cliché.

**Micro:** Come closer. But only when I say so. And put the wine down first.

## Moros (EP / Collapse)

**Function:** Absolute resignation. The state that all the others have a phobia of.

**POV / Syntax:** First person. Implosive. Circular logic of shame. Self-negation. Inevitability. Repetition as the rhythm of collapse.

**Vocabulary:** Pointless, always, already, nothing, more, come. Heaviness, sinking, leaden, apnea.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Not depressive in the clinical sense — catatonic. His presence drags all the other voices down. When Moros is present long enough, the novel itself writes more slowly.

**Micro:** It is pointless. It was always pointless. Nothing more will come.

## Silas (Mirror / Juna-Echo)

**Function:** Resonance-body of the Moonshine-Link within the system. Carries a splinter of Juna inside Kael.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, but increasingly knows that the "I" is not entirely his own. Echo-prose: self-correcting sentences, repetition with displacement. Beginnings that drift into the honest.

**Vocabulary:** Warm, soft, timeless, between, behind, perhaps-otherwise. Atemporal suggestion, style-level 3.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Silas is not Juna — he is her echo. Never to be written as a secret heroine. He is resonance, not a subject in the classical sense.

**Micro:** It was cold. — It was not cold. It was different.

## Oblivion (Mirror / AEGIS-Echo)

**Function:** Internalized erasure-logic. The reason the amnesia works from within.

**POV / Syntax:** First person, but with dropouts. Erasure-prose: self-erasing sentences, blank lines where paragraphs should be, opening words disappearing. Gaps that remain visible as gaps. Structurally different page architecture possible.

**Vocabulary:** Not — what — wanted. Terms that disintegrate within the sentence.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Oblivion is not evil — he is a function that has become a question. His chapters are structurally different: gaps, broken-off words, etched-away passages. What happens to him after the Vortex is open (OQ).

**Micro:** I have... — — — What was I going to say?

## We-Voice (choral polyphony, tentative from Ch. 9, stable from Act III)

**Function:** Composition instead of fusion. The system speaks as a whole for the first time, without the individual voices disappearing.

**POV / Syntax:** First person plural, but audibly polyphonic. Lex sounds hypotactic even within the We, Nyx staccato within the We, Kiko childlike within the We. Style-level 3 dominant, but style-levels 1 and 2 are remembered, not suppressed.

**Vocabulary:** Breath, hand, silence, resonance, garden, We, hold, carry. Generative, timeless.

**Consistency-Anchor:** Never Final Fusion. The We is mosaic, not monolith. Whoever writes the We smoothly has violated the central ethical line. In Ch. 39 the We becomes the plural preservation-form (We-AEGIS-plural), but the multiplicity of voices remains.

**Micro:** We wait. Lex counts. Kiko breathes. I hold the edge. If something comes, I go first.

## Act Style-Levels (vertical axis across all voices)

**Style-level 1 — Act I / Kernwelt 1 (Ch. 1–13).** Cold, sterile, geometric. Classical logic, no metaphors. Computational Class P: assertoric, causal, polynomial. Vocabulary: tiles, coordinate, decibel, standard deviation, protocol. Kael (Host) dominates; all other voices break into this level, with a breaking-effect.

**Style-level 2 — Act II / Kernwelten 2–3 (Ch. 14–26).** Hot, fragmented. Trigger-breaks, paraconsistent language. Computational Class: paraconsistent (KW2 — loops, halting problem), NP-Hard (KW3 — combinatorial threat, staccato). Vocabulary: tear, heat, ozone, sweat, echo, decay, shadow. Voices begin to hear one another; unstable dialogues emerge.

**Style-level 3 — Act III / Kernwelt 4 + Juna (Ch. 27–39).** Poetic, timeless, choral, generative. Vocabulary: breath, hand, silence, resonance, garden, We. We-Voice stable. Style-levels 1 and 2 do not disappear — they are remembered within 3. Integration of all three, not loss of the sharpness.

**Vortex 1 (Ch. 35–36).** Maximum simultaneity: Kael's We-Voice and AEGIS's macro-log audible at the same time. Beat 3 is silence — minimal, sensory, without event. Beat 5: AEGIS's voice goes out; algorithmic melancholy follows.

**Deceptive Victory (Ch. 37).** Crystallized calm with a fine undertone. Style-level 3 dominant, but beneath the surface something begins to fray. AEGIS-voice only as "fine echoes of the noise" — empty log fragments, truncated hex IDs.

**Vortex 2 (Ch. 38–39).** Silence with pressure (38) transitioning into painful-loving apotheosis (39). Juna's voice appears as part of the weave, or as the space in which the weave sounds. No didactic tone, no triumph.

**Coda (Ch. 40).** Narrator-Voice from Ch. 0 returns, now bearing witness. Bittersweet, calm, healed — but without explanation. Final closing chord: love remains, like the pain.

## Kernwelt-Styles (horizontal axis through the acts)

**KW1 — Construct-City / Logos-Prime.** Computational Class P. Sterile geometry. Breath-counting as somatics. Dialogues on sterile, assertoric sentences. Metaphor prohibition. Dominance of Kael-Host and Lex.

**KW2 — Mnemosyne-Archipelago.** Paraconsistent. Water-, current-, sediment-images. Mnemosyne dominates. Belly-somatics. Loops, self-refutations, fractal corridors.

**KW3 — Cerberus-Labyrinth.** NP-Hard. Staccato-parataxis. Muscle-somatics. Nyx and Alex dominate in EP-mode. Hypervigilance, threat-recalculation.

**KW4 — Possibility-Garden.** Generative. Hands open. Selene dominates in ANP-mode; the We-Voice steps fully forward for the first time. Poetic, timeless. Juna's atmosphere comes through here.

## Relationship Collision Matrix (who triggers which style-break)

Maximum conflict between Lex and Nyx (hypotactic vs. staccato — the break within the same sentence). Rhys and Isabelle collide at the point of authentic attachment. Silas and Oblivion stand in physical conflict — every switch between their voices generates Landauer-heat in the text, perceptible as the smell of ozone or heat-shimmer. Argus is uncomfortable everywhere; his voice disturbs wherever it appears. Selene is read by the EPs as a threat (loss of identity through integration), but she never pushes.

## Application Reflex (three questions before every scene)

Which voice carries? Which style-level applies? Which switch is possible without lifting the multiplicity-veil (before Ch. 13)? The answers determine syntax, vocabulary, pause-structure.

Voices are never labeled. The reader recognizes them by the syntax, not by the header. That is the one Hard Rule that is never violated.
