## 3 · Fresh-pass findings (new in v2)

### NEW — MAJOR-1 · name-quarantine convention broken inside DESIGN.md
v1 quarantined every personal name to §7 (the doc's own stated "INTERNAL ONLY"
names zone — §7 header L449, NAVIGATION L38). v2 lets names leak into four other
sections:
- **§0 revision log** (L25-37) — uses `lex`, `arg`, `mor`, "Moros", "Juna",
  "Silas".
- **§2 Source-Fidelity Map** (L98-110) — "MOROS-echo", "RHYS", "LIA", "KIKO",
  "LEX", "ARGUS", "NYX", "JUNA", "SILAS", "AEGIS axiom", "OBLIVION", "KAEL".
- **§3 voice-key legend + table** (L160-186) — "AEGIS-as-system-logic" (L162),
  and section-meta-style caps echoed in beat cells.
- **§6 art direction** (L413) — "**Silas's gold leaf**".

**Verdict — this is a discipline regression, not acceptable internal annotation.**
Reasoning: the hard rule (NAVIGATION L143-149) bars names from *output* fields,
and v2 does keep them out of every output field — so this is **not a CRITICAL**.
But the document established its OWN stricter convention (names live in §7 and
ONLY §7), and v1 honoured it. v2 silently relaxed it. The danger is operational,
not legal: §2/§3 are the tables a lyric-writer or art-director reads at the
handoff, and once "MOROS-echo", "RHYS", "AEGIS axiom", or "Silas's gold leaf"
sit in the working brief beside the track they map to, a name-leak into an
actual style box or art prompt is one careless copy-paste away. The §7 firewall
exists precisely to make that copy-paste impossible. **Required fix:** return §0,
§2, §3, and §6 to function-form (e.g. "the heavy-voice echo", "the warm-voice",
"the first system axiom", "no gold leaf — no Kintsugi"); cross-reference §7 by
*function*, never by name. Restore §7 as the sole names zone. (Note: §2's caps
mirror section-meta's annotation column, which itself uses names — but
section-meta is a SOURCE file, exempt; DESIGN.md §2 is the brief and is not.)
