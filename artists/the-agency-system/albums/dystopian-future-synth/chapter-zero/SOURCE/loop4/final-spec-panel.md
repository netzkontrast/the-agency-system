## 3 · Long-form assessment (70–100 min, ~13 tracks ≈ 5.5–7.5 min avg)

**Fidelity — confirmed safe.** Length does not change a single source fact. The
extra minutes are sonic/atmospheric expanse, not added lyrics, so the lean
word-counts in §8 (140–220 words ambient/electroacoustic; ~200–350 words
rock/industrial) still hold. The source itself supports the expanse: `vorwort`'s
*das Nichts*, `herz`'s spacelessness/timelessness, and `kollaps`'s pulse-less
free-fall are all explicitly *durational* states in the prose — long instrumental
beds are faithful, not invented. **No fidelity objection to long-form.**

**Listenability — earns its length on the right tracks; risks sag on two.**
- **Earn the minutes:** T1 (the slow-burn boot/listening from silence), T2 (the
  void as ground state), T6 (the Click + the protected second-movement
  inner-space outro — this beat *wants* room), T9 (airless self-strangulation —
  contained dread sustains), T11 (the pulse-less drone-collapse implosion — the
  void after the peak is a durational floor). These five carry expanse naturally.
- **Keep tight:** T5 (Strike — kinetic rock loses its bite if padded), T8 (the
  hush before the cliff must *load the spring*, not sprawl), and especially
  **T13** (the §8 anti-heal/anti-alienation plan and the loop3 craft consensus
  both lean toward a *brief* slammed-door coda, ~90–120 s of placed flatness — do
  NOT stretch it; a long flat coda tips from devastation into anticlimax, which
  is exactly the T13 risk flagged across the loop).
- **The sag watch:** T7+T8 are the false-calm pair. They must stay clean and lean
  — the extra album minutes belong to T1/T6/T11, not here. If long-form padding
  lands on T7/T8 the two-track deflation becomes a three-or-four-minute deflation
  and the false-ending risk is realized.

**Feasibility — needs ONE explicit addition (the assembly note).** §4/§8 do not
yet describe long-form assembly. The recommended addition (for the final design
pass, not a blocker): a short **"Long-form assembly"** subsection in §8 stating —
(1) 5–8 min tracks are built by **Suno extend/continue or multi-section
generation, then comped**, never expected from one short clip; (2) the **owned
KOH stem (Layer A) is the spine stitched continuously across the comped
sections** so a multi-pass track still reads as one coherent KOH state — this is
the asset that makes long-form *cohere*, and it already exists in the design,
just not pointed at the long-form problem; (3) the **host Persona must hold
across every continue/extend pass** within a track (reinforces the existing
single-point-of-failure note); (4) name which tracks get the length (the five
above) so durations are intentional, not emergent; (5) confirm in one line that
**lyric density does NOT scale with duration** (the extra time is instrumental —
prevents lyric-writer from padding word-counts to "fill" a 7-minute track and
breaching the §8 density ceilings). None of this changes a decision already made;
it makes the *existing* owned-spine + Persona discipline explicitly cover the
long-form case.

## 4 · Ranked findings

### MUST-FIX before freeze
1. **Add a long-form assembly note to §8** (the 5-point note in §3 above). This
   is the one genuine gap the new long-form decision opens: the production plan
   assumes ~one-generation-per-track and does not state how 5–8 min tracks are

   *(Severity: the rest of §8 is adequate for short tracks; this is the delta the
   long-form lock requires.)*

### NICE-TO-HAVE (final design pass; none block freeze)
1. **Per-track target-duration map.** Pin which tracks get the length (T1, T6,
   T11 expand; T5, T8, T13 stay tight) directly in the §3 tracklist or §8, so
   the 70–100 min total is composed deliberately rather than emerging from
   generation. Folds naturally into the must-fix note.
2. **One-line cross-reference on T6 BPM cell** ("ritard/reboot is post — see §8")
   to remove the only spot a reader might think an in-prompt tempo move is
   expected. Cosmetic.
3. **Restate the T13 brevity guidance explicitly** ("~90–120 s; do not extend")
   in §8's anti-heal block, now that long-form is the album default — to inoculate
   against the natural pull to lengthen every track.
