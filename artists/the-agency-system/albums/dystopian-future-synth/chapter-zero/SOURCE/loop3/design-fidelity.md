> **Concern:** MAXIMAL FIDELITY. One of three isolated Round-3 design variants.
> This document pursues a single goal to its strongest form: render *Kapitel 0
> — Kohärenz Protokoll* into music as a 1:1 transfer — every beat in chapter

> tier / paradox / persistence value exact per `section-meta.md`, every verbatim

## 1. Thesis emphasis — fidelity as the organizing principle

The chapter is a single descent with two lies built into it: a coherence meter
that reads *nominal* twice (0.998, then 0.991) right up to the cliff, and a
final "1.00" that is really 0.00 wearing a mask. **Maximal fidelity means the
music must lie exactly where the chapter lies and crash exactly where it
crashes** — not one beat early for drama, not one beat late for relief. The
album is the chapter's own KOH curve made audible: homeostasis 0.998 →
erosion → forced rupture at the Klick → a re-forged 0.998 calm → the JUNA cliff
(0.991 → 0.21) → cascade → trough 0.18 → surgical partition 0.00→1.00 → a host
counting tiles in an empty corridor. Every value is the source's own number;
every voice enters on the exact section the source introduces it and is silent
everywhere else; every verbatim anchor (the axiom, the three-beat denial, the
two failed sectors, the sixteen bindings, the "Klick," the 14,832 warnings, the
2,304 tiles, the counting coda) survives intact. The difficulty of the back
half is not softened — **the difficulty is the content.** If a listener finds
T9 airless, T10 a flood, T13 unbearable in its flatness, the transfer is
working.

## 3. KOH spine · voices · dynamics through the fidelity lens

**What the source demands of the KOH spine (exact, in order):**
0.998 (T1 vorwort) → 0.94 (T1 rauschen) → 0.91 (T2) → 0.86 (T3) → 0.82 (T4) →
0.74 (T5) → **0.58 (T6, the Click — first KERNEL PANIC)** → 0.61 (T7 intro
way-station) → **0.998 (T7, FIRST false-calm reset)** → **0.991 (T8, SECOND
false-calm reset)** → **0.21 (T9, crash, but tier stays ALERT)** → 0.21 (T10) →
**0.18 (T11, trough)** → **0.00 → 1.00 (T12, forced)** → 1.00-hollow (T13).
The spine is the chapter's own meter; the music must track these numbers
literally (owned C-drone master stem + automation under each Suno track).

**The two false-calm resets are non-negotiable structure.** The drone must
climb back to *full clean 0.998 density* at T7 and stay near-nominal through
T8's 0.991 — the dread is precisely that **the meter lies right up to the
cliff.** Softening either reset (e.g. leaving residual menace in the drone)
destroys the source's central trap. The system *feels safe* deep into the
album; that false safety is the setup for the misclassification at T9.

**The crash lives INSIDE schrecken (T9), before the cascade.** Fidelity insists
the crash to 0.21 happens at T9's internal-resonance escalation — *not* at the
cascade (T10), which is the *aftermath* of the crash, not its cause. And T9's
telemetry tier stays ALERT (Tier 2) even at KOH 0.21: the chapter's own
"misaligned coherence" is that the number (0.21) and the state-label (ALERT)
disagree. **The drone follows the number; the glitch-tier/palette follows the
tier. Honor both.**

**The paradox climb 0.84 → 0.99 on T9** (km L217, L237) runs *opposite* to the
KOH crash — paradox rising as coherence falls. Fidelity stages this as a
counter-motion: a thin rising element (paradox) against the collapsing drone
(KOH). 0.67 stays on T8 only.

**Max polyphony at the cascade's stack moment (T10).** sm L42 demands all four
echoes roaring at once at the peak. Honored via the locked hybrid: legible
round early, **simultaneous wall at the climax** — the loudness peak of the
entire album (the back-half contour is T9 airless → **T10 peak** → T11
implode).

**Voices — exactly what the chapter contains, entering exactly where it
introduces them:** narrator (T1, T7) · proto-host→host (T1–3, 6, 10–13) ·
heavy-voice (T1 faint seed, T10 full) · small-voice (T2, T10) · warm-voice
(T3, T6) · reaching-voice (T3, T10) · logic-voice (**first T4**, then T5, T6) ·
annotating-voice/witness (**first T4**, also T6) · kinetic-voice (T5, T12) ·
system/watch (T6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12) · signal/anomaly (texture: **strong T8,
residual T9**, never a voice) · mirror-echo (**first T9**, never earlier;
also T10) · sweep-voice (T12 only). **ABSENT and NOT forced in** (Sprachdns
L118–119): integrator, protector, sexualized-override, We-Voice. Chapter 0 ends
in amputation — **there is no integration chorus.** Adding any absent voice, or
introducing the mirror-echo before T9, is a fidelity defect.

**Dynamics (the locked back-half shape):** build → peak → void. T9 compressed/

generation.** This is not a feasibility hedge — it is the only way the chapter's
*literal* numeric curve (0.998 → 0.991 → 0.21 → 0.18 → 0.00→1.00) can be true

the bodies.

## 5. Deliberate sacrifices (honest input for the adversarial agent)

Pure fidelity *costs* listenability and feasibility. Named explicitly:

1. **T9 with the paradox numbers sung (B5 call = keep).** Reciting "zero point
   eight four → zero point nine nine" inside an already-airless, claustrophobic
   industrial track risks reading as cold/clinical filler to a casual listener —
   the self-attack (the chapter's least-legible beat) gets *more* obscured, not
   less. Fidelity keeps the numbers because they are the chapter's in-text
   signature for misaligned coherence; the cost is reduced cold-listen
   legibility. **(This clashes with an accessibility instinct to strip them.)**

2. **The two false-calm resets sacrifice momentum.** Climbing the drone all the
   way back to clean 0.998 at T7 and holding near-nominal through T8 means the
   album *deflates* for two tracks right after the T6 rupture — the listener may
   read T7/T8 as a loss of energy or a false ending. Fidelity demands it (the
   meter must lie before the cliff), but it is a real engagement risk.

3. **T13's verbatim flatness tests patience to the point of alienation.** A coda
   that is *only* a host counting tiles, forbidden from naming the grief it
   carries, with no resolution and no warmth, is an uncompromising "slammed
   door." It risks landing as anticlimax or emptiness rather than horror for a
   listener not tracking the arc. Fidelity insists the void is conveyed only by
   what is *not* said — but this is the album's single biggest aesthetic gamble.

**Secondary fidelity costs (feed the same review):**
- **T10 max polyphony is the most labor-intensive track to build** (multi-pass
  comp, per-echo generation, 12-stem extraction, phase-offset comp); the
  climactic simultaneous wall risks reading as "a wall of noise / a crowd"
  rather than "one mind fracturing."
- **The tier/number divergence on T9** (drone follows 0.21, palette/glitch
  follows ALERT) is internally "contradictory" by design and may confuse a
  listener or a mix engineer who expects state and intensity to agree.
- **The bare untranslated "Klick"** and the verbatim broken syntax (the
  three-beat denial's misplaced period; T12's em-dash gaps) resist clean vocal
  delivery and will fight Suno's tendency to normalize phrasing — high
  iteration cost, and a casual listener may hear them as errors rather than
  intent.
