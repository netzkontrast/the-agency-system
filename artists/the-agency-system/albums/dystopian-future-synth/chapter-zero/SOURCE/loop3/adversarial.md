## 2 · The Maximal-Clash Set (ranked by centrality)

Ranking principle: clashes that shape the album's **thesis line, its ending, and
its central tragic beat** come first; per-track texture choices come last. Each:
**(a)** irreducible question · **(b)** each design's position in its own terms +
cite · **(c)** steel-manned stake (what genuinely breaks if each side wins) ·
**(d)** tag.

### CLASH 1 — The founding-axiom wording (the album's thesis line)
**(a)** Does the title-track hook sing the verbatim source axiom, or the reworded singable variant?
**(b)** **FID:** verbatim-locked **"It is what *prevents* it from not being"** (km L117 = *"Es ist, was es verhindert, dass es nicht ist."*; NAV L103); repeated melodic hook, "Do not paraphrase" (FID §A, §2 T6). **FEA:** also verbatim "prevents" as melodic hook (FEA §2 T6). **ACC:** **"It is what *keeps* it from not being"** — chosen for a front-stressed K-hook landing on "keeps," sung 3× as the title chorus; ACC concedes it "departs from the §7-locked verbatim line" (ACC §2 T6, §5.1).
**(c)** If ACC wins: the album's single thesis line — the negation-as-existence axiom that the entire back half detonates — is no longer the chapter's sentence. "keeps from not being" is a *retention* metaphor; "prevents from not being" is an *active defense against negation* (the corollary "Being becomes the active, ceaseless defense against non-being," km L119, only follows from "prevents"). The reword quietly softens the violence of the thesis into something almost gentle, and it does so on the most-cited, most-repeated line on the record. If FID/FEA win: the hook stresses a weaker syllable ("what") and ACC's claim is the chorus "hooks weaker" — a real but recoverable singability cost (ACC itself notes the locked line "can still be sung").
**(d)** **OUT-OF-BOUNDS BREACH** — names the wall: **source truth / verbatim-locked axiom** (km L117; NAV L103 ground-truth tier 2; FID §A "Do not paraphrase"). This is not a tradeoff between two legal options; ACC altered a verbatim-locked line. The arbiter must rule the reword out and decide only *whether the locked line can still be a strong hook*.

### CLASH 2 — The T13 ending: pure sterile flatness vs a warm melodic ghost
**(a)** May any warmth — even an instrumental ghost the host ignores — exist under the coda, or is the void conveyed ONLY by what is not said?
**(b)** **FID:** HARD GUARDRAIL M2 — host names nothing; "**The void is conveyed ONLY by what the host does not say**"; **rendered cold, NO Kintsugi** ("the source's content is amputation, the gold is the page's lie," sm L59–60; FID §2 T13, §4 anti-heal). **ACC:** adds **a 1–2 s warm melodic ghost of T3's pretty melody** the host ignores — "the production remembers; he doesn't"; self-flags it "edges toward 'the production comments on grief'" (ACC §2 T13, §5.6). **FEA:** also places **the T3 warm-fragment ghost as Post** under T13 (FEA §2 T13, §3 post shot-list) — buildable, treated as a neutral mix gesture.
**(c)** If ACC/FEA win: a melodic callback the listener recognizes is the production *narrating absent grief* — exactly the comfort/Kintsugi gesture the house aesthetic and FID's M2 forbid. The "sameness of the timbre is the knife" payoff is undercut by an external sentimental cue that does the emotional work the flatness was supposed to do by subtraction. If FID wins: ACC's fear — that the coda "lands as anticlimax or emptiness rather than horror for a listener not tracking the arc" (FID's own §5.3) — is unmitigated; the album's biggest aesthetic gamble runs at full risk with no safety net for the cold listener.
**(d)** **OUT-OF-BOUNDS BREACH (ACC and FEA)** — names the wall: **no-Kintsugi / no-comfort rule** (sm L59–60; NAV "Ch.0 has NO kintsugi"; FID M2). Note this breach is shared: FEA committed it too (FEA §2 T13 post shot-list), which makes it more dangerous — two of three designs treat the warm ghost as acceptable, so the arbiter cannot assume a majority instinct is safe. The legitimate residual *tradeoff* underneath (how to keep a cold listener oriented without warmth) is genuine and should be re-routed, but the warm ghost itself is out of bounds.

### CLASH 3 — T3 warmth: deliberately *pretty* vs verbatim flat-affect
**(a)** Is T3 the album's one genuinely beautiful, consonant melodic moment, or flat-affect warmth that is only ever a logged, curdling *lesson*?
**(b)** **ACC:** the warm-voice phrase is "the one consonant, almost-tender hook on the record," "**deliberately the only one**, so its theft hurts"; concedes "a deliberate breach of the no-comfort rule, isolated to one phrase" (ACC §2 T3, §5.2). **FID:** verbatim warm-voice lines (km L55/L67) whose payload is that connection "makes you vulnerable" (km L71) and immediately curdles to "a first whiff of systemic logic. Cold. Necessary." (km L73); no "pretty" license — the warmth is a "trügerische Atempause" (deceptive pause), not consonance (FID §2 T3). **FEA:** warm fragment is a sequenced register, reused as a seed; no aesthetic claim either way (FEA §2 T3).
**(c)** If ACC wins: the album buys one moment of real beauty to make the loss land harder — but a flicker of genuine comfort in a record whose entire spine is "no warmth that reads as comfort" risks reading as the house aesthetic blinking, and it is the *source* of the contested T13 ghost (Clash 2). If FID wins: T3's warmth stays a cold logged datum; the "theft hurts more if it was beautiful" intuition is denied, and ACC's claim that a stranger needs one consonant anchor to feel the later loss is rejected — a real legibility cost for the book-blind listener.
**(d)** **OUT-OF-BOUNDS BREACH (ACC)** — names the wall: **no-comfort / "trügerische Atempause" rule** (house aesthetic; sm warmth-is-deceptive). ACC self-flags it as a deliberate breach. The underlying *tradeoff* — does a stranger need a consonant anchor for the loss to register? — is genuine and survives even after the breach is ruled on. (FEA's neutral position means this is a 2-vs-1 with FID isolated.)

### CLASH 4 — T9 paradox-number recitation (B5): sung vs stripped
**(a)** Are the paradox-meter decimals (0.84 → 0.99) sung/spoken inside T9, or stripped so the self-attack reads as sound?
**(b)** **FID:** **kept, sung/spoken** — "they are the chapter's in-text fidelity signature for misaligned coherence; the misaligned-coherence paradox is literally *audible as text* only if the number (0.84/0.99 climbing) sits against the state (0.21 crashing)" (km L217/L237; FID §2 T9 #3, §5.1). **FEA:** **stripped** — "on a dense industrial gen they read as cold filler and obscure the self-attack… one fewer dense element to fight = a cleaner gen" (FEA §4, §5.1). **ACC:** **stripped** (option b) — "so the self-attack reads as sound, not arithmetic"; numbers rationed to T8/T11/T13 only (ACC §2 T9, §5.3).
**(c)** If FID wins: T9 retains the audible counter-motion (paradox rising as coherence falls) that is the chapter's literal diagnosis of misaligned coherence — but inside an already airless, claustrophobic, mid-volume track the decimals risk reading as clinical filler that *further* obscures the album's least-legible beat (FID concedes this exact cost). If FEA/ACC win: the cleanest, most legible T9 — but the album loses an audible source fact, and the paradox must be carried entirely by owned-stem cliff + mirror-echo, which may not communicate "the meter says coherent while the system is in agony" at all.
**(d)** **GENUINE TRADEOFF.** B5 was explicitly *routed open* to design (DEC §B5: "Decide whether they are sung, spoken-low, or rendered as texture"). Both keep and strip are legal options; this is the cleanest 2-vs-1 (FEA+ACC strip vs FID keep) and a true fidelity-vs-legibility tension. Note: nobody proposed the middle option (spoken-low / one brief pass) the routing offered — the arbiter has an unclaimed compromise available.

### CLASH 5 — Polyphony default: "one mind fracturing" vs sequential-roster
**(a)** Across the multi-voice tracks the LOCKED T10 round→stack does NOT cover (T3, T5, T12), is the default *simultaneous overlap* (one consciousness splitting) or *sequential / take-turns* (legible, buildable)?
**(b)** **FID:** wants the source's simultaneity; even within locked T10 insists the proto-host/mirror-echo stay a phase-offset *pair* so it reads as "one consciousness fracturing, not a smeared single lead" (FID §2 T10, §3). **FEA:** **sequential-default everywhere** — T3 sequenced-not-stacked, T12 voices take turns, T10 stacks only at peak; explicitly names the risk it "can read as a procession or a roster rather than a mind splitting," citing CLAUDE.md's "dissociation is amnesia-terror, never a crew menu" (FEA §5.4). **ACC:** sequential-then-stack for legibility; relies on timbre+register+syntax tics so a stranger IDs each voice in 3 s (ACC §2 T10, §3 voice table, §5.4).
**(c)** If FEA/ACC win: every multi-voice beat outside T10's climax is rendered one-voice-at-a-time. The source's central image — *one* mind fracturing into simultaneous fragments — is repeatedly staged as a sequence of distinct characters taking turns, i.e. the exact "crew menu / roster" failure the project warns against. The dissociation reads as a cast list, not a splitting. If FID wins: genuine simultaneity on T3/T12 multiplies comp labor and risks the "wall of noise / a crowd" smear FID itself names as a cost (FID §5 secondary), and fights Suno's reliability (FEA's whole concern).
**(d)** **GENUINE TRADEOFF** (with a latent breach risk). The LOCKED T10 decision only partly reconciles this — it fixes the *cascade* but leaves T3/T5/T12 polyphony open. The tradeoff is legibility/buildability vs the source's simultaneity. **Flag for the arbiter:** FEA's sequential-default, *if applied as a blanket rule*, risks crossing the "never a crew menu" aesthetic wall (CLAUDE.md) on the non-locked tracks — so this is a tradeoff that can *tip into* a breach if pushed to its pure form. Not yet a breach; a tradeoff to watch.

### CLASH 6 — The two false-calm resets: load-bearing lie vs momentum risk
**(a)** Do T7/T8 climb all the way back to clean 0.998 / hold 0.991 because "the meter must lie before the cliff," accepting the deflation — or is that two-track de-escalation a momentum/false-ending hazard to be managed?
**(b)** **FID:** **non-negotiable structure** — drone to full clean 0.998 at T7, near-nominal through T8's 0.991; "Softening either reset destroys the source's central trap"; openly accepts the cost: "the album *deflates* for two tracks… the listener may read T7/T8 as a loss of energy or a false ending" (FID §3, §5.2). **ACC:** keeps both but *reframes their function for the ear* — T7 = "big exhale / false resolution… resets the ear so the back half can land harder," T8 = "rising hush… loads the spring"; the justification is engagement, not the meter (ACC §2 T7/T8, §3). **FEA:** preserves both as owned-stem automation; no engagement claim, treats them as mix facts (FEA §2 T7/T8).
**(c)** If FID's framing wins: the resets are protected at full depth as the chapter's central trap (the system feels safe right up to the misclassification) — but the album genuinely deflates for two tracks after the T6 rupture, and a casual listener may disengage or hear a false ending. If ACC's framing wins: the resets survive but are *justified by ear-reset rather than by the lie*, so there is pressure to keep enough residual tension that they don't deflate — which is precisely the "leaving residual menace in the drone" that FID says "destroys the source's central trap."
**(d)** **GENUINE TRADEOFF** (framing-level). All three *keep* the resets — so this is not a structural conflict but a conflict over *how clean they must be*. FID demands full-clean (the lie is the point); ACC's ear-reset rationale creates downward pressure on the cleanliness FID requires. The arbiter must decide whether the resets are protected as "the meter lying" (full clean, accept deflation) or "an ear-reset device" (manage for engagement) — these point at different drone densities.

### CLASH 7 — T13 brevity/flatness as content vs alienation risk
**(a)** Is T13's uncompromising flatness (host counts, names nothing, no resolution, no warmth) kept at full strength as the payload, or is the "slammed door" engineered with enough placed-gesture craft to avoid reading as a file cut-off / anticlimax?
**(b)** **FID:** "T13's verbatim flatness tests patience to the point of alienation"; kept at full strength because "the flatness IS the content" / "the void is conveyed only by what is *not* said"; named as "the album's single biggest aesthetic gamble" (FID §2 T13, §5.3). **ACC:** the flat ending is "**the slammed door**" — engineered with a *placed* open-interval hold "so the flatness reads as *intention*, not a file cut-off," plus the (contested) warm ghost; brevity 90–120 s (ACC §2 T13, §3). **FEA:** one Gen, 2–3 rolls, owned-MIDI arp fallback if rolls "heal," held open interval, 90–120 s (FEA §2 T13).
**(c)** If FID wins (no warm ghost, max flatness): the purest "same timbre now hollow" payoff, but maximal alienation risk for the book-blind stranger — may land as emptiness, not horror. If ACC wins: the door-slam is crafted to read as deliberate, but at the cost of the contested warm ghost (Clash 2) and a slight softening of "the flatness IS the content." Note FEA and ACC agree on brevity (90–120 s) and the placed open-interval hold — that is consensus craft; the *only* live disagreement here is the warm ghost, which is Clash 2.
**(d)** **GENUINE TRADEOFF** on flatness-depth-vs-orientation; the warm-ghost component is **already tagged BREACH in Clash 2**. Ranked low because, stripped of the ghost, the three converge on brevity + placed open-interval hold; the residual is a tone-calibration question, not a structural fork.
