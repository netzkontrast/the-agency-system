## 5 · Headline

The divergent set **can be reconciled with near-zero fidelity loss**, because the
three breaches (Clashes 1–3) are *unforced* — each is a self-flagged ACC/FEA
choice that contradicts a directly-cited source fact, not a genuine fork. Rule all
three out (verbatim "prevents"; no warm ghost; no engineered prettiness) and the
listenability concerns underneath survive intact via fidelity-safe craft (prosodic
hook placement, HOST-timbre recognition, tremulous-not-pretty warmth). Of the four
remaining tradeoffs, three already converge (5/6/7 resolve by beat-by-beat staging,
full-clean resets, and placed-gesture flatness), and the fourth (B5) has an
unclaimed middle — **spoken-low paradox numbers** — that no design took and that
threads fidelity and legibility together. The only residual fidelity *risk* is
quiet, not loud: FEA's blanket sequential polyphony, a logic-voice leak at T3, and
a trimmed T6 second movement. Watch those three.
