## 1 · Thesis emphasis — feasibility frames the album

Suno does not make albums; it makes **clips**. So Chapter Zero is engineered as
**thirteen self-contained, low-risk Suno recipes plus one owned spine and one
post shot-list** — and *everything that Suno cannot reliably generate is moved
off the generator entirely.* Each track is a vocals-first style box (≤2 genre
tags, 4–7 descriptors), one or two Personas carrying timbre continuity so the box
never double-specifies gender/register, locked C minor, and a body of plain-text
lyrics with numbers spelled as words and zero square brackets. The continuous KOH
meter, the boot, the ritard-reboot, the datamosh, the KOH cliff, the shatter, and
the collapse crush are **owned-stem or post gestures, never prompted** — that is
the single decision that turns a "13-track concept that fights the platform" into
"13 clips that drop into a session and master in two passes." Where the source
wants four voices at once or a decimal recited inside an industrial wall, I
**simplify toward the build**: sequential-with-pan, owned-stem automation, and
struck-out density. The album ships because nothing in a prompt asks Suno to do a
thing Suno does badly.

## 3 · KOH spine · Personas · post shot-list · mastering — the executable architecture

### KOH spine = one owned stem, automated (NOT generated)

Suno cannot run a shared oscillator across 13 prompts, so the spine is a **mix
asset**:

- **Own ONE sustained C-drone master stem** at the 0.998 clean state (one sine,
  sub-only). All KOH motion (0.998 → 0.94 → 0.58 → 0.21 → 0.18 → forced 1.00) is
  **automation on that one file** — partials, detune, saturation, clip,
  collapse-to-noise — rendered per segment and laid under each track at a low,
  constant sub level. This is the only way the decimal sequence is *literally*
  true in the audio.
- **Layer-B anchor verbatim on all 13 boxes** so Suno's beds sit over the owned
  drone without key-clashing.
- **Lock C minor in every box.** All detune (T9–T11) happens in the owned stem,
  never by prompting a key change. Off-key generations → **Suno Studio Pitch
  Transpose**, not a re-roll burn.

### Personas — exactly two, per-section assignment where voices alternate

- **HOST** (male mid-baritone, weary, dry close-mic): T1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 11, 12,
  13. The reuse on **T13** is the album's load-bearing emotional payoff (A11) —
  build it before T1.
- **WATCH** (cold, near-monotone, formant-flattened): T6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12.
- **Per-section Persona assignment (A6)** on **T6 / T11 / T12** so the
  proto-host↔system handoff inside one generation is explicit before
  lyric-writer runs (see §2 metatag columns).
- **When a Persona carries gender/register, those descriptors are DROPPED from
  the style box.** Custom-Model fine-tune for the back half = optional (open
  decision #1), Personas alone are sufficient to ship.

### Mastering — one pass per genre cluster, state-biased

state-axis bias from `mastering-presets.yaml`: S0 centre · S1 ~1–2 LU lower /
wider LRA · S2 ~1–2 LU louder / lower LRA / faster attack · S3 loudest / most
compressed (never past true_peak −1.0) · S4 toward centre but slightly wider LRA
than S0. Master in **one pass per cluster** (ambient / electroacoustic / cold
synth / industrial / drone-collapse) with per-track genre overrides (mastering
SKILL Step 1.5).

**B4 — T9 masters at S2 (explicit feasibility call).** The LOCKED T9 decision
(tight/airless/mid-volume) and T9's source state-tag (Tier 2 / ALERT) **agree**:
S2. This is also the buildable call — it preserves loudness headroom so T10 can
be the true peak and T11 the implosion (build → peak → void), and it avoids a
flat back-half loud plateau that would force fight-the-limiter comps. **T9 = S2
mastering despite its T3 visual palette** — flavor and loudness tier are tracked
separately (one-line note for release-director).

## 4 · Lyric / pronunciation stance — the functional Suno hazards, pre-empted

- **Density ceilings (bucket README is a build prerequisite — A8).** Before
  lyric-writer runs, create
  `artists/the-agency-system/albums/dystopian-future-synth/README.md` with word
  ceilings + per-track Target Duration, or the density gate silently no-ops.
  Targets: **140–220 words** for ambient/electroacoustic (T1, 2, 3, 7, 13);
  **200–350 words** for rock/industrial (T5, 9, 10, 12). Keep all lean — dense
  lyrics over a 3–4 min industrial gen is where Suno mangles diction.
- **Number-spelling (Suno box only; streaming lyrics keep standard form).**
  `0.998`→"zero point nine nine eight"; `0.991`→"zero point nine nine one";
  `0.67/0.84/0.99`→spelled; `0.41`→"zero point four one" (A3); `0.21`,`0.18`
  spelled; `14,832`→"fourteen thousand eight hundred thirty-two";
  `2,304`→"two thousand three hundred four"; `21`→"twenty-one";
  sectors `280`/`692`→"two-eight-zero"/"six-nine-two". Logged in each track's
  Pronunciation Notes table so `check_pronunciation_enforcement` fires.
- **B5 — strip number-recitation from Track 9 (feasibility call).** T9 is the
  album's hardest legibility track (compressed, airless, mirror-echo
  self-attack). The paradox decimals (0.84, 0.99) are source-authentic but on a
  dense industrial gen they read as cold filler and obscure the self-attack. **I
  strip them from T9** and let the owned-stem cliff + mirror-echo carry the
  paradox. Numbers stay where they are iconic and uncrowded: **T8, T11, T13**
  (and T7's single 0.41). One fewer dense element to fight = a cleaner gen.
- **Bracket policy.** **No square brackets in lyric bodies.**
  `[SYSTEM-STATUS — NOMINAL]` → "system status nominal";
  `[PROTOCOL KOH_1.0 — INITIATED]` → "protocol K-O-H one point zero, initiated".
  Brackets are reserved for section/voice metatags only.
- **Token-bias guard + ordering (A5).** The album's core vocabulary (echo, noise,
  shadow, mirror, whisper) collides with Suno's bias list. The **Suno box carries
  only the already-spelled / phonetic form** (e.g. "K-O-H", not "KOH"), and
  `Do not change any words. Sing exactly as written.` sits at the top of every
  box to lock it. **pronunciation-specialist applies phonetic substitutions
  BEFORE the token-bias guard is set**, so the guard never fights the deliberate
  spellings.
- **Narrator intros (A4).** Each narrator intro (T1, T7) is a **separate
  generation** whose own lyrics begin at `[Verse 1]`/`[Intro]`. The "hard-cut" is
  a **mix/edit butt-join** between two clips, NOT a tag bridging one clip to
  another. 2–4 lines of direct address, then the body clip takes over.
- **"Sing exactly as written" guard** is non-negotiable on this album — the
  phonetic spellings and the cold/dry diction both depend on it.

## 5 · Deliberate sacrifices (honest input for the adversarial agent)

Pushing feasibility to its uncompromising form costs the following. I name them
plainly so they can be contested downstream:

1. **T9 paradox decimals removed entirely (B5 → strip).** Fidelity has a real
   claim that 0.84/0.99 are in-text logs (`kapitel-0.md` L217/L237) and are T9's
   paradox-meter *signature*. Stripping them for a cleaner gen **loses an audible
   source fact**. A fidelity agent will likely want at least option (c) — one
   brief pass. I chose the build over the log.
2. **T10 "alle Echos auf einmal" softened to round→stack with the stack only at
   the climax.** The LOCKED hybrid already concedes this, but my feasibility
   framing leans hard into the *sequential* portion (panned, time-offset, the
   mirror-echo never simultaneous-equal with the HOST bed — B3) to de-risk the
   comp. The literal *simultaneity* of the source's "all echoes at once" is
   present only in the brief climactic pile. A fidelity reading wants more
   simultaneity; I gave the comp legibility instead.
3. **T9 mastered at S2, not S3 (B4).** Source palette tags T9 with T3 energy
   (Flame). Mastering it at S2 to protect T10's headroom means T9's "algorithmic
   terror" reads as *contained dread* rather than *peak violence*. A listener
   expecting the back-half to feel like three escalating walls instead gets a
   build; that is a real flattening of the source's extremity (it is also exactly
   what the LOCKED T9 decision asks for — but the loudness *call* is mine, and a
   fidelity/intensity advocate may argue it understates `schrecken`).
4. **Polyphony capped at "sequential / panned" almost everywhere.** T3 registers
   sequenced not stacked; T12 voices take turns; T10 only stacks at the peak.
   This is the single biggest buildability win and the single biggest nuance
   loss: the source's overlapping, simultaneous fracturing of one mind is
   repeatedly rendered as *one-voice-at-a-time*, which can read as a procession
   or a roster rather than a mind splitting. (The CLAUDE.md "dissociation is
   amnesia-terror, never a crew menu" warning is the exact risk I'm trading
   against.)
5. **Per-track durations kept lean (140–350 words) and brevity enforced on T13
   (90–120s).** This protects diction and the density gate, but caps how much
   narrative the longer beats (T6's two movements, T10's flood, T12's partition)
   can carry in-lyric. Some source detail will be carried by *sound and post*
   rather than *words* — fidelity may read that as under-stating beats that the
   chapter spends real prose on.
6. **In-track accelerando (T9) and ritard-reboot (T6) moved off the generator.**
   Suno honors these unreliably, so they become owned/post gestures. The
   trade: the *generated vocal* won't itself accelerate or stop-and-restart, so
   the performance can feel metronomically flat against an arrangement that moves
   around it. A purist would want the voice to ride the tempo curve.
