## The seven clashes — resolved

1. **Founding axiom — KEEP verbatim** "It is what prevents it from not being."
   Recover accessibility's hook *prosodically*: stress "**pre-VENTS**" on the
   downbeat as the 3-beat hook cell. No reword. ("keeps" reads cosy; "prevents"
   is the clenched refusal that is the album's engine — faithful *and* the
   better hook.)
2. **T13 warm ghost — CUT** (unanimous). Orientation comes cold: HOST-Persona
   **timbre recognition** + a placed **open-interval hold** + optional
   **dead-metronome callback** to T1's tick. If a safety net is ever needed, a
   non-melodic detuned drone partial — never the recognizable tune.
3. **T3 prettiness — NO literal consonant melody.** Achieve the felt high point
   by **closeness-then-withdrawal**: warm/tremulous/precarious timbre, legato
   close-mic vocal, the bed still refusing the tonic (modal, not consonant),
   then a hard curdle to "cold. necessary." This *is* the *trügerische
   Atempause* (km L57); it also de-fangs the Clash-2 ghost at the root.
4. **T9 paradox numbers (B5) — spoken-low / owned-texture middle.** Render the
   paradox climb **0.84 → 0.99** as a rising **owned-stem sonic counter-motion**
   against the crashing drone (best cold-listener legibility, kept off the
   fragile vocal), keeping **one buried spoken decimal** as a cold tell. Do not
   full-strip (loses the source signature) and do not full-sing (mangles the
   densest track). Preserves the number↔tier divergence = "misaligned
   coherence" (sm L41).
5. **Polyphony — per-track, NOT blanket.** Sequential on handoffs (T5/T12 — the
   source is sequential there); T3 carried by the shared HOST timbre with a
   brief **self-overlap** at the "stay / go / stay" ambivalence; **true
   simultaneity ONLY at the locked T10** round→stack comp. Reject any blanket
   sequential rule (it is both a "crew-menu/procession" infidelity and a
   roster-listen failure).
6. **False-calm resets (T7/T8) — KEEP both.** T7 **fully clean** (the meter must
   lie — a better exhale *and* a crueller setup); put the engagement/tension
   load on **T8's creeping arrangement**, not on dirtying the meter (no
   false-ending risk). Owned-drone automation, no build fork.
7. **T13 flatness — MAXIMUM flatness kept** (the flatness *is* the content), but
   **mandate the placed "slammed-door" gesture craft** (open-interval hold,
   dead-metronome callback). Promote the **owned-MIDI minor arp to PRIMARY**:
   author the open-interval harmony, let Suno carry only the flat vocal —
   anti-heal guaranteed, not gambled.
