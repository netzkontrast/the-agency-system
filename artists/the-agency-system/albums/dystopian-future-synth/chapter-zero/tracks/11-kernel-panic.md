## Concept

Track 11 is the **implosion** — the void after Track 10's peak (`kollaps`, S3
KERNEL PANIC, KOH **0.18**, the album's floor; the back-half contour T9 airless →
T10 peak → **T11 implode** completes). The internal resonance, classified as
attack, and the paradoxical defenses create a **fatal feedback loop**: every
correction amplifies the fault. The precise architecture dissolves from order to
chaos — channels collapse, data corrupts, cores fail. **Autopoiesis fails;
fourteen thousand eight hundred thirty-two warnings; self-organization failing.**

For the **fragment**, this is another level of horror: the structure that held
and defined it disintegrates — time stutters, the **walls of its own
consciousness cave in.** The cold algorithmic malfunctions become a **felt
dissolution**, and **the noise of the dying system and the inner scream fuse into
one unbearable chord.** What was built to prevent the end becomes the end — and
the Coherence Protocol is not *chosen* but becomes **inevitable** (the cold seed
for T12). Then the cacophony **crushes down to a pulse-less drone**, and the
proto-host's voice dissolves into the void.

**Craft (binding, §8):** **two generations comped** (user decision) — the chaotic
**peak/cacophony** (proto-host scream + system-failure layer fused) and the
**pulse-less collapse-drone** generated separately, the implosion comped between
them; **the collapse tail is an owned-stem (Layer A) crush.** **One Persona per
section** (proto-host vs the system — they FUSE only in the mix, never in one
generation). Pulse-less — the void, not a beat. The **proto-host dissolving into
the void is the last thing heard** (user decision). ~8 min, the durational floor.

## Mood & Imagery

A precise architecture dissolving from order into chaos. A feedback loop where
every correction makes it worse. Time stuttering, sensory hallucination, the
walls of consciousness caving in. The cold malfunction becoming a felt thing —
the dying machine's noise and the inner scream fused into one unbearable chord.
Then the over-saturated drone clips, detunes, and crushes down into a pulse-less
void. Flame Orange `#FF4500` and Corrupted Yellow `#8B8B00` over-saturating, then
guttering out. The floor of the album. No pulse. No groove. The void after the
peak.
