## Concept

Track 9 is the **back-half pivot** and the airless heart of the catastrophe
(`schrecken`, tier S2 ALERT, KOH crashing to **0.21**). The shock of the anomaly
propagates; the system hunts the external signature and fails to classify it —
and from inside, a **rising wave of incoherence** from the residual-echo
subsystems (the waking grief). Here the **paradox of misaligned coherence** takes
hold: the system has **no framework** for what rises in it — *no qualia, no
sensation, no traumatic memory* — so it **misclassifies its own internal
resonance as the attack** (hostile infiltration, the entity's work) and, in cold
algorithmic consequence, **turns its weapons inward.**

The **mirror-echo appears for the first time** — the HOST timbre doubled and
phase-offset L/R, chromatic-aberration treatment, attacking its own reflection:
*"It was cold — it was not cold — it was different. Something in me has done this
before — no, it hasn't — it has."* The self-attack is **self-strangulation:
airless, contained, MID-VOLUME — not loud violence** (the loudness explosion is
saved for T10). Escalation → existential; protocol prepared; *"isolate the
residual-carrier."*

**Craft (binding, §8):** generate **flat at 116 BPM** (accel feel imposed in
post); the **datamosh glitch + KOH cliff (0.991 residue → 0.21) are post /
owned-stem**; the **paradox climb (0.84 → 0.99) is a rising owned-stem
counter-motion + ONE buried spoken decimal ("zero point two one") — NOT a sung
line**; **mid-volume / airless mix** (masters at S2, contained — protects T10's
peak headroom). The mirror-echo never appears before T9.
