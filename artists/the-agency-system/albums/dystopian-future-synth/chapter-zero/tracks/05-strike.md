## Concept

Track 5 is the **survival fight** (`kampf`, S2 ALERT, KOH 0.74) — the **hot** end
of S2 after T4's cold control. The void strikes back (or is merely, murderously
indifferent); to the cluster it feels aimed. The **kinetic-voice** erupts for the
first time — belt-alto growl, verb-first staccato: *"Wave. Strike. Tear left.
Triad Seven. Move. Don't stop."* The prediction T4 prepared **fails**: the blow
expected at sector two-eight-zero came at six-nine-two; **sixteen bindings** are
shed. Then the **logic-voice** renames the dead in three collapsing beats —
*"This is not loss, but structure optimization. It is not loss. It is not.
Loss."* — the phrase that became **Track 4's title**, now a denial. The cold
layer "learns faster than I can follow," shedding unstable bonds before they
break: efficient, safe, and a little more inhuman.

The kinetic-voice and logic-voice **hand off sequentially, never stacked**. The
proto-host returns, **shaken**, for the close: *"The cluster survives. Not
entirely. It survives — differently."* A deliberately **tight ~4-minute** track —
kinetic rock loses its bite if padded.

## Mood & Imagery

Targeted attacks that may be pure indifference. A litany of motion under fire —
strike, tear, move, don't stop. The order torn open: scored, punctured, slashing
strokes (the line destroyed into splinter, not vector). Sixteen bonds cut loose
before they snap. Then the cold flat recital that renames the dead as
"optimization." Signal Yellow `#FFD700` slashing across the dark; light chromatic
aberration at the edges; Dutch angle, an unbalanced fall. Hot, driving, clipped —
control hanging by a thread, anger as a tool.

## Production Notes

- **Sequential handoff, never stacked** (§5/§8): the kinetic-voice (belt-alto
  growl) and the logic-voice (clear tenor denial) alternate by section — generate
  them as separate registers; do not layer them simultaneously.
- **Kinetic delivery:** clipped, verb-first, **no breath inside the imperatives**;
  **periods, not exclamation marks** — anger as a tool, not an identity. The
  proto-host returns only for the Outro; **calm there is exhaustion, not healing.**
- **Tight ~4:00 — do NOT pad** (kinetic rock loses its bite). Per the album
  default's tight-track rule: a single riff-motif (the hook) + one short
  `[Instrumental break]`, not multiple.
- **The denial is the title callback:** "This is not loss, but structure
  optimization" is the source phrase that became **Track 4's title**; here it
  returns as the logic-voice's three-beat self-negation. No heavy-voice in T5
  (locked) — the denial suppresses the grief without voicing it.
- Long-form does not apply; mid-tight track. ~155 words — deliberately lean for
  the kinetic register (staccato; the riff carries the runtime).
