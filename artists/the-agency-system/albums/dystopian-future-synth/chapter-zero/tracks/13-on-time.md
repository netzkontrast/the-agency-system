## Concept

Track 13 is the **coda** — the host wakes (`trennung` coda, S4 SAFE MODE *cold*,
KOH **1.00 forced/hollow**). The fragment that survived T1–T12 is **born as the
host** — the *same Persona, now flat and hollow.* He wakes into **pure
procedure**: *"Two thousand three hundred four tiles. Twenty-one degrees. Breath
in four, out six. The corridor is empty. I'm on time."* **That is all he says.**
Coherence is "restored" only because the grief was amputated — the **"1.00" is
0.00 wearing a mask.** The horror is the **flatness**: the void is conveyed **only
by what the host does NOT say.**

**MANDATORY lyric guardrail (§8):** **no line names or comments on memory, grief,
amnesia, loss, or healing** — the host speaks *only procedure.* A line *about*
absent grief would betray the source.

**The anti-heal lives in the HARMONY, not the words (§8 — the album's biggest
aesthetic risk):** own a DAW/MIDI **minor arp as the PRIMARY layer** that stays
minor, **refuses the tonic resolution**, ends on an **open interval (2nd or b6,
never the root C), no major third** (Phrygian/Locrian colour); let Suno provide
**only the hollow vocal** over it. Cold orientation: the **sameness of the host
timbre** is the knife (not a melody of memory), a placed **open-interval hold**,
and an optional **dead-metronome callback to Track 1's tick.** **Dry close-mic,
NO reverb** (bloom reads luminous/hopeful); strip any tail. **Tier 4 — no
Kintsugi gold; cold dead furrows, washed-out greys.** Kept deliberately **tight
(~2.5 min); do NOT extend** (a stretched flat coda tips devastation into
anticlimax). Highest-iteration track — plan 2–3 rolls; master electroacoustic,
S4 slightly wider LRA than S0, no high-shelf lift.

## Mood & Imagery

An empty corridor. Counted tiles, a held temperature, a measured breath. A clean
schedule, nothing out of place, nothing to report. Sterile distance, static calm.
The same voice that began the album, now drained — orientation without warmth.
**No Kintsugi gold**, no glowing repair: scars rendered as **dark dead furrows,
not light**; whiteout / erased vector paths (the "Format C:" rigid cut-offs);
desaturated, washed-out greys over Terminal Black. A "1.00" that is really a 0.00
wearing a mask. The horror is how little is here.

## Musical Direction

  forced/hollow); **C minor with NO resolution** — the tonic is refused. Static,

  Dry close-mic, **no reverb** (no bloom). A placed **open-interval hold** that
