## Concept

Track 8 is **the hush before the cliff** (`perturbation`, S2 ALERT, KOH **0.991**)
— the second half of the false-calm pair. An **unclassifiable signature** arrives:
no object, an **emergence** from the sea of potential, an **ontological anomaly**
that violates the reality model — *"information density: one point zero and zero
point zero at once,"* paradox **0.67**. The system reads it **cold**: full alert,
but the meter barely moves (**−0.007**) — *the dread rides the creeping
arrangement, not the meter.*

The **anomaly is texture only, never a voice** — and the **anomaly-texture is at
its strongest here**: rendered literally as a tone that is a pure sine **and**
white noise at once (the 1.0 / 0.0 paradox), an ontological-pressure shimmer that
bends the noise of Nothing around it. The **watch stays composed** — *"There is
no class for this. No operator."* — which is exactly the narrator's (T7)
foreshadowing arriving: the truth that must be **felt**, met by a system with
**no feeling in its repertoire.** The boundary of intake is exceeded; full alert,
the spring loaded.

**Craft (binding):** the anomaly is **external instrumental texture** (the watch's
voice stays pristine-cold — preserves T9's self-attack payoff); **one lean
status burst** then prose (§8 telemetry rationing). Tight **~4.5 min** — the hush
loads the spring; **do NOT pad** the false-calm.
