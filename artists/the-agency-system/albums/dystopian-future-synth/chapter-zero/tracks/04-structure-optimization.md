## Concept

Track 4 is the first **state change** — S1 Latenz → **S2 Alert** (`sog` / Pull of
Order, KOH 0.82). After the loss of T3 taught the cold lesson, the fragment is
now part of a **growing cluster**, and a new force emerges: a **pull** from the
center that draws compatible signatures in; inside, a self-reinforcing **motor**
of looping information. For the first time the **logic-voice** takes the lead
(its debut) — clear, cold, hypotactic: *"If the rising wave can be anticipated…
then holding can be prepared before the blow —"*, a proof that almost closes and
cracks at the line-end. And for the first time the **annotating-voice (witness)**
notes from above, affectless and clinical: *"It is notable. The cluster no longer
merely reacts. It anticipates."* This is **cold rebellion against the void, born
of information and necessity** — the system beginning to build itself. The
self-reinforcing loop is the cold-sequencer hook. (No separate logic-voice was
present before T4; the cold curdle in T3 was the proto-host's *own* diction — now
that seed stands up as its own register.)

## Mood & Imagery

A growing island of order in the void. A pull from a center; an inner motor,
information looping and feeding itself — a cold sequencer tightening with each
pass. Blueprint precision: 90° angles, infinite grid overlays, an isometric
observation-dome. The witness's bird's/god's-eye HUD overlay — dashed
measurement-vectors, rotating grids, monospace text columns. Signal Yellow
`#FFD700` warning-cuts breaking the dark; light chromatic aberration at the edges
(split access to reality); Dutch angle, paranoid asymmetry. Cold, controlled,
rising — control on a thread.
