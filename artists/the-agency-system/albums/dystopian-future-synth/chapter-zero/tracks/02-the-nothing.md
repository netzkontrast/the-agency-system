## Concept

Track 2 is the **heart of the void** (`herz`, KOH 0.91, state **S1 · Latenz**).
Where Track 1 was the first listening, T2 is pure durational existence: the
fragment hangs in an **active Nothing** that does not merely surround it but
*negates* it — a subsonic pressure that wants anything not perfectly coherent
gone. There is no up, no down, no time; one stretched moment, or years in a
flicker. The fragment knows only that it has a fragile inner structure — a thin
echo of something maybe before — and that its single greatest danger is to *go
out*: to let the threads slacken and fall back into meaninglessness.

Mid-drift the pure **child-fear** surfaces once — *"It is cold. I am small.
Where is — nothing."* — the album's **small-voice anchor**, planted here to
return full-voiced in the Track 10 round→stack. Isolation is the ground state;
pressing quiet broken by bursts of meaningless noise is the only company. The
faint **heavy-voice seed** from Track 1 — *"(it is pointless)"* — still murmurs
under the bed, barely audible, on its slow climb toward Track 10.

## Mood & Imagery

Spacelessness and timelessness. Honey gone hard; a plaything at the edge of an
abyss; threads going slack. Frostbitten cold, a milky dark-gray void with
contrast bleeding out (Latency Violet `#3B3355` fading in). Granular stutter;
opacity-fade reverb tails that dissolve each phrase before the next arrives. The
child motif squeezed into the lowest corner, crushed by pressing black. Glacial,
low-contrast, barely-there.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: 58–64 BPM (glacial, sub-pulse)
- **Feel**: dark ambient; durational ground state, no groove drive; granular
  stutter, opacity-fade reverb tails, contours dropping in and out;
  low-contrast loudness (S1 latency)
- **Instrumentation**: continuous sub-only drone on C (album spine), granular
  textures, sparse processed pad; no kit, no bass groove; the owned KOH stem
  (Layer A) laid continuously underneath

## Production Notes

- **Through-composed drift, not verse/chorus** — the bracketed movement tags are
  Suno scaffolding only; there is no pop hook. The child refrain *"It is cold. I
  am small."* is the single returning anchor (the plant for the Track 10
  round→stack payoff).
- **Voice spine:** the **fragile genderless head-voice held from Track 1** — one
  Persona, snapshot/reuse. This is the album's single point of failure for the
  T13 "same voice, now hollow" and T10 mirror-echo payoffs; do not let timbre
  drift between comped sections.
- **The small-voice break** is a distinct, **younger, lo-fi whispered child
  register** — generate as its own short clip and butt-join/comp in mix; keep
  clearly apart from the host head-voice (younger, whispered/fragmented, tape
  hiss vs. the host's adult sustained, faintly synthetic edge).
- **Heavy-voice undertow:** faint `(it is pointless)` parenthesized backing at
  **exactly T1's level** (V5 renders parentheses as a backing/ad-lib layer) —
  barely under the bed, never a second lead; the slow build toward Track 10.
- **Long-form (7:00) is comped** from multiple Suno passes over the continuous
  owned KOH stem (Layer A) — never one pass. **Lyric density does NOT scale with
  runtime** (193 words; the extra minutes are instrumental/atmospheric expanse).
