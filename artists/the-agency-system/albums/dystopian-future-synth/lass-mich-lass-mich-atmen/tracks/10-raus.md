## Concept

The escape-instinct track. The Fighter — who held the album's peak rage in T07 — now asks the system: *raus?* The question is sincere. After everything, is this the moment to leave the yellow room?

The Ich does **not** answer. The Ich is silent on this track. The withholding is the album's refusal of premature resolution — the third escape (heroic resolution / "I won") that the album was thematically committed to refusing.

Child-Freeze is in parenthesis — present-tense small whispers, "raus?" doubled in the child-register. She does not push for an answer either; she just echoes the question.

Post-punk × darkwave, propulsive but ambivalent, no resolution. Mid industrial-metal.

**Attacca transition out:** T10 → T11. The unanswered question tips into "Durch. Fühlen."

## Voice Lead

- **Fighter**: cracked tenor belt, hot but slightly cooled from T07's WARUM. Asks not for permission — asks for direction. "Raus? Sollen wir raus?"
- **(Child-Freeze)**: parenthetical whispered echo, same word doubled in head-voice. Child's question fitted inside the Fighter's.
- **(Ich — silent)**: structural silence. The Ich's no-answer is the album's refusal of heroic resolution.
