## Content warning ⚠

Track 07 sustains a simultaneous Fighter-belt + Child-Freeze-scream peak at 115 BPM full industrial-metal. Potentially re-traumatizing for both artist (during generation/listening) and listener (especially survivor-listeners).

**This track is marked mix-required.** Do not attempt to land it in one Suno generation; expect 4–6 generations + stem mixing. The simultaneous-belt-and-scream demand exceeds Suno's typical single-pass capability.

**User-decision A (panel-question round, 2026-05-20):** Keep the simultaneous Fighter-belt + Child-Freeze-scream peak; add CW. The album takes the trauma-authenticity risk consciously.

## Concept

The track of the WARUM-scream. The question that does not get answered — that does not need to be answered to be valid. Fighter belts the adult demand for an explanation; Child-Freeze, who was silent through T06's sleep, breaks into a sustained scream. Both at once. Same syllable. Different registers.

Military-style drums, distortion-heavy synths, full industrial-metal stack. 115 BPM — the album's fastest tempo. The schriller Alarm motif that pierced T01's intro returns here in the chorus — the threshold-marker the album opened with is now inside the question.

**Attacca transition out:** T07 → T08. The WARUM-Scream does not resolve into silence; it tips into the cold of T08's "Auch ich bin Eis".

## Voice Lead

- **Fighter**: cracked-tenor belt + controlled growl, full close-mic with throat distortion. Hot protective rage — the warrior's voice carrying the adult question.
- **Child-Freeze**: whispered head-voice escalating to sustained scream. ~10–12, breath-audible at start, fully voiced at the peak. **This is her only sustained voicing on the album** (she sleeps in T06, present-tense once in T13). The scream is her agency.

**Simultaneous peak:** the two voices land on the same WARUM at the same downbeat. Mix-engineer must keep both audible without smearing.
