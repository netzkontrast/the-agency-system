## Concept

The album's polyphonic peak. Integrator — the ageless mezzo-alto whose function is to hold all other voices in one room — leads. But the *answer* is not hers alone; the answer is *held* by all eight canonical alters simultaneously, with Albtraum present only in parenthesis (audible, contained, not silenced).

"Durch. Fühlen." — *Through. Feel.* The two-word answer that T10 withheld. Not "out". Not "free". Not "won". Through, and feel. The therapeutic position (DESIGN §7) made audible.

Dense polyphonic synth layers, chorus-rich, all voices simultaneous, orchestral string layers (echo of *Autoren-Feder*'s bridge). Low industrial-metal density — the peak is *vocal* density, not sonic-pressure density.

**Mix requirement:** the simultaneous-voicing demand exceeds Suno's typical single-pass capability. Expect 4–6 generations + stem mixing. Mix-engineer must keep all 8 voices distinguishable.

**Attacca transition in:** T10 → T11.

**Pre-release / lead-single strategy:** T11 + T02 released together as Doppel-Pre-Release before album drop.

## Voice Lead

- **Integrator**: ageless mezzo-alto. Voices the answer-words "durch", "fühlen" most clearly; the rest of the cast holds them harmonically.
- **All 8 canonical alters** (Ich / Child-Freeze / Fighter / Collapsed One / Ambivalent / Witness-of-Witnesses + Integrator + one more): simultaneous voicing on the answer. Each retains their fingerprint register; the polyphony is real, not mimed.
- **(Albtraum / Sexualized-Override)**: parenthetical only — audible at low mix-level, alien-EQ processed, contained but not silenced. The perpetrator-introject is held in the same room; that is the album's polyphonic claim.
