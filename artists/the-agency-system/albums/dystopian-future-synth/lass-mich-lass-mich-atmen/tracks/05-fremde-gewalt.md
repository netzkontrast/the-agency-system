## Concept

The third realization track and the longest of the three intertextual citation tracks (T03/T05/T09). The Ich names the violence as *fremd* — not-mine, foreign, alien — and Albtraum is given the most extended verbatim passage of the album, in alien-EQ processing that sonically marks it as *outside* the Ich's voice.

No drum kit. Foreign-sounding synth carries; breath-led structure. The Ich's lines are spare; the Albtraum's quotation is what fills the silence the Ich leaves on purpose.

This track is the album's clearest sonic argument for the perpetrator-introject-as-distinct-voice. The processing makes audible what is structural: the perpetrator's voice was always foreign material — alien to the survivor — even when it spoke in her own dreams.

## Voice Lead

- **Ich**: Sprechgesang baritone, dry close-mic, spare. "Das war fremd. Das war nicht ich. Das war Gewalt."
- **Albtraum / Sexualized-Override**: low contralto with strong alien-EQ processing, foreign-sounding reverb, controlled-performative cadence. Extended verbatim passage from *All Is Lost*. Sonically *outside* the Ich's voice — the EQ makes the foreignness audible.
