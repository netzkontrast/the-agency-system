## Concept

The track of the second realization: it was not my fault. The Ich does not declare this triumphally — the Ich works through the Schuld-Stimme's residue. Each verse the Schuld-Stimme appears with one of its old phrasings ("Ich hätte —", "wenn ich nur —", "warum habe ich nicht —"), and each refrain the Ich names the misattribution and re-locates the guilt to its actual address.

The Schuld-Stimme is *not* a canonical alter from the-eleven; it is a function-voice the album carries for this single track. By the end, the Schuld-Stimme has fragmented and gone silent. The Ich speaks the last line alone.

This is the album's most directly declamatory track. Post-punk bass line drives, cold synth lead carries, mid industrial-metal density. The voice does not soften.

## Voice Lead

- **Ich**: Sprechgesang baritone, dry close-mic, declamatory. Less paratactic than T01/T02; full declarative sentences here. "Das war nicht meine Schuld. Das war seine."
- **(Schuld-Stimme abblätternd)**: parenthetical female-leaning whisper, fragmentary, mid-sentence breaks. Same-gender-not-applicable. Falls silent before the closer.
