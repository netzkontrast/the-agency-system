## Concept

The third intertextual citation track. Collapsed One leads — the voice that carries the 2024 surrender lineage. He speaks in English throughout the track (per DESIGN §1: English at three precise moments where dissociation/foreign-voice/repertoire-direct-quotation demands it).

Albtraum (Sexualized-Override) quotes "All Is Lost" verbatim — including "set me free" — in [Albtraum]-bracketed sections. Collapsed One *answers*, not by surrendering, but by *staying*. The 2024 outro position is refused here, sustained-authorship-style: not by argument, by presence.

Doom-leaning, sub-bass dominant, dark techno underpulse. The track is dense in low end; the surrender voice has a body now.

## Voice Lead

- **Collapsed One**: deepest bass, near-spoken, EN. The 2024-surrender voice given full track-lead. He does not surrender here; he *carries* the surrender lineage and refuses its conclusion.
- **Albtraum / Sexualized-Override**: alien-EQ processed contralto, EN verbatim from *All Is Lost*. "Set me free" is the key citation; positioned to be heard but not honoured.
