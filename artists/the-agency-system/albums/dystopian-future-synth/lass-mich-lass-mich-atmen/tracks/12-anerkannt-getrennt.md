## Concept

The track of separation — *anerkannt* (acknowledged) and *getrennt* (separated). The Witness-of-Witnesses, an androgynous spoken-word voice with monotone delivery, speaks the document-sentences that name what happened, when it happened, and that it is over.

The three core sentences (DESIGN §7 / §11):

> "Das war Missbrauch. Es geschah. Es ist vorbei."

The Witness does not interpret. Does not console. Does not narrate the survivor's experience. Speaks the *recording-angel* function: the facts as facts, the past as past, the now as separate from the then.

**User-decision F (panel-question round, 2026-05-20):** Witness-of-Witnesses appears only in Track 12. The earlier "cross-album annotation" plan was dropped — one alter, one function, one track.
