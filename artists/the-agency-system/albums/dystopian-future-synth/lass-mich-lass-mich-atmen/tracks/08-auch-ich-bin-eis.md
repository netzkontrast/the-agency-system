## Concept

Attacca-arrival from the WARUM-scream (T07 → T08). The peak's heat is dissipated into glacial cold. The Heute-Ich acknowledges: I, too, am ice. Not the Child-Freeze (who froze in the past); the adult is *also* capable of freezing. The recognition is not collapse — it is honesty.

Collapsed One (deepest bass, near-spoken — the surrender-voice that carries the *All Is Lost* lineage) appears in parenthesis. He whispers the old surrender. The Ich hears him, does not concede.

Cooling industrial, bass drainage, glacial drift. Mid industrial-metal density — the heat is fading but the structure remains.
