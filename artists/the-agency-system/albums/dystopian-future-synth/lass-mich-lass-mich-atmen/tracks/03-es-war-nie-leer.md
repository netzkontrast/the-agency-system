## Concept

The first of three intertextual citation tracks (T03/T05/T09). The Ich, holding the position, recognizes that what 2024's "All Is Lost" called the *yellow void* — Lost in a void where yellow particles dance around — was never empty. The yellow particles were *bodies*. The void was a populated room. The 2024 surrender voice was the perpetrator-introject's lure language.

Albtraum (carried by Sexualized-Override) speaks the "All Is Lost" phrases verbatim. Foreign-sounding synth, alien EQ on the introject voice, sleepwalking pulse. The Ich does not argue with the quotation; the Ich names what it actually was.

**Citation device:** "All Is Lost" phrases appear in [Albtraum]-bracketed sections. They are *foreign material* the Albtraum carries — not internal motif. See DESIGN §9.

## Voice Lead

- **Ich**: Sprechgesang baritone, dry close-mic. Present-tense recognition, not argument. "Es war nie leer."
- **Albtraum / Sexualized-Override**: low contralto with alien-EQ processing, foreign-sounding reverb, controlled-performative cadence. Speaks EN verbatim from *All Is Lost*: "Lost in a void where yellow particles dance around", "All is lost, in this endless yellow void". Sonically separated from Ich (different gender register + alien processing).
