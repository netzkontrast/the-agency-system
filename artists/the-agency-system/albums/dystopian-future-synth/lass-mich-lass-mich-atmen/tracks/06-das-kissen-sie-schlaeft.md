## Concept

The album's quietest moment by intent. The pillow that pressed on the mouth in T02 — the same yellowed pillow — is here a sanctuary. The Child-Freeze sleeps in it. Nobody holds her hand. She is *allowed to rest* without supervision, without rescue.

Ambivalent leads — the microtonal alto whose function is to hold approach and withdrawal simultaneously. She does *not* address the weapon-mode of the pillow. The track is not about confrontation; it is about the doubled object's sanctuary-side being legitimately accessible. Witch-house × darkwave texture, slow oscillating microtonal pad, no drums to speak of.

**User-decision C (panel-question round, 2026-05-20):** Track 06 becomes Child's sleep moment — she sleeps in the pillow (sanctuary-mode) without hand-holding.

## Voice Lead

- **Ambivalent**: microtonal alto, oscillating — vowels held longer than expected, pitch sliding microtonally. The voice that can hold sanctuary and weapon in the same room without flinching from either, but speaks here only on the sanctuary side.
- **(Child-Freeze sleeping)**: NO active voice in T06. Her presence is sonic only — breath at the edge of audibility, slow-pulse heartbeat from the pillow's centre. She is *there*, asleep, agentially present.
