## Concept

The title-track Demand. Lass mich, lass mich atmen — the album's centroid statement, the namens-tragende moment. The pillow is on the mouth in the dream; the Ich speaks the demand from inside the dream-state but from the present tense. Doubled refrain: Ich speaks, Child-Freeze echoes — both in the same syntactic space but in different registers.

This is the album's first **full industrial-metal stack**: downtuned distorted bass, concrete-room drums, detuned guitar feedback stabs, metal-clang hits, pulverizing repetition. 105 BPM. The demand is not pleaded — it is set.

The track sits at the album's namens-Position. T01's threshold-arrival tips directly here via attacca; the demand is the first sustained statement.

**Pre-release / lead-single strategy:** T02 + T11 released together as Doppel-Pre-Release before album drop.

## Voice Lead

- **Ich**: Sprechgesang baritone, full close-mic with throat distortion, declarative. Verbal signature: short imperative clauses, no apology in the demand. "Lass mich. Lass mich atmen."
- **(Child-Freeze) — doubled refrain**: whispered head-voice doubling on the "Lass mich" phrase, slightly behind the Ich's downbeat. Same words, different register — the Child says the same thing the adult is finally saying. Parenthesis-discipline: same-gender-not-applicable (different gender + age register); doubling is the relation, not exclusion.

## Voice Notes

### Ich (Sprechgesang baritone — Album-Persona-Anchor)

- **Verbal signature**: Imperative-set, paratactic, present-tense. Short clauses stacked. "Der Puls rast." "Mein Körper trägt zwei Zeiten." No apology in the demand.
- **Syntactic shape**: Paratactic — never hypotactic. Multiple full stops per line are the rule, not the exception.
- **Punctuation habit**: Periods, not em-dashes. Streaming uses commas where a literary draft would use em-dashes (override rule).
- **Sentence-completion behavior**: Commits and closes. The demand is set, not suspended.
- **Imagery anchors**: Puls / Schlag / Tür / Körper / Faden / Mund / Tuch / Hand / Luft / Raum — concrete somatic and object-world vocabulary, no abstract emotion-naming.
- **Mic / FX**: dry close-mic, throat-audible. Lineage from *Autoren-Feder*'s "male vocal: Sprechgesang technique, slight throat distortion."

### Child-Freeze (whispered head-voice — doubled refrain only)

- **Verbal signature**: Doubles the Ich's refrain verbatim. No independent content in T02. Same words, slightly behind the Ich's downbeat.
- **Syntactic shape**: Inherits Ich's paratactic shape because she echoes the Ich's refrain only.
- **Punctuation habit**: Mirrors Ich's punctuation; parenthesized in Suno lyrics to mark backing-vocal layer per voice-craft-principles.md.
- **Mic / FX**: whispered head-voice, ~10–12 years register, breath-audible. Behind/under the Ich, not foregrounded. Suno V5 parens treatment = backing layer.
- **Restraint**: Child is NOT lead in T02. Her one sustained voicing is reserved for T07 (scream peak). Here she is *present* via doubling, signalling that the demand is the system's demand, not just the adult's.

### Doubling-discipline note

Ich + Child-Freeze are different gender + age registers — parenthesis-discipline does not strictly require parens for register separation, but T02 uses parens to mark the Child's role as **backing-doubling**, not co-lead. This sets the album's parens-as-doubling convention; T03/T05/T09's Albtraum-parens will function differently (intertextual citation, not doubling).
