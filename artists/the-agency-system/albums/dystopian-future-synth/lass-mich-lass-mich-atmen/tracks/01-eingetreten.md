## Concept

The album opens where *Moment der Klarheit*'s "Vor der Konfrontation" left off — at the word "Angekommen.". Track 01 is the first breath after that arrival. The Ich is already in the yellow room. The Child-Freeze is already in the corner. The pillow is already there. Nothing is introduced; everything is met.

The track inherits the somatic peak from the prior album. It does not build to it — it carries it. The heartbeat motif (60 BPM, from *Autoren-Feder*) opens the album, and a *schriller Alarm* threshold-marker pierces the intro. Tempo ramps from 60 to 90 BPM as the album-body wakes.

**Attacca transition:** T01 → T02. The intro pulse does not resolve; it tips directly into the title-track demand.

## Voice Lead

- **Ich**: Sprechgesang baritone, dry close-mic, throat-audible. Lineage from *Autoren-Feder*. Present-tense, paratactic. Carries the album's anchor register.
- **(Child-Freeze)**: parenthetical echo only — whispered head-voice fragments, ~10–12, breath-audible. Never the lead in T01. She is *there* but not yet *speaking*.

## Voice Notes

### Ich (Sprechgesang baritone — Album-Persona, established T02, consistent here)

- **Verbal signature**: Arrival-statement, paratactic, present-tense. Almost-knowing register ("Etwas tickt", "Ich war schon hier") — recognition without full naming.
- **Syntactic shape**: Paratactic — short clauses, multiple full stops per line.
- **Punctuation habit**: Periods. Streaming uses commas where literary draft would use em-dashes (override rule).
- **Imagery anchors**: Tür / Streifen / Raum / Luft / Puls / Wand / Alarm / Boden / Gelb / Atem / Maschine / Faden — concrete object-world + somatic.
- **Mic / FX**: dry close-mic, throat-audible. Lineage from *Autoren-Feder*.

### Child-Freeze (whispered head-voice — parenthetical echo only)

- **Restraint**: NOT lead in T01. Present only as parenthesized echo of the Ich's hook word ("Eingetreten") and one tempo-cue ("schneller"). She is *there* but not yet *speaking* — her sustained voicing is reserved for T07.
- **Mic / FX**: whispered head-voice, ~10–12 register, breath-audible, behind/under the Ich. Suno V5 parens = backing layer.
