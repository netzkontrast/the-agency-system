## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:01.7 to 0:08.** The first sung line, "Der Puls ist langsam. Der Puls ist meiner.", appears only at about 0:06 to 0:07, in tiny type at the bottom edge, after the board. The heartbeat starts at 2.35 s under a still board, so the hook is a wait. Show the room under the warning or shorten the board.
2. **0:34 to 0:36 and 0:50 to 0:53, with a repeat at 1:06 to 1:08 and 1:48.** Type runs through props. "Ich atme ein / aus" sits behind the cushion, "Kein Sieg. Kein Ende." is struck through by the cushion (strip_47-50), a curtain crosses "Jetzt frag ich nicht mehr" (sheet part 2), and "hier. Das war." runs across the curtain (full_t108.2). Move the type to clean wall space.
3. **1:48 to 2:05.** The loudest instrumental stretch is a single static room with a slow drift and a repeating "Ich bin hier. Das war." Nothing new happens for about 17 s (sheet part 3 and part 4). It needs an event, such as a new object, a light change or a cut.

- The "Kein Faden" underline and the thread vanishing at about 30 s look like an unexplained glitch.
