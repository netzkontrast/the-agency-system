## Content warning

Album re-confronts childhood sexual abuse at album length. Specific risk-points:

- **Track 07 ("WARUM")** sustains a simultaneous Fighter-belt + Child-Freeze-scream peak at 115 BPM full industrial-metal. Potentially re-traumatizing for both artist (during generation/listening) and listener (especially survivor-listeners). Marked as mix-required track.
- **Tracks 03/05/09** give an introjected perpetrator-voice (carried by Sexualized-Override) verbatim quotation rights to phrases from the artist's prior album *All Is Lost* (Together We Confide, 2024). The 2024 surrender voice is structurally identified as the perpetrator-introject's lure language.

Listener discretion essential. Recommended for survivor listeners only with grounding resources at hand. Track 13 ("Ich bin hier / Das war") lands the album in present-tense self + past-tense truth without triumph, closure, or cure-narrative.

## Concept

The Agency System has built a deliberate three-album arc:

| # | Album | Function in the arc |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Together We Confide* (Apr 2024) | **Witness** — surrender, the "perfect world" facade, the yellow void perceived as loss |
| 2 | *Moment der Klarheit* | **Descent & Confrontation** — bunker, finding the child, naming the abuse |
| 3 | *Systematic Agency* | **Authorship** — taking the pen, narrative agency, the writer's hand still shaking |
| 4 | **Lass mich, lass mich atmen** *(THIS)* | **Sustained Polyphonic Authorship — as form, not as new event** |

Album 4 is the *form* that *Kern der Wahrheit* takes when expanded into 13 polyphonic movements — sustained authorship of the truth-confrontation rather than a new event in the artist's biography. It demonstrates that authorship can hold the polyphonic truth without collapsing into one position — without surrender, without escape, without heroic resolution.

The album begins where *Vor der Konfrontation* ended ("Angekommen.") and inhabits the same moment that *Kern der Wahrheit* compressed into one song — but holds it open across thirteen voices.

## Structure

13-movement polyphonic suite. Cross-track motif inventory + heartbeat bookend (callback to *Autoren-Feder*'s 60-BPM intro/outro) connect the tracks. Not committed to attacca transitions or tonal centre — suite, not single-piece. See DESIGN.md §6.1.1 for the cross-track DNA-layer density grid (13×5).

## Themes

- **Sustained authorship of truth** as proof of *Aus Ohnmacht wird Gestaltung* (the position *Autoren-Feder* earned)
- **Refusal of three escapes**: surrender (the "All Is Lost" outro), flight (the "raus" instinct), and heroic resolution (any "I won" narrative)
- **Polyphonic stay**: voices distinct but together, alters speaking inside one sustained moment
- **Yellow as historicized object-color**: not abstract — the yellowed childhood pillow, the yellow flat room, the gelbe Lärm of the dream-Tod
- **Therapeutic position**: durch + fühlen + anerkannt + getrennt + dableiben (not heal, not escape, not surrender — stay)

## Sonic Palette

- **Beats**: heartbeat motif (60 BPM bookend, callback to *Autoren-Feder*) opens T01 and closes T13; per-track BPM 60–115 with industrial-metal density varying from "full" (T02, T07) to "none" (T13)
- **Samples**: yellow-noise / HF-glitch as flickering high-frequency layer across all tracks; [breath]-markers as percussive/structural elements
- **Vocals**: Sprechgesang baritone (Ich); whispered head-voice (Child-Freeze); cracked tenor + growl (Fighter); deepest bass near-spoken (Collapsed One); microtonal alto (Ambivalent); mezzo-alto ageless (Integrator); androgynous spoken-word (Witness); low contralto alien-EQ processed (Sexualized-Override / Albtraum)
- **Mood**: forward-projected dystopia; claustrophobic survivor narrative; trauma-honest polyphonic stay; refuses heroic resolution
