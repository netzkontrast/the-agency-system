## Concept

A pre-dawn apartment. Four chairs at one kitchen table. Keys on the counter. A folded note in handwriting that's almost yours. A coat over a chair you didn't take off last night. Nothing dramatic — the small evidence the apartment was lived in by four people who all share the same body.

The track moves through:

1. **Disorientation** — a voice that can't account for the night (M1, Searching).
2. **Offered permission** — a voice that asks the door to stop being held shut (F1, Healer).
3. **The explanation for the locked doors** — a voice that closed them on purpose, that says why (F2, Gatekeeper).
4. **The post-fight reflection** — a voice that fought against the others and is starting to recognise the shared room (M2, Warrior).
5. **Reconciliation duet** — Searching and Healer trade lines, four lines, no overlap.
6. **The four-voice room-sharing agreement** — the bridge, one line per voice, strict gender-alternation.
7. **The calm exit into the day** — the Gatekeeper's outro, liberating without triumph.

**Less blumig than the source.** The source lyric (preserved in the track file's *Source Material* section) leans on abstract poetry — *Lichtschein, Echos, Gedankenfluten, Stimmen die das Herz betören*. The rewrite trades those for concrete objects (Schlüssel, Spiegel, Zettel, Tinte, Tür, Schritte, Hemd, Auto) and somatic actions. Future Rap delivery carries the production weight so the words can stay close to objects.

**Hook.** *Ich und ich* — repeated verbatim across all four choruses. Grammatically wrong in German (*ich und du* / *wir* would be conventional) — the wrongness is the point. The system addresses itself in second person while remaining first.

## Voice Architecture

Four distinct voices. Strict gender-alternation between adjacent solo sections. Four clearly distinguishable Stimmfarben — register separation, mic distance, processing chain, and delivery register all differ between any two voices.

| # | Name | Gender | Register / Color | Delivery | Mic / FX |
|---|------|--------|------------------|----------|----------|
| **M1** | **Searching** *(Verlorener)* | male | mid-baritone, conversational | half-rapped, weary, behind the beat | dry close-mic, light tape compression |
| **F1** | **Healer** *(Heilerin)* | female | warm soprano, vowel-forward | melodic singing, breathy at edges | intimate close-mic, soft room reverb |
| **M2** | **Warrior** *(Krieger)* | male | low bass-baritone, chest-weighted | controlled forward, assertive without aggression | dry mid-distance mic, slight saturation on lows |
| **F2** | **Gatekeeper** *(Wächterin)* | female | deep mezzo-alto, ageless | declarative melodic spoken, slow legato | plate reverb wide, architectural space |

**No labels in lyrics.** Identity is carried by syntax, vocabulary, pause, and timbre — never by `[Voice Name]` tags or name-adlibs. Per `../../../../../overrides/voice-craft-principles.md`.

See `tracks/01-ich-und-ich.md` §Voice Architecture for per-voice verbal fingerprints and section assignments.

## Themes

- Inner plurality without pathology
- Renegotiation of guardianship — who holds which key
- Pre-dawn quiet as an emotional register (not noir, not bright)
- German interior life rendered through concrete objects
- The hopeful side of a multi-voice inner system — without forced fusion

## Sonic Palette

- **Genre**: dark / introspective Future Rap (working name). Distributor genre = cloud-rap. German scene reference register: Schmyt / Edo Saiya / Pashanim adjacent — voice-forward, atmospheric, sub-heavy, no festival energy, no autotune-as-effect.
- **Tempo**: ~80 BPM half-time feel (cloud-rap canon — feels 40 BPM melodic with double-time hats at 160 BPM in chorus).
- **Beats**: deep 808 sub-bass with slides, distorted/saturated 808 booms on downbeats, half-time trap drums (snare on the 3, kick on 1), hi-hat patterns sparse in verses with triplet rolls in choruses.
- **Atmosphere**: detuned warm pad (slow attack, slow release) foregrounded in pre-chorus 3 / bridge / outro where drums pull back, occasional muted piano pluck or guitar pluck loop.
- **Vocals**: four distinct delivery registers (see voice table above). Verses and pre-choruses are solo voices. Choruses stack all four voices in harmony on the hook only. Bridge gives each voice one line in sequence, each in its own stereo position.
- **Mood**: pre-dawn stillness, soft hopefulness without warmth, the apartment finally inhabited honestly. Mineral palette, restrained, not noir.
