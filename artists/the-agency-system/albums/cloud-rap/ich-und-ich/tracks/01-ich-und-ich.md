## Concept

A standalone single. Four inner voices share one body and, over the course of one track, stop guarding doors against each other. The source lyric (see *Source Material*) is the architectural sketch; this version strips the imagery down — fewer metaphors, more concrete objects (Schlüssel, Spiegel, Tisch, Tür, Zettel, Schritte), less abstract vocabulary (no *Lichtschein*, *Echos*, *betören*, *Gedankenfluten*). Future Rap / dark cloud-rap delivery, half-time 808s, atmospheric pad — the genre carries the production weight so the words can stay close to objects.

**Hook.** *Ich und ich* — repeated verbatim across all four choruses. Grammatically wrong in German (the conventional form is *ich und du* or *wir*) — the wrongness is the point. The system addresses itself in second person while remaining first.

## Voice Architecture

**Four distinct voices, strict gender-alternation between adjacent solo sections, and four clearly distinguishable Stimmfarben.** No alter is named in the lyric — identity carried by syntax, vocabulary, pause, and timbre per `../../../../../overrides/voice-craft-principles.md`.

### Voice DNA

| Slot | Name | Gender | Register / Color | Delivery | Mic / FX |
|------|------|--------|------------------|----------|----------|
| **M1** | **Searching** *(Verlorener)* | male | mid-baritone, ~40s, conversational range | half-rapped / half-spoken, behind the beat, slightly under-articulated, weary | dry close-mic, light tape compression, no reverb |
| **F1** | **Healer** *(Heilerin)* | female | warm soprano, ~30s, vowel-forward legato | melodic singing, breathy at the edges, lullaby-adjacent phrasing | intimate close-mic, soft room reverb |
| **M2** | **Warrior** *(Krieger)* | male | low bass-baritone, ~30–40s, chest-weighted | controlled, forward, assertive without aggression, no vibrato | dry mid-distance mic, slight saturation on lows |
| **F2** | **Gatekeeper** *(Wächterin)* | female | deep mezzo-alto, ageless quality | melodic spoken, declarative full stops, slow legato, long vowels held | plate reverb suggesting architectural space |

**Why these four colors are clearly distinct:** male mid-baritone vs. male bass-baritone are separated by ~5 semitones of comfortable range, by mic distance (close vs. mid), and by delivery (weary half-rap vs. controlled forward). Female warm soprano vs. deep mezzo-alto are separated by ~10 semitones, by phrasing (breathy melodic vs. declarative spoken), and by space (intimate room vs. plate reverb hall). No two voices share register *or* delivery *or* processing.

### Section assignments (gender-alternation rule)

When two solo sections sit back-to-back, the genders alternate. The chorus / pre-chorus-3 / V3 duet are mixed sections and serve as buffers between same-gender solos (PC1 → V2).

| Section | Voice | Gender flow check |
|---------|-------|-------------------|
| Intro (spoken) | **M1** Searching | — |
| Verse 1 | **M1** Searching | (continuous from intro) |
| Pre-Chorus 1 | **F1** Healer | M1 → F1 ✓ |
| Chorus 1 | all four (mixed stack) | buffer |
| Verse 2 | **F2** Gatekeeper | mixed → F2 (different color from F1) |
| Pre-Chorus 2 | **M2** Warrior (reflective register) | F2 → M2 ✓ |
| Chorus 2 | all four (mixed stack) | buffer |
| Verse 3 (duet) | **M1** Searching + **F1** Healer alternating line-by-line | M→F→M→F internally ✓ |
| Pre-Chorus 3 | all four voices, soft unison | buffer |
| Bridge L1 | **F1** Healer | — |
| Bridge L2 | **M2** Warrior | F1 → M2 ✓ |
| Bridge L3 | **F2** Gatekeeper | M2 → F2 ✓ |
| Bridge L4 | **M1** Searching | F2 → M1 ✓ |
| Chorus 3 (climax) | all four (mixed stack) | buffer |
| Outro | **F2** Gatekeeper *(liberating, calm)* | M1 → F2 ✓ |

**Bridge is the strict-alternation showcase.** F1 → M2 → F2 → M1 — four voices in sequence, gender alternates every line, voice color changes every line (warm soprano → bass-baritone → deep mezzo → mid-baritone). The bridge is where the user hears the four-voice apartment most clearly.

### Per-voice verbal fingerprints (to be applied at lyric-writing)

- **M1 Searching** — questions framed as observations, sentences that trail into em-dashes, "I don't know" syntax, "Wer hat …?" stacks. Imagery: Spiegel, Tinte, Zettel, Auto, Hemd — small evidence of a night he can't account for.
- **F1 Healer** — permissions cast as facts ("Du musst nicht —", "Hör auf —"), vowel-forward, never imperative. Imagery: offered hand, opened palm, the kitchen light left on, soup.
- **M2 Warrior** — clipped declaratives, three-word lines, no breath inside imperatives, possessive escalation. Reflective in PC2: longer line, controlled — same fingerprint, slower cadence. Imagery: fist unclenched, weight on the back foot, post-fight stillness.
- **F2 Gatekeeper** — declarative full stops, "Türen offen.", first-person plural without strain, "Wir gehen rein." Imagery: doors, keys, locks, the hallway, the threshold.

## Mood & Imagery

A pre-dawn apartment. Four chairs at a kitchen table. Keys on the counter, a folded Post-It in handwriting that's almost yours, a coat over a chair you didn't take off last night. Nothing dramatic — the small evidence the apartment was lived in by four people who all share the same body. Slate-grey window light, the hum of a fridge, the distant traffic outside, four breaths in different rooms inside the same walls.

The track moves from the morning-after disorientation (M1) through the offered permission (F1), the determined explanation of why the doors were locked (F2), the post-fight reflection (M2), the reconciliation duet (M1+F1), the four-voice room-sharing agreement (bridge), and the calm exit into the day (F2 outro).

No fog, no shadow as mood-marker. The atmosphere is **soft pre-dawn of an apartment finally inhabited honestly** — mineral palette, restrained, not noir.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: ~80 BPM half-time feel (cloud-rap canon — feels 40 BPM melodic with double-time hats at 160 BPM in choruses)
- **Genre**: dark / introspective Future Rap (working name), distributor genre = cloud-rap. Voice-forward, atmospheric, sub-heavy. No festival energy, no autotune-as-effect.
- **Instrumentation**: deep 808 sub-bass with slides, distorted/saturated 808 booms on downbeats, half-time trap drums (snare on the 3, kick on 1), hi-hat patterns sparse in verses with triplet rolls in choruses, atmospheric detuned warm pad (slow attack, slow release), occasional muted piano pluck or guitar pluck loop, vocal stacks on the chorus hook only.
- **Mix architecture (for the four distinct rooms)**:
  - M1 (Searching) — centre-front, dry, close
  - F1 (Healer) — centre-left, intimate room reverb
  - M2 (Warrior) — centre-right, mid-distance, light saturation
  - F2 (Gatekeeper) — centre-back depth, plate reverb wide
  - Bridge sequence: each line steps through its own stereo position so the listener registers four different rooms in the same apartment.
- **No autotune as default.** Subtle vocal compression and tape saturation only. Choruses stack all four voices in harmony on the hook lines.

## Source Material

1. Strip the abstract poetry (*Lichtschein, Echos, betören, Gedankenfluten, Lieder die mein Herz betör'n, Mauern niederfallen*) for concrete objects and somatic actions.
2. Rebuild the four voices as the **Searching / Healer / Warrior / Gatekeeper** quartet documented above — strict gender-alternation between adjacent solos, four clearly distinguishable Stimmfarben.
3. Translate the singer-songwriter cadence into Future Rap delivery — half-rapped verses, melodic-but-restrained hooks, sub-heavy chorus stacks, behind-the-beat phrasing.
4. Retain the hook "ich und ich" verbatim across all four choruses; let the rest of the chorus take one line of variation per pass.

## Production Notes
