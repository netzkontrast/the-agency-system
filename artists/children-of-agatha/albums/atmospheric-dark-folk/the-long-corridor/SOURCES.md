## Album Descriptions

### Short (No Sources)

```
Children of Agatha is a collective of patients at the St. Agatha Krankenhaus in Köln,
a clinic for psychosomatic medicine. The Long Corridor is their debut album — dark,
atmospheric, and elemental; music made to process trauma in the company of others.
The album's title comes from the fluorescent-lit hallway that connects all the rooms:
a space walked countless times, alone and together, that slowly becomes your own.
```
