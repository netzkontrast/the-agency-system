## Album Descriptions

### Short (No Sources)

```
Children of Agatha is a collective inspired by patients at the Cellitinnen-Marienborn
St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit in Köln-Niehl — Germany's top-ranked
specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychosomatic medicine. The Long Corridor is their
debut album: dark, atmospheric, and elemental; music made to process trauma in the
company of others.

The album takes its title from the fluorescent-lit hallway that connects all the rooms
of an inpatient ward — a space walked countless times, alone and together, that slowly
becomes your own. Music therapy has been practiced at St. Agatha for over 40 years,
officially described as "a protected space for encounter and play."

The album opens on that space.
```
