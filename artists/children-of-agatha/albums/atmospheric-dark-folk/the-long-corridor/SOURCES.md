## Album Descriptions

### Short (No Sources)

```
Children of Agatha takes its name from the Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha
Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit in Köln-Niehl — Germany's top-ranked specialist
clinic for psychiatry and psychosomatic medicine, where music therapy has been
practiced for over 40 years.

The clinic officially describes group music sessions as "ein geschützter Raum für
Begegnung und Spiel" — a protected space for encounter and play. The Long Corridor
opens on that space.

The album takes its title from the fluorescent-lit hallway that connects all the
rooms of an inpatient ward: a space walked countless times, alone and together, that
slowly becomes your own.
```

### Liner Notes (Full)

```
[To be completed — awaiting final track list and lyrics]

The St. Agatha clinic was founded by the Cellitinnen — a Catholic nursing order
following the Augustinian Rule — who arrived in Köln-Niehl in November 1894 to care
for factory workers' families. Their hospital opened on 5 February 1905: the feast
day of Saint Agatha of Sicily, their patron. She was a third-century martyr whose
body was destroyed so that she could not be broken — a figure of bodily suffering
and indestructible witness.

The hospital's psychosomatic medicine department opened in 1980, the first in Cologne.
Music therapy has been practiced there for over four decades. In January 2023 the
clinic completed its transformation into a specialist facility entirely dedicated to
mental health — psychiatry, psychosomatic medicine, psychotherapy. It now holds the
top ranking in North Rhine-Westphalia.

The Kulturkapelle — the clinic's former convent chapel, now a public art exhibition
space — stands open to the neighbourhood. The wall between patient world and outside
world is not sealed.

Children of Agatha is a fictional collective inspired by what that space makes possible.
```

## Legal Notes

- **Patient privacy**: No identifiable patient information is used; all patient voices are fictional composites inspired by the institutional context
- **Institution**: Named as setting and inspiration; no investigative claims about staff, outcomes, or incidents
- **Quoted language**: *"geschützter Raum für Begegnung und Spiel"* is institutional language from the clinic's own published materials — to be credited accordingly in liner notes
- **Hagiographic material**: Agatha's story is clearly marked as received tradition, not historical fact; the Catholic Encyclopedia's assessment of the Acts is the research anchor
- **Courtesy contact recommended**: Before release, notify the clinic's communications office as a professional courtesy; they may wish to be involved or provide a statement
