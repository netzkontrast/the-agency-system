# Thorsten Ester-Bode

**Role:** Oberarzt & Leitung PIA (Psychiatrische Institutsambulanz)  
**Organization:** Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit

## Overview

Thorsten Ester-Bode holds dual board certification in Psychiatrie und Psychotherapie and Neurologie — a qualification combination that reflects the standard German pathway of completing two full Facharzt training sequences (each approximately 5 years). He leads the Psychiatrische Institutsambulanz (PIA) at St. Agatha.

Before joining St. Agatha, he operated as an independent specialist at the MVZ für seelische Gesundheit in Wuppertal-Elberfeld (Wesendonkstraße 7, 42103 Wuppertal). That MVZ closed at the end of 2025, leaving approximately 900 patients without a provider. According to the Wuppertaler Rundschau, he did not transfer the practice to a successor but "reoriented himself professionally" — consistent with the move to St. Agatha.
