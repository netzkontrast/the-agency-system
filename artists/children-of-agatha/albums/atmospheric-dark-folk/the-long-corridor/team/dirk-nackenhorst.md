# Dirk Nackenhorst

**Role:** Patientenfürsprecher (Patient Advocate)  
**Organization:** Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit  
**Appointed:** January 2026 (successor to Apollonia Joeres)

---

## Overview

Dirk Nackenhorst serves as Patientenfürsprecher — an independent patient advocate, legally mandated under NRW hospital law (Krankenhausgestaltungsgesetz NRW) for all psychiatric facilities. The role is held by an external volunteer with no employment relationship to the clinic; the advocate receives and investigates patient complaints and concerns, and reports annually to the Landtag.

Nackenhorst took up the role in January 2026, succeeding Apollonia Joeres.
