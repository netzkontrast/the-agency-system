## Overview

Dr. Blasius holds the position of Leitender Oberarzt (senior consultant) at St. Agatha. He carries dual board certification in Psychiatrie und Psychotherapie and Neurologie, supplemented by therapeutic training in depth psychology-based psychotherapy (tiefenpsychologisch fundierte Psychotherapie) and cognitive-behavioural therapy (Verhaltenstherapie). His clinical focus areas include affective disorders, personality traits, and personality disorders.

A prior affiliation with the Psychiatrische Universitätsklinik Zürich (PUK Zürich) appears on his ResearchGate profile, indicating a likely research or residency posting in Switzerland before his arrival at St. Agatha. An archived profile page at MVZ Psyche in Bonn (mvzpsyche.de) existed at one time; that practice has since been absorbed into the Valeara network.
