**Organization:** Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit  
**Day Clinic opened:** 16 October 2018 (16 beds)

---

## Overview

Dr. Gairing leads the psychiatric day clinic at St. Agatha, which she inaugurated in October 2018. She has extensive academic and clinical experience spanning psychiatric residency in Zurich and Basel, research at the Max Planck Institute, and work on maternal mental health at LVR-Klinik Köln. She combines clinical practice with a published research record in coercion reduction, open-ward policies, and therapeutic alliance. Her ResearchGate profile lists 19 publications with approximately 409 citations.
