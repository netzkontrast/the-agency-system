# Vera Meyer

**Role:** Katholische Seelsorgerin (Catholic Chaplain / Pastoral Care)  
**Organization:** Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit

---

## Overview

Vera Meyer serves as the Catholic pastoral care provider (Seelsorgerin) at St. Agatha. Pastoral care at a Cellitinnen-operated facility reflects the founding order's Augustinian Rule and Catholic nursing tradition. The Seelsorge offers spiritual accompaniment to patients, families, and staff — operating alongside but distinct from the psychotherapeutic treatment.

The Kulturkapelle, the clinic's former chapel converted into an art exhibition space, lies within Vera Meyer's institutional context even if it now serves a public rather than confessional function.
