# Susanne Jost

**Role:** Krankenhausdirektorin & Geschäftsführerin (2012–2024); now 1. Vorsitzende, Förderverein  
**Organization:** Formerly Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik; now Förderverein der Freunde und Förderer des St. Agatha Krankenhauses e.V.  
**Tenure at St. Agatha:** 12 years (2012 – April 2024)

---

## Overview

Susanne Jost led St. Agatha for twelve years as Krankenhausdirektorin, later also serving as Geschäftsführerin and Prokuristin of the Cellitinnen-Marienborn entity. She arrived having spent nearly two decades in other Cellitinnen-group institutions, and she oversaw the most consequential structural transformation in the hospital's modern history: the conversion from a general acute hospital into a dedicated mental health specialty clinic.

Since April 2024, she holds the volunteer position of 1. Vorsitzende (first chairperson) of the Förderverein der Freunde und Förderer des St. Agatha Krankenhauses e.V. — the hospital's support association, founded in 1993.

## Achievements at St. Agatha

- Directed major structural modernization and capacity expansion
- Built out inpatient and outpatient mental health services
- Oversaw construction and opening of the psychiatric day clinic (October 2018, 16 beds)
- Participated in developing the thyroid centre
- Introduced an ethics café for staff
- Led the full conversion of the general hospital into a psychiatric specialty clinic
- Managed the organizational merger with MARIENBORN gGmbH
