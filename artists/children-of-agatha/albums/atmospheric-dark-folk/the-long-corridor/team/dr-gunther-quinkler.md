## Overview

Dr. Günther Quinkler served for decades as Chefarzt of Internal Medicine and intensive internal medicine at St. Agatha Krankenhaus, Köln-Niehl. He holds triple board certification in Innere Medizin and Kardiologie. After the hospital's conversion into a psychiatric specialty clinic (completed 2023), he established a private cardiology and internal medicine practice — based at the same address as the clinic (Feldgärtenstr. 97, 50735 Köln). He speaks German, English, and French.

He remains connected to the institution through his role as 2. Vorsitzender (Deputy Chair) of the hospital's support association.
