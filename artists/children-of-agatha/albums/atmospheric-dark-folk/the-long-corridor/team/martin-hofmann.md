## Overview

Martin Hofmann was appointed Pflegedirektor on 1 March 2026, succeeding Karin Janz in the nursing leadership role. He oversees nursing direction for the clinic's inpatient, day-clinic, and ambulatory services. He works alongside Deputy Nursing Director Sarah Bechtold.
