## Overview

Dr. Walter Schurig served as Chefarzt of the Psychosomatik (psychosomatic medicine) department at St. Agatha Krankenhaus Köln during the clinic's pre-2023 operational phase. At that time St. Agatha ran as a psychosomatic specialty clinic (Fachklinik für Psychosomatik und Psychotherapie) before its conversion in 2023 to a psychiatric specialty clinic (Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit) under Dr. Susanne Kowohl's medical leadership and eventually Dr. Hans-Christian Schilling from July 2024.

His name appears in archived clinic brochures held on Yumpu (the document-sharing platform used to host older Cellitinnen/Marienborn institutional publications).
