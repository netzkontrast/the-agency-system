## Overview

Nicola Weller is a Kunsttherapeutin (art therapist) at St. Agatha, where she has worked since 2018. She trained in art therapies at Hogeschool Zuyd in the Netherlands (graduating around 2004) and has since built a career in social psychiatry and psychiatric rehabilitation in the Köln-Düsseldorf region. Her background spans community mental health, addiction rehabilitation, and acute psychiatric inpatient care before settling at St. Agatha.
