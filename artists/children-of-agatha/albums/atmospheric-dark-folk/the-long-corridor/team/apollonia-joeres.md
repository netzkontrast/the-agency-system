# Apollonia Joeres

**Role:** Patientenfürsprecherin (Patient Advocate, former)  
**Organization:** Formerly Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit  
**Succeeded by:** Dirk Nackenhorst (January 2026)

---

## Overview

Apollonia Joeres served as the independent Patientenfürsprecherin at St. Agatha before her successor Dirk Nackenhorst took up the role in January 2026. As patient advocate, she held the externally appointed, legally mandated role of receiving and investigating patient complaints under NRW hospital law.
