# Stephan Koebke

**Role:** Oberarzt / Leitung Station Noah

---

## Overview

Stephan Koebke was appointed Oberarzt and placed in medical charge of Station Noah at its opening in August 2019. The station is St. Agatha's psychiatric admission and acute care ward, accepting patients under ICD-10 F-diagnoses, PsychKG (compulsory psychiatric treatment under NRW law), and BtG (legal guardianship placements). It serves the northern Cologne catchment area (Niehl and Riehl districts). The station name — Noah — was chosen for its connotation of "comfort and peace," in keeping with the hospital's Catholic ethos.

The clinic's 2019 press release states: *"steht unter der medizinischen Leitung des neuen Oberarztes Herrn Stephan Koebke."*

His Doctolib listing contains an unusual detail: his specialty is categorized as *Kinder- und Jugendlichenpsychotherapeut* (child and adolescent psychotherapist), while search metadata simultaneously labels him *Psychiater und Psychotherapeut*. In Germany, a physician can hold Facharzt für Psychiatrie und Psychotherapie (adult psychiatric specialist) alongside a separate licensure as Kinder- und Jugendlichenpsychotherapeut — suggesting an earlier training or parallel qualification. No source explains the combination directly.
