# Dr. med. Susanne Kowohl

**Role:** Chefärztin & Ärztliche Direktorin (2016–2024); now Oberärztin at Marienborn Zülpich  
**Organization:** Formerly Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit  
**Tenure at St. Agatha:** 8 years (2016 – July 2024)  
**Contact (post-retirement role):** s.kowohl@marienborn-zuelpich.de

---

## Overview

Dr. Kowohl served as Chefärztin and Ärztliche Direktorin at St. Agatha for eight years (2016–July 2024), directing the structural transformation of the hospital from a general acute clinic into a dedicated psychiatric specialty facility. She retired from St. Agatha in July 2024 and was succeeded by Dr. Hans-Christian Schilling. Shortly after, she took on an Oberärztin role at Cellitinnen-Marienborn Psychiatrische Klinik in Zülpich, remaining within the MARIENBORN gGmbH group.

## Achievements at St. Agatha

- Led the structural transformation of a predominantly somatic hospital into a dedicated psychiatric specialist clinic
- Inaugurated the psychiatric day clinic (October 2018; 16 beds)
- Established mandatory care catchment for Cologne districts Niehl and Riehl (from October 2018)
- Supported the merger into MARIENBORN gGmbH
