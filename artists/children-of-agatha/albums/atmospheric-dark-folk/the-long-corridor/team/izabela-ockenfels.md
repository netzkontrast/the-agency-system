## Overview

Izabela Ockenfels holds the communications brief for St. Agatha within the broader MARIENBORN gGmbH group. She is an art historian by training who moved into trade fashion journalism, built a freelance crossmedia communications practice, and subsequently took on a staff corporate communications role in a Catholic health-care group operating psychiatric and psychotherapy facilities in Cologne and Zülpich.

She is also currently training as an art therapist (Kunsttherapie), a training begun in 2024 and ongoing.
