# Jessica Euler

**Role:** Verwaltungsdirektorin  
**Organization:** Cellitinnen-Marienborn St. Agatha Fachklinik für Seelische Gesundheit  
**Appointed:** September 2024 (successor to Susanne Jost)  
**Additional role:** Schriftführerin (Secretary), Förderverein der Freunde und Förderer des St. Agatha Krankenhauses e.V.

---

## Overview

Jessica Euler was appointed Verwaltungsdirektorin at St. Agatha in September 2024, succeeding Susanne Jost who retired after twelve years. She manages the administrative and commercial direction of the clinic. In addition to her operational role, she serves as Secretary of the hospital's support association (Förderverein).
