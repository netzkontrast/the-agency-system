## Overview

Appointed on 1 July 2024 as the clinic's Ärztlicher Direktor and Chefarzt, Dr. Schilling arrived at St. Agatha after approximately twelve years as Oberarzt at the Evangelische Stiftung Tannenhof in Remscheid. He oversees the clinic's full treatment spectrum: 85 inpatient beds, 27 day-clinic places, and the Psychiatrische Institutsambulanz (PIA).

Born approximately 1975–1976 (age 48 at appointment). Lives in Leverkusen with family.
