## Overview

Appointed on 1 July 2024 as the clinic's Ärztlicher Direktor and Chefarzt, Dr. Schilling arrived at St. Agatha after twelve years as Oberarzt at the Evangelische Stiftung Tannenhof in Remscheid. He oversees the full treatment spectrum: inpatient wards, 27 day-clinic places, and the Psychiatrische Institutsambulanz (PIA), as well as close cooperation with the sister clinic MARIENBORN Fachklinik für Psychiatrie und Psychotherapie in Zülpich.

Born approximately 1975–1976 (age 48 at appointment). Lives in Leverkusen with family.

In November 2025, under his leadership, the clinic achieved **first place in NRW** for psychiatric specialty clinics in the F.A.Z.-Institut / ServiceValue "Deutschlands beste Krankenhäuser 2025" study (89.2/100 points).

### Tannenhof Institutional Context

Tannenhof Remscheid is a major independent psychiatric foundation in NRW. Its current Ärztlicher Direktor is Prof. Dr. Eugen Davids. The psychiatry department includes specialty stations for: qualified detox (Entzugsbehandlung), psychosomatic and psychotraumatology, sociotherapy and rehabilitation, adolescent psychiatry, and gerontopsychiatry with memory clinic. Schilling worked alongside a team that in the arzt-auskunft.de directory includes Prof. Dr. Hans-Joachim Braune, Dr. Christoph Glaser, and Dr. Eva Niederhofer.

In October 2024 (after Schilling had left), Tannenhof opened the new Paul-Gerhardt-Haus building (100 beds across three stations).
