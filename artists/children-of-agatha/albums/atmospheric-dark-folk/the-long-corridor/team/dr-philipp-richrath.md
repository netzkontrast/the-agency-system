## Overview

Dr. Philipp Richrath is a specialist in plastic and aesthetic surgery (Facharzt für Plastische und Ästhetische Chirurgie). He appears on the LinkedIn company page for St. Agatha Krankenhaus Köln, but no specific current role at the Fachklinik is documented in publicly accessible sources. His connection likely predates the hospital's 2023 conversion to a purely psychiatric specialist clinic, when it still operated general surgical departments including plastic/reconstructive services.
