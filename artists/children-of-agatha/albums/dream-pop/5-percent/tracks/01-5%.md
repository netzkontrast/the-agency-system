## Concept

First-person portrait of a teacher in low-power mode. The track renders depression **not** as a breakdown but as a **numb, ongoing state**: he functions, gives, grades, smiles — inside, 5%. The danger isn't that he feels too low; it's that he no longer *feels* the empty at all — the tiredness signal has gone numb. *"I didn't feel tired — that was the problem."*

That numbness is the source of the distorted gauge: a felt **"7"** over a real **"3"**. It doesn't *lie* — it's **numb** — so he keeps giving, pouring his light into his students (being needed is the one signal that still registers). The turn isn't a resolution; it's a **small failed gesture** — his hand stalls over the grading pen, a name won't come — and he sees the true number, at a cost (a dull texture, never an aphorism).

Agency returns as **letting go** (putting the pen down), not as gripping control — never a willpower story. The ending is not a cure: he **reaches** for the mask, doesn't put it on, lets the amber light in, and breathes once. Not healed.

Two lead metaphors only: **the 5% battery** (the body) and **the oxygen mask** (the gesture). **Show, don't announce** — see [../README.md](../README.md) ("For the lyric-writer") and [../RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md) for accuracy guardrails.

## Mood & Imagery

Empty classroom after the last bell; the buzz of the fluorescent tube; a stack of papers to grade at two a.m. A phone at a red 5% bar (low-power mode). The oxygen mask dropping above the clouds — heard once, not preached. A hand that stalls over the pen; a student's name that won't come. Amber dawn light reaching the grading stack through fogged glass — cool grey tipping slowly into pink. Weary tenderness, luminous, not tidy.

## Musical Direction

- **Tempo**: ~78 BPM (slowcore, dragged); never double-time
- **Feel**: low energy, floating, lots of space. **Density = battery level** — no drums in V1 (just a heartbeat-rate bass pulse so it breathes), brushed drums enter only at the bridge (steady, no buildup, no triumph); fragile, half-whispered final hook
- **Instrumentation**: reverb-drenched tremolo guitars, warm round bass, organ/synth pad bed, tape warmth; a high, child-pure **instrumental hook** (the students' silent voice) seeded in the intro, broken through once in V2, bloomed at the end; intimate dry close-mic mid-baritone, layered floating chorus (no collective "we")

## Production Notes

- Vocal delivery: **dry close-mic, breathy, conversational, barely-there** weary-warm mid-baritone; first person throughout. Layered floating chorus (no collective "we"). The final hook stays **fragile / half-whispered**, never a triumphant swell.
- Bittersweet, life-affirming but **not tidy** — no triumph, no cure. The ending is a reach + one breath ("not healed"); warmth comes from a concrete remembered child detail + amber dawn light, so "not healed" never reads as "not touched."
- **Density = battery level**: no drums in V1 (heartbeat-rate bass pulse so it breathes), brushed drums enter only at the bridge (steady, no buildup).
<!-- SERVICE: suno -->
- **Arrangement directive (user request):** explicit **instrumental-instruction metatags** (`[Instrumental Intro]`, `[Instrumental break]`, `[Outro - instrumental]`); a wordless **instrumental hook** — a high, child-pure motif (= the students' silent voice) — **stated clearly-but-veiled in the intro, ghosted under the choruses, broken through once in V2, bloomed at the final hook**; **instrumental breaks** between vocal sections. The suno-engineer builds the Style/Lyrics boxes accordingly.
- **Suno drift mitigation**: Style Box leads with `slow, spacious, sparse, lots of empty space, unhurried`; Exclude-Styles `no double-time, no busy drums, no buildup, no upbeat`; describe the hook melody explicitly (`recurring high glockenspiel figure`) or Suno buries it.
- V5 tip: keep metatags descriptive (no character names); put instrumental tags on their own lines so Suno reads them as arrangement cues, not lyrics. Target ~4:00.
