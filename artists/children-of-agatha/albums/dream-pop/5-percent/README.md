## Concept

*"5%"* is a standalone, English-language single by Children of Agatha — deliberately outside the St. Agatha clinic world of the earlier albums. First person throughout: a teacher who has been there for everyone so long that he now runs on 5% — and has forgotten how to take care of himself.

The song renders depression **not** as a breakdown scene but as a **numb low-power mode** — a man on autopilot who still smiles, still grades, still gives. The danger isn't that he feels too low; it's that he no longer feels the empty *at all*. The tiredness signal has gone numb (blunted interoception). That is where the distorted self-gauge comes from: a felt **"7"** over a real **"3"** — the gauge doesn't *lie*, it's simply **numb**, so he keeps giving. (See [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) §2.) This stays a *structural* truth — the song **shows** it (he keeps grading, smiling, giving) and never announces it.

His **students** are the *what-for* — being needed is the one signal that still registers. The turn doesn't arrive as a resolution; it arrives as a **small failed gesture**: his hand stalls over the grading pen, a name won't come, he reads an answer without taking in a word. He sees the true number, and it **costs** him — quietly, as a dull texture, never as an aphorism.

**Agency** returns — but not as "hands back on the wheel." It shows up as **letting go**: putting the pen down, suspending the giving for one breath. (Deliberately not a willpower story: depression isn't a discipline problem, self-care isn't a cure — [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) §4.)

The tone stays **bittersweet, not tidy**. The ending is **not** a cure: he does *not* put the mask on — he only **reaches** for it, lets the light in, and breathes once. Not healed. Beginning, again, to act.

## Structure

Single — one track, one self-contained arc. **Guiding idea: density = battery level.**

1. **Instrumental Intro** — the wordless, high, child-pure **instrumental hook** (the students' silent voice), stated **clearly but veiled**. No drums.
2. **Verse 1** (no drums; a slow **heartbeat-rate bass pulse** so the emptiness *breathes*, not stalls) — autopilot on 5%; concrete, teacher-specific. The gauge reads "okay."
3. **Hook** (open / universal — the **I→you→I** axis) — the overheard mask announcement, half-heard. *"Secure your own mask — I'm at five percent."*
4. **Verse 2** — the giving falters: *you can't charge another device from a dying one.* Mid-verse the **instrumental hook breaks through once, unbidden** — the signal reaching him before he can feel it; he can't answer it.
5. **Bridge** (emotional peak; **brushed drums enter** — soft, steady, **no buildup, no triumph**) — the small failed gesture; the true number; the cost (a dull texture, not a slogan). Agency = **putting the pen down**.
6. **Final Hook** (fragile, half-whispered; the **instrumental hook blooms** = dawn as *return*) — *"secure my own mask"*; he **reaches**, doesn't put it on; one concrete **remembered image** (a child's offhand "thanks" / a drawing held out); amber light reaches the grading stack; he lets it in; one breath. Not healed.

## Sonic Palette

- **Density = battery level (guiding principle).** **Verse 1 has no drums** (just bass + a single tremolo figure, lots of empty air); **brushed drums enter only at the bridge** — steady, *no buildup, no triumph*. Texture density tells the charge state.
- **Motion inside the stillness:** under Verse 1, a slow **heartbeat-rate bass pulse** at ~78 so the emptiness *breathes* rather than stalls. Sparse ≠ still.
- **Tempo:** slowcore, ~78 BPM — dragged, unhurried, spacious. **Never double-time.**
- **Textures:** reverb-drenched tremolo guitars, warm round bass, organ/synth pad bed, tape warmth. Luminous dream-pop haze over slowcore weight.
- **Instrumental hook:** a high, child-pure motif (glockenspiel / wordless voice-pad) = the students' silent voice — **stated clearly-but-veiled in the intro, ghosted under the choruses, broken through once in V2, fully bloomed only at the final hook** (dawn as *return*).
- **Vocals:** first person, **dry close-mic, breathy, conversational, barely-there** (not flat/muddy); weary, warm mid-baritone. The final hook stays **fragile / half-whispered**, floating inside the instrumental hook — never a triumphant swell. Layered floating chorus (no collective "we"). **English.**
- **Mood:** weary tenderness, bittersweet, luminous. Life-affirming without triumph; honest, not tidy.
- **Target duration:** ~4:00 (every extra minute of no-drums slowcore raises drift/boredom risk).

**Reference anchors:** Beach House, Mazzy Star (dream-pop haze); Slowdive / Mojave 3 (slowcore-shoegaze warmth).

## For the lyric-writer (guardrails)

- **Show, don't announce.** The "felt 7 / real 3" thesis stays *structural* — render it (he keeps grading, smiling, gives; "I didn't feel tired"), don't let the song explain itself. The numbers may stay implicit; the title + the drumless V1 already say "5%."
- **Two lead images only:** battery + mask. The stalled pen / forgotten name / amber light are *single beats*.
- **Hook stays open:** keep "you/your" pronominal (no classroom vocabulary in the hook) so it travels beyond teachers; lock teacher detail into the verses; flip "your → my" in the final hook.
- **The cost is unsharp** — a dull texture (reading the same line three times), not a quotable aphorism. Avoid self-pity.
- **Warmth must be concrete:** one remembered child detail + the amber-light beat + a small warmth-verb ("lets the light in"), so "not healed" ≠ "not touched."
- **No willpower framing, no tidy recovery.** See [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

## Representation / Accuracy

This single is *fictional* (not a documentary) — but it must **not misrepresent depression**. The clinical and lived-experience research lives in **[RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md)**. Binding guardrails for lyric-writer & suno-engineer:

- **"felt 7 / real 3" = a missing signal, not a lie or arrogance** (numbness, blunted interoception).
- **No willpower narrative**: depression isn't a discipline problem; self-care isn't a cure.
- **Burnout ≠ depression** — don't conflate; avoid "quit the job and it's fixed."
- **No tidy recovery arc**; the ending stays open (reaching, not healed).
- **Dignity, person-first**; no romanticizing, no "tortured artist."
- **No method/detail** of suicide or self-harm; never a plot device.
- **Help signposting** belongs in the description / release notes, **not** in the lyric; plus an "art, not treatment advice" note.
