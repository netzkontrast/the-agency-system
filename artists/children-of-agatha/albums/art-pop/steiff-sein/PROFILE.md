# Anke — Subject Profile

> **PRIVATE — background only.** This profile compiles everything shared in this
> session about the real person at the center of the single *„Wir sehen dich"* and
> the EP *„Steiff sein"*. It exists so the writing is accurate and kind.
>
> **Binding rules:** The suspected/possible **diagnoses below are NEVER named** in
> any lyric, title, Suno metatag, promo, or artwork — they are context only. **No
> real name** reaches a public-facing output. In songs, only the *lived experience*

## 1. At a glance

- **Who:** mother, wife, friend; lifelong florist; passionate Steiff collector; makes art (paints / decorates) constantly.
- **Age:** over 60.
- **Core tension:** caught between *Aufbruch / sich öffnen / gesehen werden* and what *pulls backward / das Alt-Gelernte*. One step toward the wish for warmth, one step back toward **Funktion**.
- **The thesis of the single:** *„Du siehst die Stärken anderer — wir sehen Deine."*
- **The fight of the EP:** *ums Sein-Dürfen* — permission to simply be, without functioning or producing.

## 2. Strengths & longings (as the family sees her)

- Brave (*mutig*), loving, very interested in life, witty/funny.
- A great mother and wife, a good friend; *„du managst alles"*.
- Learning to be strong enough to also **show weakness** sometimes.
- *„Mama-Hände"* — hands that see everyone; *„du gibst die besten Umarmungen"*.
- *„Deine langen Haare sind so phänomenal"* (line originally in Gülay's voice).
- Longs for warmth and closeness — but pulled back toward function.
- **She dedicated a special song to each great love of her life** — the EP's
  mandatory self-love climax (Track 06, *„Steiff sein / Du darfst"*) inverts this:
  the song she finally writes **for herself**.

## 3. Origin & conditioning

- **Family = only functioning.** Growing up, it was always only about *funktionieren*; warmth was scarce. She **learned hugging only late** in life.
- **Learning difficulty.** Because of it, not every path was open. While other children had time to play, she had to drill **Diktate** over and over — from early on, everything was only **demand**.
- **Vocation not from the heart.** She wanted to work creatively, but on her **father's advice became a florist (Floristin)** — a craft she never burned for. Daily beauty as duty; the creativity she wanted stayed deferred.
- **Father's humor.** Her father met bad events **with a laugh**, took everything with humor, and wished every customer who said "schönen Tag" a *„wunderschönen Tag"* back. She **inherited the laugh** — staying witty is both gift and armor.

## 4. The loud outside / the armor

- Keeps distance from everyone by appearing **hard and loud**, always everything *im Blick und Griff*.
- Because of the armor she is **misjudged (verkannt)** — nobody sees **the little child in her**.
- Has often been the one who **carries others** (*andere huckepack*) and never asks who carries her.

## 5. Inner world

- A constant **inner critic**; growing **lack of self-love**.
- In age, much becomes clear — but she feels **trapped in how she always was**; she neither **recognizes** nor **accepts** herself.
- The fight is with **„die Bestie im Bauch"** and with a **body that cannot relax**.
- Her **emotion went numb** — *„weil sie für sich selbst zu viel war."*
- Caged also in **compulsive making** — constant painting / artistic creating / having to produce things that are **seen** (worth tied to visible output).

## 6. The body

- **Constant daily physical pain.**
- Tension that won't release; the body carries what the mind was never allowed to put down.
- The dread-question: *„Wie soll das alles werden — jetzt, wo alles weh tut?"*

## 7. Relationships

- **Her husband** — lovingly nicknamed by her *„Männlein"* (ennobling). He tries, **in his own way**, to take things off her; he can gently hold up a **„3"** (private couple's sign) to show her when she is *drüber* and wanting too much. But he is also **a man of his own space** — affection at a distance, **without much hugging**.
- **Her pattern:** closeness was always what she needed but **never let herself admit**; so she kept choosing **partners who were similarly distant** — partly because she could never grant the *wish for closeness* to herself.
- **Lilifee — her daughter.** She is **very, very proud** of her. Crucially: **with and at her daughter, hugging WORKS** — the one relationship where closeness and embrace flow freely. *(Nickname carries the soft „Prinzessin Lillifee" resonance — handle with tenderness, never mockery.)*
- **Dogs:** Lucky and one more *(second name not yet given)*.

## 8. Joys turned to burdens

- **Steiff animals** — thousands of them; the **Steiff Festival** is *only for her inner child*. A lifelong love that **drove and enriched** her — now, in crisis, increasingly also a **burden**.
- **Seasonal home decorating** — 3–4 new looks a year. Once filled her with joy; now a **torment and duty** that mirrors back **how alone she is**.
- **Painting / making** — see §5 (cage and survival both).

## 9. Background — possible diagnoses *(NEVER named in songs)*

Shared by the family as context only; **none confirmed**, none to appear in any output:

- Suspected **Borderline** traits and/or **ADHD** (likely late/unrecognized & masked).
- Signs she believes are present of **trauma** and **depression** — *but never so extreme as to be diagnosed*, because she **manages it internally through self-burden**.
- To explore further as background: **complex PTSD (KPTBS)** from developmental/relational conditioning (the childhood of only-functioning).
- **Decades-suppressed emotional instability** (~6); now the **strength to mask is running out**.
- **Deep grief & loss-anxiety (Verlustangst)**; at 60+, acute fear of losing **work ability (Arbeitsfähigkeit)** and the **small, hard-won life she built for herself**.

> See [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) §3–§4 and the Round-4 addenda for grounded, non-clinical background on how these interrelate — and the craft rules for rendering them **without ever naming them**.

## 10. The journey (open questions — the EP's content)

- Not only what to **do for herself** to *allow* herself to be — but what to **actively claim/demand (einfordern)** for herself.
- *„Wie soll ich wieder funktionieren, wenn jeder Weg, den ich in Therapie lerne, nur mehr weh tut?"*
- What is still needed for her journey — held **open**, never answered with a slogan.
