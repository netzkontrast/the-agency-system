## Music / spoken-word fingerprint  (role only — no name)
In the **music** layer the narrator is the **framing / direct-address** voice of
a track's spoken intro (e.g. Chapter Zero's *Vorwort* cold-open, generated as its
**own clip** then butt-joined to the sung body). Calm, philosophical, measured;
**slow, deliberate, *pointiert*** — each phrase weighted, generous pauses, the
philosophy given room to land. A faint **drone may bleed in** under the clip.
**It MUST stay timbrally distinct from the sung host** (`host.md`) — don't let the
framing voice and the waking self blur; if the host is rendered fragile/synthetic
at genesis, the narrator reads as the grounded, composed outside voice (or vice
versa), but never the same timbre.

Built with the reusable **3-layer Suno toolkit** in
`overrides/suno-preferences.md → "Narration / Spoken-Word Delivery DNA"`:
(1) **per-line bracketed delivery metatags** on their own lines (V5 reads them as
directions, not lyrics); (2) **ellipsis pause-cues** `…`; (3) **CAPS** on the
single load-bearing word of a line. **Reformat for breathing** (line breaks /
blank lines) to slow the read — **never rewrite or expand the words.**
name_exposure holds: role language only, no personal name in any music field.

## Novel voice  (name allowed)
Erzähler: mediator-voice between world and reader. Essayistic, philosophical. Direct reader-address permitted. Hypotactic-philosophical, long sentence-arcs. Fluid scaling between 1st-person host-sight and 3rd-person AEGIS-description. Vocabulary: reflective, ontological, no jargon — Werdung, Trennung, Stille, Substrat, Echo, Resonanz. Never didactic, never explanatory; in Kap 40 witnessing, not resolving. Mikro: *Was du jetzt liest, ist nicht das, was geschah. Es ist, was übrig blieb, als das, was geschah, sich entschied, sich erinnern zu lassen.*

## Role in system
The framing voice of the Genesis-prologue (Kap 0) and the Coda (Kap 40), with scattered appearances at act-transitions. In the Coda it returns bittersweet, calm, healed — but without explanation.
