## Music / lyric fingerprint  (role only — no name)
Mid-baritone, weary, present-tense, slightly under-articulated; dry close-mic, no reverb. Sentences that trail and don't punctuate. Voice of someone who keeps almost-remembering: "I must have —", "Someone in here —". Holds the surface, suffers the gaps. Do NOT resolve the trailing lines or sound certain. **(This is the *mature* host timbre — see "Voice arc" below; a genesis/proto-host work may cast it differently.)**

## Novel voice  (name allowed)
Kael: 1st person, present dominant. Sober, observing, short-declarative. Gaps where memory should be. Counting-mania as somatic tic (2,304 tiles, 21°C, 4-second breath). Stilebene 1 vocabulary — never describes own feeling directly; world outside, feeling between the lines. No DKT terms in Act I.

## Visual grammar  (role only — no name)
Extra-hard graphite (6H–9H), ruler, compass on pore-free Bristol; Clinical White / Void Black, absolute desaturation. Razor-sharp continuous vectors, zero tremor — the "perfect windowless cube." Strict orthographic symmetry, crushing negative space. Under stress: micro-breaks, "edge jittering."

## Voice arc  (role only — no name)
The host timbre is **developmental, not fixed**. At **genesis** the proto-host
may be rendered **fragile and nascent**; in later work it **matures toward** the
weary mid-baritone above. The exact per-album casting is **album content** — the
album's `DESIGN.md` owns the precise descriptor (e.g. *Chapter Zero* casts the
proto-host → host spine, T1→T13, as a fragile genderless head-voice). This note
only records that the **arc exists**.

**Rules:** hold ONE chosen timbre per work, unbroken first track → last — the
"same voice, gone hollow" payoff lives on that continuity. The **narrator**
(framing voice) must stay timbrally **distinct** from the sung host
(`_modes/narrator.md`), and at genesis the host stays distinct from the child
small-voice (`child-freeze.md`).
