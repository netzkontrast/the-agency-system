# Captions that win (from 87 Opus 5.5 posts on X, Sep 2026)

- **The model name in the first three words**, then the result: "Opus 5.5 one-shotted a Mortal Kombat-style game starring me."
- **Under 15 words for the hook**, then at most 1-2 proof lines. The video does the rest.
- **A constraint beats an adjective:** "One prompt. One HTML file." / "No video model. No images." / "every frame drawn in JavaScript". Hype words ("insane", "crazy", "wild") are common but mid-tier; many users don't want them at all. Ask or leave them out.
- **A famous reference** people recognize in one frame (Minecraft, Mario Kart, Mortal Kombat) spreads best. Say "-style" when it isn't the real thing.
- **Hard numbers stick:** "$1874.40 of tokens", "12 hours", "1,605 lines".
- **Something to take away:** the prompt, a playable link, or the source repo in the first reply drives bookmarks.
- **A curated list is bookmark bait.** "10 launch videos that ..." / "every trick in this video, listed" in the post or first reply: one list post drew more bookmarks than likes. When the video teaches, give the list.
- **Make it theirs:** if the user is in the video, say so ("starring me"); strangers won't know otherwise.
- **Honest:** "one-shotted" only for an untouched one-shot file. Otherwise "built with Opus 5.5 in one prompt, then a few tweaks".

First reply template: "One prompt. One HTML file. [Where the art/audio came from]. [Link or 'prompt below']."

LinkedIn: same idea, fuller sentence, first person, one plain reaction line, no hashtags wall. Hebrew posts for Hebrew audiences if the user writes in Hebrew.

**Brand spec pieces:** say it in the post ("spec piece, not affiliated with <brand>"). Fonts, colors and logo paths pulled from the brand's live site are stand-ins until the brand supplies masters; never let an image model draw a logo.

## The brand's own launch posts

When the film launches someone's product, they also need the posts that carry it. These are in the brand's voice and about the product, not about how the film was made (that is the maker's post, above; offer it separately).

- One post per place it will run, each written for that place: X short and first, LinkedIn a fuller first-person sentence or two, Instagram a line and at most a couple of the brand's own tags.
- Open with the film's line or a question the viewer can answer in a word. A line any brand could post ("order yours today") loses to one only this product could say.
- One action, with the link they gave. No invented link, price, date or number.
- No wall of hashtags, no hype words the brand does not use itself.
- Give two or three options for the first line when the person is choosing, and mark them as yours.
