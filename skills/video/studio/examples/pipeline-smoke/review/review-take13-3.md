## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 7.** A big, saturated knot and four beat bounces in 2 s grab attention, but frame 0 is crowded: the knot covers the orange and yellow blocks behind it (full_t0.png).
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** The knot reads well, but from 4 s on the blocks are about 20 px wide on phone_360px.png (rows 2 and 3) and become confetti.
- **Motion quality: 6.** Pops, not moves: the knot jumps in size between frames at 0.92 and 1.04 (strip_0.0-1.1, row 3); the ring breaks into dashed arcs and the shadows come loose at 1.38 (strip_1.4-2.9, row 2).
- **Variety: 6.** Every second shows the same seven objects in the same ring. From 2.6 to 8.0 the frame-to-frame change is close to zero apart from the flat-shape beat (sheet_1fps_part1.png, frames 3 to 8).
- **Composition: 6.** The knot is the focal point but keeps colliding with its own satellites. At 1.38 the orange icosahedron sits in front of the knot. At 0.42 everything crowds the top while the bottom 40% is empty, and the orange block is about 8 px from the top edge.
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 7.** Good on the key words: an orange flash lands on 2.50 ("atmen") and the shapes go flat at 4.00 ("ich"). The rest is weak: the beats at 4.53, 5.0, 6.53 and 7.03 get no visible hit (beats_grid2.png, frames 2, 3 and 7). The audio fades out from 7.0 to 8.0, but the picture neither settles nor fades.
- **Overall: 6.** A tidy hand-drawn look, but it plays like a looping demo, not a finished clip.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:01.4 (1.38 s)**: a render fault at full speed. The ring breaks into gaps, the shadow discs float away from their objects, and an orange block covers the focal knot.
2. **0:04.1 to 0:05.5**: a dead stretch. After the flatten on "ich", the flat shapes sit still for about 1.4 s through two beats (4.53 and 5.0), so the 120 bpm pulse stops being visible.
3. **0:07.0 to 0:08.0**: no ending. The music fades, but the picture keeps orbiting at full brightness and cuts off mid-move at 7.95 (strip_6.4-7.95, last frame).

## 4. Looks off or cheap at full size
- Double or ghost rings at 1.71 and 6.0 (strip_1.4-2.9 frame 7; strip_5.4-6.1 row 3) look like two overlaid draws.
- The colour flash at 2.46 comes with a hard jump in camera framing, so it reads as a glitch, not a hit.
