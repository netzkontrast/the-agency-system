## Retelling
Seven painted toy shapes rise out of a sheet of paper and draw in twice as the singer says "lass mich, lass mich",
then all burst outward on "atmen" — except one teal knot that stays put. On "ich bin hier" the camera leaves the
others behind and closes in on that knot, which turns alone and pulses with the beat. Then it fades back into the
blank paper the film began on.

(Retelling works in three sentences: no BLOCKING #1.)

## NOTES
#1 · shot 2 · 00:02.5–00:04.0 · The exhale overshoot throws shapes across the frame: on the 2.7 still the purple cube
is cut by the left edge and the orange icosahedron swings into the lower foreground as the largest object in frame,
pulling the eye off the teal knot whose stillness is the setup for shot 3 (before-the-first-cut §6, §7: a bouncy
spring over distance throws things across neighbours). · Cap the outward travel/overshoot so every shape stays fully
inside frame and none grows larger than the knot's screen size; keep the knot the visual centre of the exhale.

#2 · shot 2 · 00:03.1–00:04.0 · After the settle (~0.8 s) the board has no new change until "ich" at 4.00; the 3.0 and
3.5 beats and the 3.532 downbeat pass unmarked, so ~0.7 s sits as a state while shot 4 uses beat swells. Short, not
dead (< 6 s), but the pulse vocabulary is inconsistent. · Give the ring a small shared breath on the 3.53 downbeat (or
on 3.0/3.5) so the whole film shares one pulse, and so the knot's stillness against it is still visible.

#3 · shot 3 · 00:04.0–00:05.0 · Two reads ("one shape is chosen", "the others make room") run in the same 1.0 s
window. Here they read as one compound move, so it holds, but the board does not sequence them. · Start the others'
drift on "ich" (4.00) and let the push-in lead to land on "hier" (5.00), with the others clear of frame by ~4.6 so the
last 0.4 s of the push is the knot alone; or write them as a single read.

#4 · shot 3 · 00:04.5 · On the 4.5 still the yellow knot leaves upward into the sky band (top-left) and the purple cube
sits on the horizon line, while the camera pushes in. Shapes shrinking while the camera approaches reads as them
fleeing into the sky, against the floor logic of the earlier frames. · Send the others outward along the floor (left
and right, past the frame edges) or keep their exit on the ground plane; keep exits leaving on the two sides, not up.

#5 · shot 4 · 00:05.0–00:07.0 · The knot is small on the 4.5 still and fills about half the frame on the 6.2 still;

say. · Add a 5.0 s still to the sheet and state the settled framing; keep any further growth explicitly as a slow push
(camera spring) distinct from the beat swells.

#6 · shot 1 · 00:01.0 · The 1.007 transient and "lass" (stress 1.00) pass with only the orbit; the first event is the
