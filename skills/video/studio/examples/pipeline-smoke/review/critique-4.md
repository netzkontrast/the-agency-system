## Retelling (as a viewer)
Coloured paper shapes rise out of a blank page and drift on a curved paper floor; as the singer says "lass mich… lass
mich" they draw in twice, and on "atmen" they all spring outward at once. On "ich bin hier" the camera leaves the group
and pushes in to one teal knot, which turns alone and pulses on the beat. Then the camera backs away and the page
fades back to blank paper, as it began.

Retellable in three sentences: yes.

- **Arc 7** — page → group → one → page is a real shape; the six other shapes leave without the viewer seeing where.

- **Sync plan 7** — mich / atmen / ich / hier all on stressed syllables and measured transients; the closing gesture's stated downbeat placement is wrong.

## BLOCKING
**#1 · shots 3–5 · 00:04.5–00:07.85 · a second knot sits in the hold that should show one shape alone.**
The board says "the other shapes have moved out of frame" and the read is "it turns alone in the centre", but the sheet
shows the small orange knot hanging in the sky at the top of frame at 4.5, 6.2 and 7.2 s (and the purple shape
half-cut at the left edge at 4.5). It is the same form as the hero (a knot), warm against the cool paper, and it stays
for about 3.4 s, roughly 40 % of the film. The viewer's eye splits between "the chosen one" and "the other knot", and
the board and sheet disagree. **Fix:** clear the orange knot and the purple shape out of frame by the time the push
settles on "hier" (5.00): move them out past the frame edge during the push (4.0–5.0), or let the push's tilt keep them
below the frame edge. Re-draw the 4.5 / 6.2 / 7.2 s frames and check that only the teal knot (and paper floor) are in
them from 5.0 s to the end.

## NOTES
**N1 · shots 0–2 · 00:00.3–00:02.7 · "seven shapes" reads as six; the hero's colour isn't unique.**
In every early frame the seventh shape (a teal polyhedron) is hidden behind the gold icosahedron; you only see a teal
sliver. It is also the same teal as the hero knot, and the hero shares its form with the orange knot. **Fix:** move the
seventh shape so its silhouette is clear in the wide, and give it a colour that is not teal (teal belongs to the hero alone).

**N2 · shot 1 · 00:00.5–00:02.35 · the hero's setup is buried.**
At 0.3 s the teal knot is half behind the big front icosahedron; at 1.5 s it overlaps the orange icosahedron. The board
wants it as "the still hub of the ring", but the eye goes to the big orange icosahedron in front. **Fix:** keep the
teal knot's silhouette clear of every neighbour from 0.5 to 2.35 s (open sky or floor around it), still same size.

**N3 · shot 2 · 00:02.5–00:04.0 · the gold icosahedron is cut by the right edge at the outward breath.**
At 2.7 s it sits half off-frame (with the hidden teal shape behind it). A shape waiting half off-screen on the payoff
reads as a framing mistake. **Fix:** cap the overshoot radius so every shape stays inside the frame at the peak of
the spring (overshoot at 2.5–2.9 s), or let it leave clearly, not halfway.

**N4 · shots 3–5 · 00:04.0–00:07.85 · the six other shapes leave with no payoff.**
Nothing shows where they go; the pull-back in shot 5 reveals an empty floor, raising "where did they go?". **Fix:**
either show them drift off in shot 3 as part of the push (a clear exit, one direction), or let the pull-back in shot 5
reveal them faintly at the edges as the page covers them, so the ending rhymes with shot 0 (all seven, then paper).

**N5 · shot 5 · 00:06.65–00:07.85 · the downbeat claim is off, and the end lands off the release.**
The board says the release downbeat (7.51) is "at the gesture's middle", but the middle of 6.65–7.85 is 7.25; 6.65 is
also off the beat grid (beats at 6.5 / 7.0). The release section (RMS drops to −23 dB at 7.507) is the music's own
"exhale" and nothing visible marks it. **Fix:** start the pull-back on the 7.0 beat (after the 6.5 swell), fade the page
over it and land blank paper on the 7.51 downbeat; hold blank paper 7.51–8.0 (0.49 s instead of 0.15 s).

**N6 · shot 5 · 00:07.85–00:08.0 · last read held 0.15 s (rule: ≥ 2 s).**
Locked — producer's call: the 8 s length and the next word at 8.00 leave no room for a 2 s hold. N5 at least triples it.

## Keep

- The two pull-ins on the stressed "mich" (1.50, 2.04), the second deeper, and the 0.15 s anticipation into "atmen".
- The outward breath landing on the "atmen" stress (2.50, the measured transient) and held 1.5 s before the next read.
- The push starting on "ich" (4.00) and settling on "hier" (5.00); the swells on 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.5 in the voiceless hold.
