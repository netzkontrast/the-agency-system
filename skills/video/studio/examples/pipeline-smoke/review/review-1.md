# Review — round 1

## 1. Retold
Seven coloured shapes (cubes, icosahedra, two knots) drift above a curved paper floor; then everything except the teal knot flies out of frame, the knot fills the centre, slowly turns, shrinks, flattens into a fish-like outline and the screen ends on blank paper. I could follow it. I could not tell why the knot ends as a fish outline.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 4.** Frame 0.0 is blank paper (f_0.0); at 0.3 the shapes are still pale and washed out (full_t0.3). Nothing moves fast or reads strongly in the first 2 s.
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** The shapes read clearly (phone_360px), but the yellow knot and purple cube shrink to specks at the top edges from 4.5 s (strip_4.0-5.0, frames 9–10).
- **Motion quality: 6.** Smooth and free of jitter, but 2.3–2.8 hardly moves (strip_2.3-2.8). At ~7.35 the shaded knot jumps to a flat outline in one frame instead of changing over time (strip_7.0-7.6, frames 7–8).
- **Variety: 5.** One unchanged layout holds from 0.3 to 4.0 (3.7 s). One rotating knot holds from 5.0 to 7.0 with almost no change (frames 5.4–5.7 are nearly the same).
- **Composition: 6.** Seven shapes of equal weight until 4.0, so the eye has no single place to land (beats_grid 0.3–2.7). The fly-out cuts shapes on the frame edges (cube at the left edge at 4.1–4.2, icosahedron at the bottom edge at 4.3–4.4). No type appears in the picture.
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 4.** The 120 bpm thump falls every 0.5 s, but nothing in the picture pulses with it. The downbeats at 1.53, 3.53 and 5.53 show no visible event. The stressed "atmen" at 2.50 gets no visual answer (strip_2.3-2.8). The push-in starting at 4.0 does land on "ich", and the fade at 7.0–8.0 matches the audio fade.
- **Overall: 5.** A clean look and a clear single move, but it is mostly static and does not answer the music. Real makers would cut or hit on the beat.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:01.5–0:03.5:** The picture ignores the beat and the key word: steady drift, no hit on the downbeats (1.53, 3.53) and nothing on "atmen" (2.50).
2. **0:00.0–0:00.3:** Opens on empty, pale paper. The first readable frame is washed out, so there is no hook.
3. **0:05.0–0:07.0:** Two seconds of one slowly rotating knot with no new event. The downbeat at 5.53 passes unmarked.
