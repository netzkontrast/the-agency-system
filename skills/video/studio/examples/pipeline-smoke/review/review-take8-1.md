# Review — take 8, round 1

## 1. Retold
"A teal knot turns slowly over a beige paper floor with a ring of coloured blocks; around the 'atmen' line the floor warms and a ring draws out, then the blocks go flat like paper cut-outs, pop back to 3D and more rings spread out. Oh, and the knot's shadow is a bunny face." I could follow all of it. The bunny is what a friend would remember.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 5.** 0:00–0:02.4 is one slow turn with no event (strip_0.0-1.1: 12 frames almost the same). The first hit comes at 2.50.
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** The bold flat colours read well. By 7.5–7.95 the camera has pulled back and the knot is about 1/6 of the frame width (phone_360px, last row).
- **Motion quality: 6.** Three things break the flow. At 2.46 to 2.50 the layout jumps in one frame. At about 5.55 the shapes snap from flat to 3D between two frames (strip_5.4-6.1, frames 2–3). At 6.9–7.4 the rings blink on, off, on (strip_6.9-7.95).
- **Variety: 6.** Nothing new happens from 0:00 to 0:02.5. After that a new event comes about every 1.5 s, at 2.5, 4.0, 5.5, 6.5 and 7.5.
- **Composition: 7.** The knot is a clear single focal point. Two faults: at 0:00 it hides the orange and yellow blocks behind it (full_t0), and at about 2.75 the orange icosahedron is cut by the bottom edge (beats_grid1, row 3, first frame).
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 6.** Three hits land: 'atmen' at 2.50, 'ich' at 4.0 (the shapes flatten) and the downbeats at 5.53 and 7.51. Many do not. The steady thump every 0.5 s has no visual pulse: average brightness holds within ±1 outside 2.5–2.9 and 5.5–6.1. Downbeats 1.53 and 3.53 show nothing. During the fade-out (7.0–8.0) a new ring is growing, so the picture does not wind down with the sound.
- **Overall: 6.** The look is coherent and pleasant. The empty open, the stray face and the jumpy transitions read as a test render, not a finished clip.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:02.5–0:07.95.** The knot's shadow is a cartoon rabbit head with eyes, and at about 4.6 a mouth (full_t4.4, strip_3.9-5.2). It does not match the knot that casts it. In an abstract-shapes film it reads as a bug or an in-joke, and it is visible at 360 px.
2. **0:00.0–0:02.4.** A dead open. Nothing changes on the beats at 0.53, 1.03, 1.5 and 2.04 or on the downbeat at 1.53, so a muted viewer gets 2.5 s of a slow spin before anything happens.
3. **0:02.46–0:03.0.** The 'atmen' hit lurches. The blocks jump outward in one frame, the camera pushes in until the orange icosahedron is cut by the bottom edge (about 2.75), then pulls back out by 3.05.

## 4. Looks off at full size
- At 0:04.4 the knot loses its tube form and reads as a lumpy blob (full_t4.4).
- At 7.95 the block shadows sit far from the floating blocks, while the knot's shadow sits directly under it. The lighting does not agree.
- At about 7.0 a pale ghost ring appears for a frame (strip_6.9-7.95, frame 1).
- The film stops at 7.95 while a ring is still growing. There is no closing image.
- Locked items: none of them hurt the film.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Every axis is below 8. The lowest is hook (5).
