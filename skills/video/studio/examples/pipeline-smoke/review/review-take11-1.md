The camera pulls back, a ring flashes, the shapes collapse flat onto the floor, then pop back up, and it just stops." I could follow it. ## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 5.** 0:00–0:02 is a calm tableau and a slow pull-back. Nothing changes until the warm flash at 2.67 s (`sheet_1fps_part1` panels 1–3, `strip_0.0-1.1`).
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** Flat colours and outlines read well. After the pull-back (2.7 s on) the shapes are thumbnail-sized and the knot fills about 15% of the frame (`phone_360px` rows 2–4).
- **Motion quality: 7.** Easing is smooth. Each shape lifts well clear of its shadow on the bounce (0.9–1.0 s, my frames a_03–a_04), so it reads as detached, not jumping. A doubled, ghosting ring appears at 5.8–6.1 s (`strip_5.4-6.1`, panels 7–10).
- **Variety: 6.** New events land at 2.67, about 4.2, 5.4 and 7.6 s. The first 2.6 s add nothing, and 6.1–7.5 s only repeats the opening layout.
- **Composition: 6.** There is one clear focal point. But the orange hexagon is cut by the bottom edge at 5.33–5.5 s (my frame `b_5.4`, `strip_5.4-6.1` panels 1–2). From 0 to 3 s the knot hangs up and to the right of its own shadow and hides the orange shape behind it (`full_t2.5`).
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 5.** Nothing happens on "atmen" at 2.50 s; `full_t2.5` matches its neighbours, and the flash comes about 4 frames late at 2.67 s. The downbeats at 1.53 and 3.53 s show nothing (`beats_grid1`). The flatten near "ich" at 4.00 s starts at about 4.1–4.2 s, which is late. The hits at 5.53 and 7.51 s land.
- **Overall: 5.** It is a clean style test, not something a maker would ship as a clip. It has too few events and its main accents miss the song.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:02.5:** The stressed word "atmen" gets no picture event. The flash and ring arrive at 0:02.7, so the main accent of line 1 is missed.
2. **0:05.3–0:05.5:** The orange hexagon slides off the bottom edge, and its shadow is cut at 0:05.6. That is the most visible framing error in the film.
3. **0:00.0–0:02.6:** The hook is dead. For 2.6 s the picture only drifts, the downbeat at 1.53 s is not marked, and a muted phone viewer has no reason to stay.

## 4. Off / cheap at full size
- At 0:05.8–0:06.1 two concentric rings overlap like a render ghost.
- The knot's shadow is offset from the knot from 0 to 3 s, so it looks pasted on.
- The audio fades out over 7.0–8.0 s but the picture keeps moving at full brightness and simply cuts at 8.0 s (`d_7.9`), so the ending feels truncated.
- The purple octahedron is hidden behind the knot at 7.5–7.9 s.
- Locked item: the 8 s length and the even 120 bpm pulse give little to build to. Even so, accents could land on "atmen" and "ich".

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Several axes score below 8: hook 5, sound sync 5, variety 6, composition 6, and readability and motion 7 each.
