# Review, round 1 (take 4)

## 1. Retold
A big teal knot hangs over a sandy dome, coloured blocks rain in and circle it, then they shrink away and leave the knot turning alone until everything fades to pale grey. Easy to follow; what it *means* (the breath, "I am here") does not reach a muted viewer.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook 7**: the large, well-drawn knot at 0:00.0 holds the eye, and blocks falling in by 0:00.6 give early motion (strip_0.0-0.6). But the first frame is static and the knot nearly touches the top edge (full_t0).
- **Readability at 360 px 8**: no type. Every shape reads cleanly at phone width (phone_360px).
- **Motion quality 7**: eased, smooth and with no jitter (all strips). But from 1.5 to 3.9 s the blocks barely move (strip_2.3-2.8, all 12 frames nearly identical), and there is no visible pulse on the 0.5 s beat.
- **Variety 6**: there are four states (entry, orbit, recede, lone knot plus fade). 1.5–3.9 s is about 2.4 s of near-static orbit, and 5.0–7.0 s is a lone knot that only turns and grows (beats_grid1 row 3, beats_grid2).
- **Composition 7**: there is one clear focal point. But the knot crowds the top edge at 0:00.0 (strip_0.0-0.6 frame 1), and at 0:01.0–0:01.5 the knot sits on top of the small cube and icosahedron in a tangled cluster (beats_grid1 row 2, left).
- **Data accuracy N/A**: no figures.
- **Sound sync 6**: the recede starting at 4.0 lands well on "ich" (strip_3.9-4.4), and the fade matches the audio fade at 7.0–8.0. But the stressed "atmen" at 2.50 gets only a faint pale tint on the knot (f_2.5 vs f_2.58), the downbeats at 1.53 and 3.53 show no event, and there is no per-beat motion anywhere despite a thump every 0.5 s.
- **Overall 6**: clean, pleasant and on-model. It plays like a polished shader test, not something a maker would ship as a song moment.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:02.5**: the key word "atmen" (breathe) has no visible picture event. The scene from 0:01.5 to 0:03.9 is near-frozen. A breath (a swell of the knot or of the ring of blocks) is the obvious missing hit.
2. **0:05.0–0:07.0**: the knot is alone on a bare dome, only rotating and scaling. Two seconds pass with nothing new and no beat response while a 120 bpm thump plays (beats_grid1 last frame, beats_grid2 first two).
3. **0:04.3–0:04.6**: the receding blocks shrink to 1–3 px dark specks on the horizon before vanishing (strip_3.9-4.4 row 3, frames 1–2). They read as dust or dead pixels, not a deliberate exit.

## 4. Off or cheap at full size
- 0:02.5–0:02.8: a milky, pale-green blotch with stray dark marks appears on the top loop of the knot (strip_2.3-2.8 row 3). It looks like a shading glitch rather than a light.
- 0:05.9 (full_t5.9): hard contour banding stripes run along the tube. That reads differently from the smooth tube at 0:00.0, so the knot looks like it changed material mid-film.
- 0:00.0: the knot outline nearly touches the frame top.
- The near-uniform grey-beige palette in the last second (strip_7.0-7.9) is fine as a fade.
- No type, people or hands appear.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN**: hook 7, motion 7, variety 6, composition 7, sound sync 6 and overall 6 are all below 8.
