# Review, round 2 (8 s abstract test piece, 16:9)

## 1. Retold
A teal knot hangs over a beige floor. Coloured solids rain in and ring it. The floor blushes orange on "atmen". The ring collapses away on "ich". The knot, now alone, swells and tumbles on the beat, then fades to a blank grey-white frame.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 7.** The big knot in frame 0 grabs attention, and solids drop in by 0.6. But the knot shrinks away from the viewer in the first 0.5 s (full_t0, strip_0.0-0.6), so the opening loses energy.
- **Readability at 360 px: 8.** No type. Every shape reads at phone width (phone_360px). The knot is small but clear from 1.5 to 4.0.
- **Motion quality: 7.** Smooth, consistent rotation across all strips. The 4.0 exit is the exception: it happens in about 3 frames (strip_3.9-4.4, 3.96–4.12) and looks like a pop, not a move.
- **Variety: 7.** Something new arrives at 0.6, 2.6, 4.0, 5.0 and 7.0. From 1.0 to 2.5 and from 3.0 to 3.9 the layout does not change, and the only beat cue is a faint floor pulse (beats_grid1, cells 1.5–2.04 and 3.0–4.0).
- **Composition: 7.** There is one focal point and nothing is cut by an edge. From 1.5 to 4.0 the knot fills only about 12 % of the frame width inside a busy ring, and the horizon line runs straight through it (beats_grid1).
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 8.** Picture hits land one frame after the beats (1.58, 2.08, 3.58, 5.54, 6.04, 6.54, 7.04). The orange flush starts at 2.54 on "atmen". The collapse lands on "ich" at 3.96–4.08. There is no hit on the 4.5 beat (4.1–5.0 is a slow swell).
- **Overall: 6.** The piece has a coherent look and is musical, but the second-half shading change and the glitchy exit put it below what makers ship.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:04.0 (3.96–4.12).** The solids do not shrink or fly away. They squash into crumpled, open-sided shards: the cube turns into a flat triangle, and a fold is visible in the orange icosahedron (frame at 4.04). On screen this looks like a render bug.
2. **0:04.3 onward (full_t5.0, frame 6.04).** The knot's surface switches from soft shading to hard banded stripes with dark purple seam lines. The look changes mid-film for no visible reason, and the seams look like mesh edges.
3. **0:07.4–0:08.0 (strip_7.0-7.9).** Sky and floor wash to the same grey while the knot dissolves, and the last frames (7.92 onward) are blank. The film ends on nothing instead of a final image.

## 4. Off at full size
- Grain noise covers every surface. It is pleasant on the paper background, but on the knot it reads as dirty texture.
- The outline weight changes between the first half (thin, dark) and the second (thin purple seams inside the shape).
- The sky shifts from warm beige to cool grey after 6.5 s, and nothing on screen motivates the change.
- Locked items: none hurt the film.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Five axes score 7 or below.
