# Review, round 2: 8 s abstract test piece

## 1. Retold
Colored cubes, polyhedra and knots float over a curved paper-tan floor and jostle to the beat. Then the camera pushes in on the teal knot, which turns slowly and fades to grey. I could follow it, but nothing tells me why the teal knot is the one that matters.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 6.** It opens on a washed-out, near-white fade-in (full_t0, 0.0–0.5 s), then six shapes sit almost still. Nothing in the first 2 s grabs attention.
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** The color blocks read well (phone_360px), but the two knots are tiny until 4.5 s. The last tile is empty grey.
- **Motion quality: 6.** Every frame is held twice, so the motion steps at 12 fps (pairs of identical frames in strip_2.3-2.8; the frame difference is exactly 0 on every other frame). The push-in at 4.0–5.0 judders as a result.
- **Variety: 6.** From 0.5 to 4.0 s the wide layout stays the same and only jostles. From 5.0 to 7.0 s the single knot just turns, with no new event for about 2.5 s (sheet t=5, 6).
- **Composition: 6.** From 0.5 to 4.0 s there is no focal point: six objects carry equal weight. The purple cube is cut by the left edge from 3.0 to 4.9 s (beats grid, frames 4–5). The orange icosahedron crosses the bottom edge at 4.4 s. No drawn type.
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures.

1. **0:07.5–0:08.0:** the picture is flat, empty grey (f_7.52) while the audio is still fading out until 8.0. The downbeat at 7.51 falls on nothing, and the film ends on dead air instead of an image.
