## 2. Scores
- **Hook: 6.** At 0:00 the knot is large and turning, but for about 1.5 s the only thing happening is a slow pull-back (strip_0.0-1.1). There is no hit.
- **Readability at 360 px: 7.** The knot reads in every frame. From about 2.5 s on, the satellites are only a few pixels wide and the flat cut-outs at 4–5 s almost vanish (phone_360px, row 3).
- **Motion quality: 6.** Rotation is smooth, but the solids snap flat in about 2 frames at 3.95–4.0, and almost nothing moves from 4.1 to 5.3 (strip_3.9-5.2).
- **Variety: 7.** The beats are pull-back, ring, flash, flatten, rebuild and levitate. That is a fair rate of change, but the 4.1–5.3 span shows nothing new.
- **Composition: 7.** There is one clear focal point, centred, with no type. At 0:00 the knot nearly touches the top edge and covers two satellites (full_t0). After 2.5 s the cluster fills only the middle third, and the rest is empty paper.
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 7.** The warm flash peaks on "atmen" at 2.5 and the solids flatten on "ich" at 4.0. Rings land on 1.53, 2.04, 5.53 and 7.51. The beats at 4.6 and 5.0 get no visible hit (beats_grid2, frames 1–3).
- **Overall: 6.** It is a clean, coherent sketch. It is not yet at the level of a shipped clip: the middle is dead and the shadows look cheap.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:04.0–0:05.3.** The flattened section stalls. For about 1.3 s the cut-outs barely move. The knot reads as a squashed, melted blob rather than a knot (full_t4.6), and two pulse beats pass with no picture response.
2. **0:07.4 (also 0:01.0 and 0:06.5).** The shadow discs are hard-outlined, flat beige ellipses that do not shrink or soften as the solids rise. Objects float well above empty ovals that look like holes or stickers (pulled frame f_7.4). On the orange flash at 2.5 the discs stay grey-beige and look pasted on (full_t2.5).
3. **0:00.0–0:01.5.** The opening is weak. Nothing hits until the first ring at about 1.5 s. The first frame is crowded: the knot covers the orange and yellow solids and nearly touches the top edge (full_t0).

## 4. Off, cheap or wrong at full size
- Ring outlines double into two thin concentric lines at about 3.1 s (strip_1.4-2.9, frame 7) and about 5.9 s (strip_5.4-6.1, frame 8). It looks like misregistration, not intent.
- The 3D-to-flat swap at 3.97–4.0 pops rather than morphs.
- The ending has no visual resolution while the audio fades from 7.0 to 8.0. The last frame is just a calmer copy of the opening.
- No edge seams or compression artefacts were seen.
- Locked: no words are drawn, so muted viewers depend on the .srt.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Hook, motion, variety, composition and sound sync are all below 8.
