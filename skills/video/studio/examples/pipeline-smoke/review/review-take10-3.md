- **Variety: 7.** New ideas arrive at 2.5, 4.0 and 5.5 s. After that, 6.3–7.95 s brings nothing new, only the same orbit (rev10tmp/tail.png, sheet frames 6–7).
- **Composition: 7.** The knot is a clear single focal point. But the ring and blocks are cut by the bottom edge at about 4.7 s (strip_3.9-5.2, row 3), the cluster clumps and overlaps at 2.0–2.5 s (full_t2.5), and a ghost ring crosses the frame edges at 5.2 s and 6.1 s.
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.

The small per-beat brightness dips land about 0.1 s (2–3 frames) late: 1.62, 2.12, 3.62, 4.12. The 7.51 downbeat gets no visible event.
- **Overall: 7.** Tidy and well timed, but it ends in a holding pattern and has edge and clutter slips that a shipped clip would not have.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:06.3–0:07.9.** The ring dissolves, and then nothing new happens for 1.6 s while the audio fades. There is no closing gesture and nothing on the 7.51 downbeat, so the film stops rather than ends.
2. **0:04.6–0:04.8.** The expanding ring runs past the bottom and left edges. The yellow square and orange hexagon sit about 30 px from the bottom edge (rev10tmp/f_4.6.png, strip_3.9-5.2 row 3 col 1). The film's key image is cropped.
3. **0:02.0–0:02.5.** The pull-in crushes everything into a tight clump. The purple and yellow blocks overlap, and the knot sits on top of the orange and yellow blocks behind it (full_t2.5). The "atmen" moment begins on clutter instead of a clear silhouette.

## 4. Off at full size

- A small white speck or gap shows in the knot's top tube at 2.5 s (full_t2.5, about x505 y155) and again at 4.6 s.
- Faint double "ghost" rings at 4.9, 5.2 and 6.1 s look like a leftover trail, not a design choice.
- The paper grain and vignette are clean, with no banding or seams.
- Locked: the song gives only two short lines and a steady pulse, so the picture carries all the variety itself.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Hook, variety, composition and overall all score below 8.
