# Review, round 3: 8 s abstract test piece

## 1. Retold
A teal knot turns on a beige paper horizon. Coloured blocks (cubes, octahedra, icosahedra) drop in and circle it, the floor glows warm for a moment, then on a hit the blocks fly off. The knot stays on its own, swells, rolls over, and fades to near-white. I could follow all of it. There is no story to miss.

## 2. Scores
- **Hook (first 2 s): 7.** The knot reads at once (full_t0), but it barely moves for 0.2 s and is still again from 0.8 to 1.05 s (sheet_1fps, frame diff). Nothing in the opening is a surprise.
- **Readability at 360 px: 8.** One clear silhouette in every phone_360px frame. Only the last frame, which is fading out on purpose, washes out.
- **Motion quality: 6.** The rotation and the settle are smooth (strip_5.4-6.1, strip_2.3-2.9). The scatter at 4.00 to 4.10 s takes about 2 frames: in the 4.04 frame the blocks are cut off at the right edge, and by 4.08 they have shrunk to small specks. It reads as a pop, not as flight (strip_3.9-4.4).
- **Variety: 7.** The first half changes about every 1 to 1.5 s. After 4.1 s it is one knot on an empty floor for 3.9 s, and from 4.1 to 4.55 almost nothing moves (frame diff about 0.6).
- **Composition: 7.** There is one focal point and it stays centred, but the knot's top always sits exactly on the horizon line, which runs through it (full_t4.3, beats_grid2). Blocks are clipped by the frame edge at 4.04. Two specks stay near the top from 4.15 to 4.3 and look like dust (frame pulled at 4.2).
- **Data accuracy: N/A.** No figures appear.
- **Sound sync: 8.** Small kicks land 1 frame after each 0.5 s beat (1.58, 2.08, 3.08, 3.58, 5.58 …). The scatter hits "ich" at 4.00 exactly, and the fade matches the audio fade from 7.0. On "atmen" (2.50) the warm wash starts on time but peaks at about 2.71, so it reads soft. Downbeats get no more weight than the other beats, except at 5.58.
- **Overall: 7.** It is clean and coherent, but it reads as a capable render test rather than a shipped music loop. The second half is thin.

## 3. Three worst problems
1. **0:04.0 to 0:04.1:** The exit lasts only about 2 frames. Blocks are cut off at the right edge, then shrink to specks that hang around until about 4.3. The film's biggest moment looks like a glitch.
2. **0:04.1 to 0:05.5:** The knot is alone and nearly static on a bare floor, with no beat response from 4.1 to 4.55. The film loses its energy right after its peak.
3. **0:07.0 to 0:08.0:** The fade ends on a ghostly grey-green knot on a near-white field (full_t7.95). There is no final image or resolving pose, so the ending feels like the film running out rather than an ending.

## 4. Off at full size
- The horizon is a thin hard black line on a soft paper texture, and it looks like a CAD guide line.
- The knot's grain is noisy where its colour is flat, around 4.2 to 4.3.
- The warm wash at 2.6 to 2.8 tints the whole lower half evenly, so it can read as an exposure flicker rather than a deliberate light.
- There are no people, faces or type to check.

## 5. Verdict
**RETURN.** Motion quality is 6, and hook, variety, composition and overall are each 7.
