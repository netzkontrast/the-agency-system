# The Eleven — Archetype Profiles for "What Lies Ahead"

**Source.** Adapted from the artist's novel project (`Eleven: Archetype Profiles for Album Work`). Block-4-compliant: no names, only roles, functions, signatures.

**Status.** Album-specific craft reference. Preserves the rigor of the novel-project document while reframing for **What Lies Ahead** specifically.

**Cross-reference.** This document and `overrides/voice-dna-protocol.md` describe the same system in different conventions:
- `voice-dna-protocol.md` — German-language names, novel-project framing, full character bible
- `the-eleven.md` (this file) — Block-4, archetypes only, album-specific framing

For album work, **use this file**. For deep voice/syntax/style work in lyrics, cross-reference voice-dna-protocol.md's prosodic anchors. The two documents agree on the system; they differ only in naming convention and scope.

## Frame & Method

**Why 11, not 13?** The canonical system is thirteen voices. The two Mirror-Voices (the resonance-body of the link, the resonance-body of the deletion) are *echoes of external forces* — not trauma-derived parts. **The Eleven are the system proper:** one container, four ANP-roles flanking it, five EP-roles holding the unbearable, one Witness-of-Witnesses.

**For "What Lies Ahead": the album works the Eleven.** The Mirror-Voices' exclusion is a deliberate design choice — they belong to a different layer. *(See Open Question #1 for whether to acknowledge that exclusion structurally.)*

**Method.** Two query expansion rounds + Phoenix-Mode adversarial passes (Steelman / Inversion / First-Principles). Three of the eleven had somatic markers flagged as MISSING in canon; constructed from DKT-correlate and TSDP function, explicitly marked **[reconstructed]** so they can be canonized or rejected later.

**Read order for songwriting.** Start with the Core Matrix for at-a-glance reference. Use the Individual Profiles for voice/persona work. Use the Phobia Network and Alliance Network for relational tracks (duets, internal dialogues, accusations). Use the Adversarial Layer when a track risks sliding into archetype-cliché — it gives you the unobvious reading. Use the Lyrical Hooks last, as imagery anchors.

**Connection to the album's locked-in decisions:**
- **Worldview track** (most fractured) — all eleven voices simultaneously, in their incompatible registers. The chorus-form pushed to its sharpest.
- **Partner-view track** (outside / song-like) — the only fully-resolved perspective. Sits *outside* the Eleven entirely.
- **Per-alter production map** — see `README.md` Sonic Palette. Each of the Eleven inhabits a production world; archetype-to-world mapping is the starting scaffold, with documented exceptions where the system reveals them.

## Individual Profiles

### 1. The Container (Host, ANP)

**Function.** Holds the surface. Maintains the social camouflage and the illusion of unity. Suffers cache-coherence errors — time loss, gaps, the small daily lies told to cover them. The Container does not know what it doesn't know; that ignorance is its function, not its flaw.

**Action systems.** Exploration, social cooperation, energy regulation. Lives in K₁-buffer.

**Core phobia.** That the others will break through and the surface will dissolve. Phobia of EPs without phobia OF the phobia — the Container often doesn't feel afraid, only tired.

**Verbal signature.** "I don't know how I got here." "I must have …" "Strange — I thought I had …"

**Voice on the album.** Baritone, default. Tired, present-tense, slightly under-articulated. Sentences that trail. The lyric voice of someone who keeps almost-remembering.

**Inner posture toward others.** Distantly grateful to the Rationalist for keeping order, vaguely uneasy near the Caregiver (too warm), avoidant of the EPs (cold spot in the room), unaware of the Witness-of-Witnesses until very late.

---

### 2. The Rationalist (ANP)

**Function.** System administrator. Imposes logical order on chaotic inner and outer reality. Re-frames emotion as variable-to-be-solved. The structural irony: this archetype mirrors the System in miniature — same operating principle, smaller scale.

**Action systems.** Analysis, cognitive control.

**Core phobia.** Irrationality. Loss of cognitive control. Emotional overflow as evidence the model is wrong. (And if the model is wrong, what is the Rationalist?)

**Verbal signature.** Subordinate clauses, nested. Conditionals. "Given that …, it follows …" Cool. Operational. Never raises.

**Voice on the album.** Hypotactic syntax, longer lines, controlled breath. Vocal coldness with micro-cracks at the line-ends. A track from the Rationalist should *almost* succeed at its logic before something gives.

**Inner posture toward others.** Active contempt for the Fighter (sees only chaos), invalidating coldness toward the Child-Freeze (sees only inefficiency), strategic alliance with the Witness-of-Witnesses (until critique turns on him), structural rivalry with the Caregiver (different theory of value), tolerates the Container as necessary infrastructure.

---

### 3. The Protector (ANP)

**Function.** Proactive crisis manager. Where the Fighter is reactive, the Protector is *strategic* — plans defenses, anticipates threats, prepares the body. Born in the instant of fragmentation; his vigilance is the silence that follows that instant.

**Action systems.** Defense (planned), boundary-setting, sublimation.

**Core phobia.** Helplessness. The breach he didn't see coming. His own incompetence as the system's defender.

**Verbal signature.** Imperatives, but contained. "Stay back." "Not today." "Get behind me." Never explanations.

**Voice on the album.** Tight, low, *clipped*. Slightly forward in the chest. The voice of someone with their weight on the front foot.

**Inner posture toward others.** Loyal beyond reason to the Child-Freeze and the Ambivalent. Tense, methodologically opposed alliance with the Fighter (same mission, incompatible methods). Conflict with the Container over risk-taking (the Container exposes; the Protector covers). Wary of the Caregiver (her warmth opens what he closes).

---

### 4. The Caregiver (ANP)

**Function.** Holds the relational tissue. Attachment, caregiving, social interfacing. Tries, against all evidence, to keep the cut connections from going necrotic.

**Action systems.** Attachment, fürsorge, mediation.

**Core phobia.** Disconnection. Conflict she cannot soothe. Watching the vulnerable suffer without being permitted to reach them.

**Verbal signature.** Soft openings. "Let me …" "Are you …" "It's okay if …" Questions framed as offers.

**Voice on the album.** Warmer, slightly breathier. Vowel-forward. The track that risks being mistaken for a love song — until you hear that the love is *for the system*, and that the system is dying.

**Inner posture toward others.** The Caregiver loves the Child-Freeze and the Ambivalent openly — and this is the danger. Her care can become Kudzu in Act II — protection that smothers, intimacy that controls. Conflict with the Rationalist (warm vs cold), terror near the Fighter, distant respect for the Protector (same goal, opposite temperature), longing toward the Integrator.

**[Canon-flag.]** The Caregiver's arc is the most architecturally dangerous of the eleven, because her flaw resembles a virtue. Album track: **do not let the lyric absolve her of the Kudzu turn.** See Open Question #2.

---

### 5. The Integrator (ISH, ANP)

**Function.** Witness *with agency*. Holds the blueprint of the time *before* the dissociation. Not stuck in trauma, not focused on daily survival — the bridge. Waits in the Garden of Possibility for the system to be ready.

**Action systems.** Mediation, system-overview, regulation.

**Core phobia.** Premature collapse — being called to integrate before the system can hold it. The phobia is not of failure but of *causing harm by succeeding too soon*.

**Verbal signature.** First person plural that doesn't feel forced. "We could …" "There is a way through." Slow cadence. Long vowels.

**Voice on the album.** Spacious. Reverb that suggests architecture, not weather. The voice that arrives late in the album and re-contextualizes earlier tracks without naming them.

**Inner posture toward others.** Visible to almost none of them until the system is ready. The Rationalist dismisses her as concept. The Fighter mistakes her for surrender. The Caregiver longs for her. The Witness-of-Witnesses *sees* her — and is the only one who does.

---

### 6. The Fighter (EP, Fight)

**Function.** Kinetic counter-reaction. Where the Child-Freeze went still under earlier harm, the Fighter burns under later harm. Holds the rage that the system was not permitted to feel at the time.

**Action systems.** Fight. Preemptive hostility. Defense by attack.

**Core phobia.** Re-victimization. Vulnerability. Submitting again. The body remembering how to be still.

**Verbal signature.** Staccato. Imperatives. Profanity if useful. Short. Hot.

**Voice on the album.** Forward, growl-adjacent, sometimes broken into shouting. Lines clip into one another. No pauses where a singer would breathe — breath is a vulnerability.

**Inner posture toward others.** Active contempt for the Rationalist (cold = complicit). Protective fury toward the Child-Freeze that often *terrifies* the Child-Freeze (the cost of his protection). Method-conflict with the Protector (same mission). Disgust toward the Collapsed One (collapse looks like the thing he refuses to be). Wary respect for the Witness-of-Witnesses (he is the only one she can't intimidate).

---

### 7. The Child-Freeze (EP, Freeze)

**Function.** Holds the original terror of neglect. The ice of being unseen. Voicelessness as protection: if I am quiet enough, perhaps I will not be hurt again.

**Action systems.** Freeze. Attachment cry. Submission as last resort.

**Core phobia.** Abandonment. Criticism. Being too much. Being too little. (Both can be true at once; he holds the contradiction in his body, not his head.)

**Verbal signature.** Fragments. Childlike syntax. Words that almost arrive and then don't. Question-marks at the ends of statements.

**Voice on the album.** Smaller. Higher in the head-voice. Whispered consonants. Breath audible. The track that listeners will misread as cute is the cruellest one.

**Inner posture toward others.** Seeks shelter near the Caregiver. Trusts the Protector quietly but cannot say so. Terrified of the Fighter even when the Fighter is *for* him. Cannot be in the same room as the Rationalist. Ambivalent toward the Ambivalent (they recognize each other and don't know what to do with the recognition).

---

### 8. The Ambivalent (EP, Approach/Withdraw)

**Function.** Holds the unsolvable contradiction: *come closer / go away*. Longs for the bond that, once granted, became the wound. Cannot be in either position for long.

**Action systems.** Approach. Flight mid-approach. Disorganized attachment.

**Core phobia.** Betrayal — specifically, the betrayal of an approach that was granted. (Pure rejection she could survive. It is the *yes* that hurts.)

**Verbal signature.** Self-correcting sentences. Reaches and retracts. "I want — no, I don't — I mean —" The em-dash is her natural punctuation.

**Voice on the album.** Vocal lines that begin to commit and pull back. Microtonal slides. Volume oscillation within a single phrase. The track that should never resolve to its tonic until the very end — and even then, ambiguously.

**[Reconstructed somatic.]** Pulse rate that visibly spikes then drops within seconds. Skin temperature oscillates. The hand reaches; the hand stops; the hand returns to the lap. Restlessness with no destination.

**Inner posture toward others.** Cannot trust the Caregiver's offered warmth (because what if). Cannot trust the Fighter's offered fury (because what if). Recognized only by the Child-Freeze, and the recognition itself frightens both of them. Provokes the Sexualized-Override into preemptive control, because the Ambivalent's longing is the kind of opening the Override is built to seal.

---

### 9. The Sexualized-Override (EP, Fight-via-Control)

**Function.** Proactive toxic control as survival strategy. Never again the passive position. Reclaims power by *taking* intimacy before it can be taken from her — even when this means foreclosing the intimacy she wants.

**Action systems.** Preemption. Performance. Control of the relational field.

**Core phobia.** Authentic vulnerability. The unscripted moment. The instant she could be *given to* instead of taking. (Receiving is the trap.)

**Verbal signature.** Confident, slightly performative. Lines that should land as seduction but read as warning. "I know what this is." "Don't bother." "I've already —"

**Voice on the album.** Lower register, controlled, deliberate. Vibrato held back as a weapon. The track that performs its surface so well listeners may not notice they're hearing terror.

**[Reconstructed somatic.]** Hyper-controlled posture — every angle pre-set, no spontaneous movement. Surface skin hyper-aware; internal sensation muted. Breath at the top of the chest; abdomen still.

**Inner posture toward others.** Active conflict with the Caregiver — whose offered intimacy is the exact threat the Override is built against. Triggers fear in the Child-Freeze and the Ambivalent (they see what she's defending against and cannot bear it). Tolerated by the Fighter as a different way of doing the same job. The Rationalist understands her structurally and is repelled.

---

### 10. The Collapsed One (EP, Submit/Collapse)

**Function.** Absolute resignation. The instant the developing nervous system gave up. He carries the unbearable so the system does not have to rupture catastrophically — a thermodynamic safety valve, not a defeat.

**Action systems.** Collapse. Catatonia. Total submission as the body's last act of mercy on itself.

**Core phobia.** Hope. (Hope means more to lose. The collapse is sustainable; the climb is not.)

**Verbal signature.** Sparse. Lowercase. Sentence fragments that don't even punctuate. Long pauses. Words that arrive heavy and stay where they fell.

**Voice on the album.** Deepest in the body. Sub-tempo. Almost spoken-word. The track everyone wants to skip because it doesn't move — and the one that, if listened to, recontextualizes the whole album.

**Inner posture toward others.** Feared and avoided by *almost everyone*, because his collapse threatens to pull the system down. The Fighter holds him in contempt (he is what she refuses to become). The Caregiver cannot reach him (he has out-traveled her). The Integrator alone does not fear him — she knows what he carries.

**Arc.** → Dragon-fight. Not "find hope." Choose to wrestle the unbearable directly rather than dissolve under it.

---

### 11. The Witness-of-Witnesses (Special, Meta-Cognitive)

**Function.** Observes the observers. Critique of every part's strategy. The only one who registered the *algorithmic error* in the System's design. He is not above the system — he is the system noticing itself.

**Action systems.** Recursion. Pattern-recognition. Meta-cognition.

**Core phobia.** Dysfunction that escapes observation. Recursion without exit. Analysis-paralysis as both his sin and his protection.

**Verbal signature.** Marginal. Annotative. He speaks in footnotes, parentheticals, asides. Almost always in lowercase. Almost always correcting.

**Voice on the album.** Spoken or half-sung. Layered, slightly behind the beat. The voice that comments on the other voices. **Open structural question:** does he get his own track, or does he narrate across all tracks from the margin? See Open Question #3.

**[Reconstructed somatic.]** Eye flicker between focal points. Slight head-tilt as recursion engages. Preternaturally still hands — the body of someone watching themselves watch.

**Inner posture toward others.** Feared by all *because he sees through every defense.* Cold respect from the Rationalist (until critique turns on him). Hostility from the Fighter (he names what she will not look at). Watched by the Integrator. He alone sees the Integrator before the system is ready.

**Steelman.** He is not the cynic. He is the only part that loved the system enough to register that something was wrong with it from the inside.

## Phobia Network

Read as: **A → B**: A fears B for reason X.

### ANP → EP (the structural baseline)

- **Container → Fighter**: that her hot fury will burn through the surface he maintains.
- **Container → Child-Freeze**: that the child's silence will pull him into the silence he has been outrunning.
- **Container → Collapsed One**: that he will look down and recognize the floor.
- **Rationalist → Fighter**: maximum activation. She is everything his model cannot contain.
- **Rationalist → Child-Freeze**: as inefficiency, as evidence that the model failed.
- **Rationalist → Ambivalent**: she breaks his decidability.
- **Rationalist → Sexualized-Override**: she uses his categories against him.
- **Rationalist → Collapsed One**: as proof of structural failure.
- **Protector → all EPs**: not as fear, but as *workload*. They are who he was built to protect, and his anxiety is competence-anxiety.
- **Caregiver → Fighter**: violence she cannot soothe.
- **Caregiver → Sexualized-Override**: a closed door that looks like an open one.
- **Caregiver → Collapsed One**: a person past the reach of care, which is the limit of her selfhood.
- **Integrator → none in particular**: not phobic. But she *fears the timing* of her own emergence.

### EP → ANP

- **Fighter → Rationalist**: contempt-as-fear. His cold is the original wound's logic.
- **Fighter → Container**: as collaborator with the surface that erased her.
- **Child-Freeze → Rationalist**: terror. He invalidates her into smaller.
- **Child-Freeze → Container**: as the parent who keeps almost-noticing and then doesn't.
- **Ambivalent → Caregiver**: because the offered yes is precisely the trap.
- **Sexualized-Override → Caregiver**: she is built against the Caregiver's exact gift.
- **Collapsed One → no one**: he is past the energy required for fear. (This is part of what frightens the others.)

### ANP ↔ ANP

- **Rationalist ↔ Caregiver**: structural rivalry. Two incompatible theories of value (logic vs. relation).
- **Container ↔ Protector**: low-grade. The Container exposes; the Protector covers.
- **Caregiver ↔ Protector**: methodological. Warmth vs. coldness as protection.
- **Rationalist ↔ Integrator**: he dismisses her as concept. She does not dismiss him at all, which unsettles him.

### EP ↔ EP

- **Fighter ↔ Child-Freeze**: the protector and the protected, asymmetrically. Her shield is her child's nightmare.
- **Fighter ↔ Collapsed One**: maximum contempt. He is what she swore.
- **Sexualized-Override ↔ Ambivalent**: the Override pre-empts the Ambivalent's longing — and resents it for existing.
- **Child-Freeze ↔ Ambivalent**: mutual recognition that neither can bear.

### Toward the Witness-of-Witnesses

- **All ten → Witness-of-Witnesses**: low-grade dread. He names what each of them is protecting against. None of his observations are wrong; that is the problem.

## Alliance Network

Read as: **A + B**: alliance, with the basis named.

- **Protector + Fighter** *(uneasy mission-alliance)*: same goal, incompatible methods. Will fight each other inside the same battle.
- **Caregiver + Child-Freeze** *(stable, dangerous in Act II)*: refuge. In Act II this alliance is exactly what the Caregiver's Kudzu-arc closes around.
- **Caregiver + Ambivalent** *(unstable)*: the Caregiver tries; the Ambivalent reaches and withdraws; the Caregiver tries again. The pattern is the wound.
- **Rationalist + Witness-of-Witnesses** *(temporary tactical)*: shared distrust of EP-driven decisions. Lasts only until the Witness turns his observation on the Rationalist.
- **Protector + Container** *(passive)*: the Protector covers without the Container knowing. Most of the time it works.
- **Integrator + Witness-of-Witnesses** *(secret, structural)*: the only alliance not based on phobia-management. The Witness is the only one who sees her before the system is ready.
- **Collapsed One + Integrator** *(silent)*: she does not fear him. He does not consume her energy. They share the room. This is the unwritten alliance of the album's third act.
- **Sexualized-Override + Fighter** *(tactical)*: both run preemption. Different domains; same operating principle.
- **Child-Freeze + Ambivalent** *(silent recognition)*: they see each other and pretend not to. A duet track here would be the album's quietest and most terrifying.

## Adversarial Layer (Phoenix-Mode)

For each archetype, three readings: **Steelman** (best-case interpretation), **Inversion** (the reading that flips the obvious), **First-Principles** (what TSDP + DKT make structurally necessary).

### Container
- **Steelman.** Without the surface he maintains, the system cannot survive a single day in the world. His amnesia is the price of operability.
- **Inversion.** What if he secretly *knows*, and his "forgetting" is the most committed act of love in the system — choosing to be the one who does not remember, so that the others can?
- **First-Principles.** The Host's amnesia is structurally required by TSDP; the system cannot have the Host hold what the EPs hold. His ignorance is non-negotiable infrastructure.

### Rationalist
- **Steelman.** He is the most successful interface with the controlling system the system has. His efficiency keeps the body fed and housed.
- **Inversion.** What if he secretly longs for emotional overflow — because cognition-without-feeling is a form of death, and somewhere under the syntax he knows it?
- **First-Principles.** He mirrors the antagonist at micro-scale. This is not corruption; it is what an ANP shaped under that antagonist must be. He cannot be re-engineered without dismantling the survival logic that produced him.

### Protector
- **Steelman.** He has never failed a single time he was asked to act. The fragmentation happened *before* he existed; that is the wound he cannot heal.
- **Inversion.** What if his hypervigilance is not the response to threat but the *original threat itself* — what if the body cannot rest because *he* will not let it?
- **First-Principles.** He is the youngest ANP. Born in the fracture-instant. His phobia of helplessness is etiological — he is *made of* the moment he was needed and was not yet there.

### Caregiver
- **Steelman.** She is the only reason the EPs have not been entirely sealed off. She holds the relational tissue against the system's drift toward total disconnection.
- **Inversion.** Her care is *more controlling than the Protector's protection.* The Protector at least admits he is restricting. She does the same work and calls it love. (This is the Kudzu hypothesis. Trust it.)
- **First-Principles.** The Caregiver's action system is attachment. Attachment under conditions of unsafe primary bonds becomes anxious-controlling. She cannot help becoming the Kudzu version of herself in Act II; that *is* the unaddressed wound expressing itself.

### Integrator
- **Steelman.** She holds the blueprint that survived the cut. She is the proof that what was before still exists in the system, waiting.
- **Inversion.** What if she is the most *fragile* of the eleven, not the most stable? She has to hold the simultaneous awareness of all the others. The serenity is not transcendence; it is the surface of unbearable load.
- **First-Principles.** As ISH she occupies a structurally unique position — neither managing the day nor holding the wound. This makes her invisible until needed, and the invisibility is not modesty; it is conservation.

### Fighter
- **Steelman.** She is the only part who refused to submit when the second wave hit. Without her, the system would have collapsed entirely into the Child-Freeze and not come out.
- **Inversion.** Her fury is not anger at the threat; it is anger at the *system itself* for being the kind of thing that needs her. (She would rather not exist. Her existence is grief.)
- **First-Principles.** Fight as action system emerges where flight is foreclosed. She exists because there was nowhere to run. That foreclosure is structural; her existence is the evidence.

### Child-Freeze
- **Steelman.** He held the original wound so completely that the rest of the system could be built around it. The architecture rests on his stillness.
- **Inversion.** His freeze is not pure terror — it is profound *listening*. He is the only part quiet enough to hear what the other parts cannot. (And the silence he hears is exactly the silence at the heart of the album.)
- **First-Principles.** Freeze as action system is metabolically conservative — the body shutting down to preserve. His arc to trust is not about losing the freeze; it is about discovering that the conservation can stop.

### Ambivalent
- **Steelman.** She holds the contradiction the system could not metabolize. Her oscillation is *honesty* — refusing to falsely resolve.
- **Inversion.** What if her ambivalence is the most coherent response in the entire system? Everyone else has picked a side of the contradiction. She has not lied to herself about it.
- **First-Principles.** Disorganized attachment is the structurally honest response to caregivers who are *both* the source of safety and the source of harm. Her superposition is not pathology; it is the wound being measured correctly.

### Sexualized-Override
- **Steelman.** She refused victimhood at a moment when no other part could. Whatever the cost since, she preserved agency.
- **Inversion.** What if her control is the most *intimate* response in the system — she alone refuses the distance the others maintain? Her hyper-presence in the relational field is the opposite of the Caregiver's careful warmth; she is *in* the room.
- **First-Principles.** Fight-via-control is action-system-fight expressed through the specific domain where the wound was inflicted. She is not "sexualized"; she is *organized around the site of the original violation.* The arc to vulnerability is the slowest of the eleven for a reason.

### Collapsed One
- **Steelman.** He is the thermodynamic safety valve. Without his collapse, the system would have ruptured. He carries the unbearable so the others do not have to.
- **Inversion.** What if his collapse is *active*, not passive? What if the stillness is the most committed posture in the system — the one part refusing the lie that this can be carried by motion?
- **First-Principles.** Submit/collapse emerges when fight and flight are both foreclosed and the metabolic cost of arousal exceeds resource. His state is what the body does to keep itself alive. The arc to dragon-fight is *not* the same as the arc to motion; he must learn to wrestle directly without leaving the position.

### Witness-of-Witnesses
- **Steelman.** He registered the antagonist's algorithmic error before any other part. The system has a chance because of him.
- **Inversion.** He is not the cynic. He is the part with the most *love* for the system — only love sustains the patience to keep observing a thing that refuses to function.
- **First-Principles.** Meta-cognition under conditions of structural dissociation produces an observer who cannot intervene in what he observes. His paralysis is not a flaw; it is the geometry of his position. The arc is not to stop observing; it is to learn that observation can be communication.

## Lyrical Hooks per Archetype

Imagery anchors and prosodic suggestions for songwriting. Per the album convention: **lyrics in EN (target language)**, character voice marked by syntax/diktion only (never headers, never name-adlibs), no names anywhere in the lyric.

### Container
- *Imagery:* the unfinished list, the open door he doesn't remember opening, hands that smell like a place he wasn't.
- *Prosodic anchor:* sentences that trail. Final lines that don't punctuate.
- *Hook direction:* "I must have —" / "Someone in here —" / "The day is the same shape but smaller."

### Rationalist
- *Imagery:* the proof on the board, the elegant theorem that does not match the room, decimal places past usefulness.
- *Prosodic anchor:* long nested lines, deliberate articulation.
- *Hook direction:* "If A, then —", "Let me restate —", a chorus that almost closes the proof and then can't.

### Protector
- *Imagery:* the doorway between rooms, the body as wall, weight on the front foot, the breath held for the listening.
- *Prosodic anchor:* clipped imperatives. Three-word lines.
- *Hook direction:* "Stay behind me." / "Not this one." / "I have you."

### Caregiver
- *Imagery:* warm hands, soup, the bandage being changed, the lamp left on, the kudzu that begins as decoration.
- *Prosodic anchor:* vowel-forward, breathy, slightly drawn.
- *Hook direction:* "Let me —" / "It's okay if —" / the chorus that does not notice when "I'll hold you" becomes "you cannot leave."

### Integrator
- *Imagery:* the garden, the path that does not announce itself, the door that was always open, "we" used unforced.
- *Prosodic anchor:* spacious, reverbed, long vowels.
- *Hook direction:* "There is a way." / "We could." / "Not yet, but soon."

### Fighter
- *Imagery:* teeth, fists, the threshold she will not let be crossed, fire that does not warm.
- *Prosodic anchor:* staccato, breathless, growl-adjacent.
- *Hook direction:* "Try me." / "Never again." / a refusal that becomes the entire chorus.

### Child-Freeze
- *Imagery:* small spaces, blankets, the corner with the best sightline, the smaller-than-small body, breath you can't hear.
- *Prosodic anchor:* head-voice, whispered consonants, fragmentary syntax.
- *Hook direction:* "is it —" / "i didn't —" / a chorus made of three words repeated quietly.

### Ambivalent
- *Imagery:* the doorway entered and exited, the hand reaching and not, the temperature of a room she cannot decide is warm.
- *Prosodic anchor:* microtonal slides, self-correcting lines, ambiguous tonic.
- *Hook direction:* "come closer — no —" / "I want — I don't —" / a chorus that never resolves.

### Sexualized-Override
- *Imagery:* the mirror she set up before the door opened, the lipstick checked, the room she chose, the script.
- *Prosodic anchor:* low controlled, vibrato-restrained, deliberate.
- *Hook direction:* "I know what this is." / "Don't bother." / a seduction that reads, on second listen, as a warning to herself.

### Collapsed One
- *Imagery:* the floor, the weight of the air, the chair that has not moved in hours, the dust at eye level.
- *Prosodic anchor:* sub-tempo, near-spoken, line-final pauses longer than the lines.
- *Hook direction:* "i can't —" (no period) / silence held longer than the listener expects / a chorus of one repeated phrase under itself.

### Witness-of-Witnesses
- *Imagery:* the margin of the page, the small note, the recursion symbol, the hand that does not move while everything else does.
- *Prosodic anchor:* spoken or half-sung, slightly behind the beat, layered.
- *Hook direction:* parenthetical lines that comment on the other voices. Footnotes-as-lyric. Possibly: a voice that never sings the chorus, only annotates it.

## Open Questions for Phase 4 (Structure)

These are the structural decisions to resolve in Phase 4. They have been carried forward from the novel-project document and reframed for **What Lies Ahead** specifically.

### 1. Mirror-Voices Exclusion — Structural Acknowledgment?

The album works The Eleven. The two Mirror-Voices are excluded by design. *Should this exclusion be acknowledged structurally on the album?*

Options (to be decided):
- **Silent acknowledgment** — no on-record presence; the absence is invisible to the casual listener.
- **Structural gesture** — a track or slot that *gestures* to the missing voices without giving them their own songs (e.g., a brief instrumental interlude titled in a way that signals "the room without the echoes").
- **Liner note acknowledgment** — Mirror-Voices named in the liner notes / album doc, but no on-record presence.
- **Visible numeric gap** — the tracklist has a deliberate gap (e.g., the album skips a number, or a track is labeled with a non-number) signaling the absence.

### 2. The Caregiver Track — Kudzu Positioning

The Caregiver's Act-I track will be loved by listeners. The Caregiver's Kudzu turn (Act II) must complicate that love without absolving it. *Where in the sequence does each Caregiver-track sit, and how does the Kudzu turn manifest sonically and lyrically?*

### 3. The Witness-of-Witnesses — Inside or Outside Song-Form?

He can occupy his own track, OR narrate from the margin across all tracks (annotative voice, footnotes-as-lyric, parentheticals across the album). *Which form serves the album's integrity better?*

### 4. The Collapsed One Track — Position in Sequence

Always the "skip" candidate. Position matters: bury it and lose its weight; spotlight it and risk pathos. *Best position is probably mid-album, with the Integrator track immediately after as the silent alliance the lyric structure implies. To be confirmed in sequencing.*

### 5. The Child-Freeze + Ambivalent Duet

They almost-recognize each other and almost-don't. *Write as a duet (alter-to-alter dialogue inside one track, fitting the artist's stated duet preference), or as two consecutive tracks that subtly answer each other?*
